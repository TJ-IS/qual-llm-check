# Bounded major-section workflow

## 1. Freeze the checkpoint

Record:

- paper and major section;
- current writing stage and applicable requirement-control IDs;
- predecessor path and version/status;
- included and excluded subsections;
- contextual safety boundary: protected outcome, risk-bearing action or harm chain, actor, observable information, allowed control, failure consequence, and excluded non-safety performance concerns;
- whether the study actually claims a malicious actor, attack capability, protected asset, trust boundary, or attack path and therefore needs a traditional threat model;
- canonical ledger entries to use;
- authoritative source sections and local evidence files;
- nearest-neighbor work, intended algorithm/artifact contribution, and benchmark-name boundary;
- feasibility assumptions and unresolved decisions;
- intended output path and acceptance owner.

Use paths and stable identifiers. Hash only when input drift is a real risk; a hash does not prove content quality.

Before proceeding, apply any registry conflict locks. The confirmed `M-ANCHOR-01` permits an initial full-section draft but requires verified sentence prototypes before acceptance. The superseded `M-PUNCT-01` has no independent zero-count branch; punctuation remains part of prototype and prose review.

## 2. Read before compressing

Read the authoritative carrying section in full. If the context budget permits, read the preceding and following major sections. Take notes while preserving paragraph order and rhetorical function; do not substitute a short summary for the comparison text.

For each source paragraph, note: reader question, claim/evidence function, citation role, connection to the prior paragraph, and what it enables next. Separate transferable structure from domain-specific content.

Use more than one functionally matched authority when the section combines ISR theory/design structure with security algorithm or benchmark responsibilities. Bound compatible differences by function. If two authorities prescribe incompatible choices for the same target responsibility, record both choices and consequences, mark the control `conflict`, and obtain the user's confirmation before final selection. Prefer recent papers for prose organization while allowing older original theory or method sources to carry their actual definitions.

## 3. Build our argument spine

Before sentence drafting, define the target section's paragraph sequence. Each planned paragraph should have:

- a reader question or transition;
- one dominant claim;
- its warrant/evidence or author-defined protocol;
- its relationship to theory/design responsibility;
- the next paragraph it licenses.

Also record a continuous paragraph-chain view: what each paragraph inherits, the one new relation it establishes, and which later paragraph or section consumes it. A collection of individually plausible paragraphs does not pass when their information does not accumulate.

Use subsections only for real conceptual units. Do not expose every ledger row, design debt, or validation item as a heading. Several related paragraphs usually belong under one meaningful subsection.

There is no universal sentence quota. Nevertheless, a major section with only a few short paragraphs or many one-paragraph subheadings is a structural warning. Diagnose whether the argument is missing comparison, mechanism, boundary, evidence, or synthesis before adding words.

## 4. Draft the full section

Enter this step only after every applicable conflict that changes drafting or acceptance has been confirmed and written back to the registry.

Write coherent formal paragraphs rather than ledger-shaped fragments. The visible paper explains the research phenomenon, gap, mechanism, artifact, and evaluation; internal search history, discarded options, budget deliberation, sub-agent process, and audit debts stay in ledgers. Place a necessary boundary in the closest theory, method, or evaluation passage without letting defensive prose replace the positive chain.

Introduce constructs only when they perform distinct theoretical work. Keep paper 3 complementary to paper 2: post-action runtime control obtains new observations and controls consequences; it does not refute the pre-action recognition study. Paper 1 remains the attack-path discovery and diagnosis study.

For design-science material, maintain this computation chain:

`source knowledge -> contextual operationalization -> computational or evaluation responsibility -> main component -> independent measurement/intervention/external outcome -> main test -> rejection/withdrawal outcome`

Record whether a construct changes representation, constraint, learning objective, or action rule inside the artifact, or instead supplies an external counterfactual/evaluation responsibility. The latter must not be described as an internal mechanism. If any link is absent, or removing the theory name leaves the design and evaluation unchanged, record and resolve the failure instead of hiding it in generic motivation.

The main algorithm claim also requires substantive trainable or computational capability based on real data and executable processing. Prompt-only changes, static rules, manual heuristics, and an easily added auxiliary score are supporting baselines or components unless the research question explicitly makes one of them the artifact under study.

## 5. Re-read at three scales

1. **Whole major section:** Does it answer one reader-level question and lead to the next section?
2. **Paragraph sequence:** Does each paragraph change the argument rather than repeat a citation or claim?
3. **Sentence and citation:** Are subjects, predicates, referents, source boundaries, and punctuation clear?

Compare the full target section side-by-side with the authoritative section after drafting. Then apply [writing-prototype-search.md](writing-prototype-search.md) iteratively to every reader-visible sentence. Sentence-level imitation is subordinate to the source's whole-section logic.

For source facts and inherited theory or method claims, reopen the exact original support. Separately, reopen every writing-prototype candidate in its full paragraph and carrying section before verification. Author-designed protocols, planned tests, and expected results may match a looser but functionally analogous disclosure sentence; they do not require the same historical procedure or finding, yet they still require a prototype. A writing prototype guides rhetorical function without becoming citation evidence. Punctuation is compared by function inside this review, not by an independent zero target.

## 6. Persist and hand off

Update the canonical evidence, citation-network, paper-specific, and writing-standard ledgers only where responsibilities changed. Record:

- sources newly read and exact sections;
- adopted, bounded, or rejected claims;
- predecessor and successor files;
- remaining blockers and next bounded checkpoint;
- acceptance state: provisional, accepted, superseded, or rejected.

Do not label the whole manuscript complete because one checkpoint passed.

When every reader-visible major section that already exists in the declared manuscript stage has a checkpoint, hand off to [whole-manuscript-integration.md](whole-manuscript-integration.md). Existing failed or provisional sections may be reopened but not excluded; only unwritten future-stage sections may remain outside the integration target. A material cross-section repair reopens every affected section and ledger.
