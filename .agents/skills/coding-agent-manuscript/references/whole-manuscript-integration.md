# Whole-manuscript integration gate

Use this branch only after every reader-visible major section and the references that already exist in the declared manuscript stage have individually reached reviewable checkpoints. It is a separate integration pass, not a replacement for section-level source work and not a long-running Goal.

## Freeze the integration target

Record the manuscript path/hash, declared writing stage, every existing reader-visible major section and references, accepted/provisional/failed section records, genuinely unwritten future sections, paper-specific ledger, corresponding authoritative sections already used, and current sentence-prototype coverage. Existing failed or provisional sections may be reopened but cannot be excluded from integration. If the manuscript is still a pre-results design and the results, discussion, implications, limitations, or conclusion have not yet been written, those absent future-stage sections remain milestones rather than current defects.

## Reopen changed evidence

Do not trust old section verdicts after cross-section edits. Reopen every materially changed target section and the decisive authoritative carrying section. Use prior section records as locators, not as acceptance substitutes.

## End-to-end gates

1. **Reader narrative:** the abstract/introduction claim, literature gap, theory or problem knowledge, artifact, evaluation questions, and stated contribution describe the same research object and payoff.
2. **Section handoffs:** each major section consumes what the previous section established and supplies what the next requires. Analysis unit, construct, label, time point, and outcome keep their identities.
3. **Proportion and hierarchy:** headings represent meaningful units; introductions/theory are not skeletal; methods/evaluation do not absorb responsibilities that belong earlier; repeated definitions and repeated research questions are removed.
4. **Theory-to-artifact identity:** every retained construct performs distinct work through a responsibility, component, observable mechanism, main test, and rejection consequence. Implementation choices that come from engineering or fairness are labeled accordingly.
5. **Evidence distribution:** consequential claims retain direct sources, old theory has later development/current adoption where relevant, adjacent sources use explicit bridges, and no convenient paper carries unrelated paragraph responsibilities.
6. **Formal prose:** paragraph chains remain cumulative across section boundaries; terminology is stable; every visible sentence retains a verified authoritative writing prototype; natural Chinese, proper names, punctuation, and boundary statements serve the argument rather than expose the audit process. Punctuation counts are diagnostic only.
7. **Benchmark and integrity:** benchmark names match actual comparison roles; baselines exclude named competing explanations; planned tests and expected diagnostics are not written as observed results.
8. **Series boundary:** the paper's information time, artifact, training data, evaluation unit, and contribution remain distinct from the other two papers while shared keys and upstream/downstream interfaces stay explicit.

## Propagation

A material integration defect reopens every affected section and ledger responsibility. Update the source/evidence ledger, paper-specific ledger, requirement registry when a general rule changed, and predecessor/successor status together. A local prose repair is insufficient when it changes theory, data, labels, algorithm identity, or evaluation.

## Verdict

- `integration-ready` means every reader-visible major section and reference list that already exists in the declared stage, plus every gate above, is reviewable with named residual conditions.
- `accepted` additionally requires the user's acceptance for the stated manuscript stage.

Counts, hashes, mapping coverage, or prior section PASS labels cannot issue either verdict.
