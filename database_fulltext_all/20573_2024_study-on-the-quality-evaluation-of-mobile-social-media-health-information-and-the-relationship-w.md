Keywords: Mobile social media Health information quality Evaluation index system WeChat communication index Health information dissemination

## A B S T R A C T

Mobile social media has become one of the most convenient, widespread ways for users to obtain health in formation. We construct a mobile social media health information quality evaluation system by conducting a questionnaire survey among 20 experts and 684 users. Then, 498 health WeChat official accounts were selected for empirical research. Results show that health information quality on WeChat official accounts is not high, which is positively correlated with the WeChat communication index, but the content value of health infor mation is not correlated with the WeChat communication index. Our research has reference value for health information governance in mobile social media.

## 1. Introduction

Mobile social media is currently becoming the most direct and practical way for users to disseminate, generate, access, and share health information. This phenomenon is driven by several factors, including the advancement of mobile Internet technology, the widespread popu larity of mobile devices, and the proliferation of social applications [1]. More than 70 % of American adults have searched for health informa tion online [2]. The search results could influence customers’ intentions, behaviors, healthcare decisions, and self-perception of health [3–6]. The significance of online healthcare services is evident in China, with approximately 298 million individuals utilizing these services as of December 2021, making it a prominent area of interest within Internet society [7]. Several studies in the United States reported increased use of social media for health communication [8–11]. Similarly, young people in the United Kingdom are increasingly turning to social media as a source of health information [12,13]. Moreover, hospitals, clinics, dis ease centers, and other health regulatory bodies use social media to communicate about health [14]. During and after disease epidemics such as Covid-19 [15], H1N1 [16], and Ebola [17], WHO, national CDCs and national health management-related bodies used social media platforms as vital tools for timely information dissemination [18].

A global health crisis is also an information crisis, according to an action initiative published in issue 3 of a 2020 volume of the Journal of the American Society for Information Science and Technology (JASIST), which suggests that one of the current challenges is how to screen out accurate and relevant information from the overwhelming and constant influx of data. Due to the loose regulation of mobile social media, stakeholders have expressed worry about the quality of health infor mation. With a high level of specialization in health information and a lack of standardized evaluation criteria for social media platforms, there is a heightened risk of the public believing false health information or rumors. Social media health information (SMHI) is frequently informal and may be written by nonhealth professionals, making it more prone to quality problems than traditional health media channels like books, journals, and magazines. Unlike these channels, social media lacks essential metadata like disclaimers, author information, year of publi cation, publisher, and target audience. Users of social media are frequently inexperienced and eager to find answers [19–21], and social media users who are looking for Internet health information should be especially cautious due to its informal, unregulated nature. Moorhead et al. [14], Orizio et al. [22], and Adams [23] emphasize that while social media is an effective platform for disseminating health informa tion, concerns about content quality, reliability, and availability remain significant.

In light of these challenges, it is essential to assess the quality of the health information, as it directly impacts users’ health decision-making. Previous research has mainly focused on three aspects through literature retrieval and analysis (see Table 1). First, some researchers attempted to conduct empirical studies on the quality assessment of health articles or videos on social media platforms like YouTube [24,25], TikTok [26,27], and WeChat [28]. These studies have utilized established traditional online health information quality assessment tools such as Journal of American Medical Association (JAMA) benchmark [29,30], DISCERN tool [31], and Health on the Net Foundation Code of Conduct (HON code) principles [32] to evaluate the quality of health information on social media platforms. The design of these tools was originally aimed at the quality of online, website, or paper health information. However, there are common links between mobile social media health information and traditional website health information, and the quality of health information of both is related to public life and health. Their health information publishers need to have high medical expertise to ensure quality, and there are also obvious differences, especially in terms of information cost, security, ease of operation, technological level and information reliability [33]. Second, using a simple scoring method to evaluate the quality of social media health information, which involve inviting experts to independently evaluate the quality of information on examination, diagnosis, and treatment of diseases on social media [25, 34,35], but these studies mainly rely on the experience and professional level of evaluation experts. Third, a few studies have attempted to construct new tools for evaluating the quality of social media health information. One study used the Delphi method and invited 18 experts to conduct three rounds of surveys and developed the Social Media Health Related Information Principles (PRHISM) [36] to evaluate the quality of health-related social media content. Another study used a random sampling questionnaire to survey 550 users [37], Attempts have been made to construct a system of information quality evaluation in dicators for mobile social media based on user perception, but these studies have not empirically verified the availability of evaluation in dicators and the practicality of evaluation tools. Therefore, it is neces sary to establish a set of health information quality assessment tools for mobile social media and verify the scientificity and effectiveness of the assessment tools through empirical research.

Some scholars [38,39] have pointed out that the quality assessment list proposed mainly based on experts’ perceptions can no longer effectively meet users’ needs. Users are the main subjects of perceived information and it is easy to collect data widely. Collecting users’ feel ings and opinions on the importance of information quality evaluation indicators is the most direct method and is in line with users’ needs [2]. Due to the lack of professional knowledge and experience among ordinary users, the evaluations provided during the evaluation process also have certain flaws and deficiencies. Therefore, our study creatively blends the views of both professionals and users to screen the quality evaluation indicators of mobile social media health information.

There are three types of assignment methods, which include sub jective assignment method, objective assignment method, and combi nation weighting methods. The combined weighting approach may efficiently utilize the subjective knowledge and experience of persons while obtaining objective data from the initial data, balancing the respective drawbacks of various weighting methods and improving the accuracy and stability of the evaluation results [40,41]. Therefore, the combination assignment method was applied to scientifically assign the evaluation indexes and perform empirical research in the particular application scenario – WeChat official accounts – in our study.

The significance of the existence of mobile social media health in formation is to facilitate the public’s efficient and quick access to highquality health information and achieve better health communication effects, to guide the public to make correct health decisions. Health in formation quality and information sources have significant impacts on health communication effects [42], and source credibility and infor mation content quality are important factors influencing social media users’ health information adoption behavior [43]. Therefore, how the quality of mobile social media health information affects the degree of information dissemination and influence is another question worth exploring.

Based on the aforementioned context, our study proposes to addres the following questions:

RQ1: What is the quality of mobile social media health information? What dimensions are included?

RQ2: How to evaluate the quality of mobile social media health in formation scientifically and effectively.

RQ3: What is the current status of mobile social media health in formation quality?

RQ4: How does the quality of mobile social media health information affect its information dissemination and influence?

In order to solve the above problems, this paper will integrate the dual perspectives of experts and users. and use the combination weighting method to build a mobile social media health information quality evaluation system and conduct an empirical study. Taking WeChat official accounts as an example, we investigate the current sit uation of mobile social media health information quality and explore its relationship with information dissemination influence, to provide a basis for scholars to conduct relevant research on the evaluation and governance of mobile social media health information quality.

## 2. Theoretical basis

## 2.1. User perception theory and elaboration likelihood model

User perception is the process by which customers perceive the services provided by the service industry through their senses, thoughts, and other means. It also refers to the process of face-to-face interaction and contact between customers and service providers, or the interaction between customers and the service delivery system. The impact of customer satisfaction and the customer’s assessment of the quality of the service is influenced by customer perception [44]. At present, user perception theory has been widely applied in the field of network health information quality evaluation, and more and more researchers have begun to attach importance to the evaluation of user perception quality [33]. In the process of information quality evaluation, the main position of information users has been clarified [45,46]. This study combines user perception with the quality of health information on mobile social media, defining it as the most direct and authentic expectations and measurement judgments of users towards the quality of health infor mation on mobile social media, in order to reflect the standards and evaluation indicators that should be adopted for the quality of health information on mobile social media.

The Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM) assumes that readers typically assess a text’s veracity based on its content (such as its con ceptual, logical, or cognitive aspects) or through the use of outside cues (e.g., information sources) [47]. According to the ELM model, there are two paths to persuasion, the central path and the peripheral path. The central path assesses the credibility of a message logically by thinking deeply about it. The peripheral path relies on external cues to determine the credibility of the source, that is, relies on simple hints or heuristics. The ELM theory is also applicable to the fields of online health infor mation quality assessment and online information dissemination research. One study reported that users’ evaluation of online health information largely relies on peripheral clues [42] and one study invited experts and potential users to participate in the importance evaluation of natural disaster management websites [48]. Therefore, this study is based on user perception theory and the ELM model, combining the opinions of experts and potential users to select evaluation indicators for the quality of health information on mobile social media.

## 2.2. Source credibility model and communication and persuasion theory

In the 1950s, Hovland, a psychology teacher at Yale University, proposed the concept of Source Credibility, which is defined as the "degree to which an information source is considered trustworthy and competent by the public"[49], and pointed out that the credibility of information source mainly includes two dimensions: the professionalism and trustworthiness of the information source. The former refers to the degree to which the recipient believes that the provider can provide effective information; The latter refers to the degree to which the recipient believes that the provider is willing to provide true informa tion. Hovland’s Communication and Perception Theory believes that the main factors affecting changes in audience attitudes are four types of variables: source, information, channel, and destination, namely the subject (source) factor of information dissemination, dissemination content, information channel, and the information dissemination object (receiver) factor. Among them, the credibility of the information source has the greatest impact on whether the audience accepts information. When the credibility of the information source is high, information re cipients are more susceptible to being influenced by information and engage in corresponding behaviors [50]. When the audience has a high evaluation of the credibility of the information source, the persuasive effect of the information is better [51]. Therefore, Hovland's information source credibility model and persuasive communication theory are not only applicable to traditional information dissemination scenarios but also used to explain information dissemination behavior in the network environment [52].

We take the WeChat official account and the health information it carries as the research object to explore the relationship between the quality of health information and the communication effect. WeChat, as a prominent mobile social media platform, has emerged as a significant channel for distributing information on the Internet, particularly through its WeChat official accounts feature. WeChat official accounts have gained popularity among Chinese speakers worldwide, with a strong presence in regions such as mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macao. WeChat has become a pivotal player in shaping the land scape of mobile social media in China, including the dissemination of health-related content [53]. Therefore, this study chose to take WeChat health official accounts as the research object.

To reflect the level of communication and influence of the sampled WeChat health official accounts, the WeChat Communication Index (WCI) was adopted for this study. WCI is a highly recognized and authoritative indicator of new media communication and influence [54, 55], which is a standard quantitative value based on the original data source of the public platform. It is a balanced value after considering various influencing factors and can accurately indicate the maturity and impact of the public number, the hotness and coverage of the articles pushed by the public number, and the trend in development and overall hotness of the WeChat official accounts [56].

## 3. Hypothesis development

The quality of health information on mobile social media in this study includes four dimensions: interactivity and reliability of mobile social media, as well as the credibility and value of health information content. This paper takes the health information on WeChat official account as an example to propose a set of assumptions to study the relationship between the quality of health information on mobile social media and the spread of health information. The higher the quality of health information, i.e., the more complete the information is, the more correct it is, and the clearer it is expressed, the higher the willingness of the audience to adopt the information will be [57]. Advanced infor mation quality can have a positive impact on users’ cognitive and emotional responses [58]. Some studies have shown the positive impact of information quality on user behavior and attitudes in other situations, such as the behavior of users accessing online health information using mobile phones [59]. In addition, the quality and functionality of blog systems can also affect users’ willingness to use blogs [60]. Lee also found that information quality has a significant beneficial impact on users’ behavioral attitudes [61]. Therefore, this paper proposes the following hypotheses:

H1: The dissemination and influence of health information in mobile social media are positively correlated with the information’s quality.

Interactivity is the degree to which two or more communicators implement synchronized effects on each other, the communication medium, and the message [62]. The quality of a system’s interactions positively affects users’ transaction-specific and cumulative satisfaction [63]. The study found that the interactivity of the WeChat platform positively affects WeChat users’ information following, information sharing [64]. Besides, interactive emotionally rich health information would be recognized when studying what characteristics of health in formation would lead users to spread it more actively in their circle of friends [57]. Based on the above research, we propose the following assumptions:

H2: The dissemination and influence of mobile social media are positively connected with its interactiveness.

In this study, the source credibility of the quality of the health in formation in mobile social media is represented by the dependability of those platforms and the credibility of the health information content. Source credibility influences the audience’s willingness to receive in formation [65]. Cha et al. [58] further demonstrated that source trust worthiness has a considerable, favorable impact on user responses. In the meantime, information trustworthiness and quality have a consid erable beneficial impact on user need satisfaction [66]. Therefore, the following two hypotheses are proposed in this paper:

H3: The dissemination and influence of mobile social media are positively correlated with its dependability.

H4: The dissemination and influence of health information are positively correlated with the accuracy of its content.

Furthermore, the content value of health information, which evalu ates the completeness and comprehensiveness of the information, is a focal point in this study. The completeness, professionalism, presenta tion, and accuracy of health information have a positive effect on the health information-sharing behavior of WeChat users [67]. Similarly, the comprehensiveness of the quality of information in health video social media also affects users’ willingness to adopt information [68]. Therefore, the following hypotheses are proposed:

H5: The dissemination and influence of health information are correlated with the information’s content value.

## 4. Methodology

First, the literature research method was used to screen the attribu tion dimensions and mobile social media health information quality evaluation indicators in this study. We initially determined a list of evaluation indicators, as shown in Appendix A. Next, a combination of expert consultation, user questionnaire survey, and factor analysis was employed to screen and verify the results. Then, indicator weights were established using the entropy value modified G1 combination assign ment method. Finally, WeChat, the most widely used mobile social media network in China, was the subject of empirical research. To evaluate the current state of health information quality in mobile social media and investigate the relationship between health information quality and the communication influence of WeChat official accounts, we conducted an empirical investigation involving 498 health-related WeChat official accounts. WeChat, being the dominant mobile social media platform in China, provided an ideal setting for this study. By examining the quality indicators that significantly affect the dissemi nation and influence levels of health-related WeChat official accounts, our research aims to provide valuable references and recommendations for enhancing and optimizing the quality and communication strategies surrounding health information in the mobile social media context.

4.2.2. Based on empirical sample data, an objective weight computation is made

The following are the precise steps for determining objective weights based on the empirical sample data from WeChat:

(1) Process of dimensionless normalization.

The evaluation indicators in this study are all positive indicators, so their calculation formula is as follows:

$$
V _ {i j} = \frac {X _ {i j} - X _ {j \min}}{X _ {j \max} - X _ {j \min}}, i \in [ 1, n ]

where $X _ { \mathrm { i j } }$ refers to the value of the ith evaluation indicator of the ith sample, $V _ { \mathrm { i j } }$ is the dimensionless index value, and $X _ { \mathrm { j } }$ min and $X _ { \mathrm { j } }$ max denote the minimum and maximum values of the jth index, respectively.

(2) Entropy value method weight calculation.

By using the formula of the entropy method, the entropy value of the indicator is obtained, as follows:

$$
e _ {i j} = - \frac {1}{\ln (n)} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} V _ {i j} \ln \left(V _ {i j}\right)

where $e _ { \mathrm { j } }$ is the entropy value of the jth indicator and $V _ { \mathrm { i j } }$ is the dimen sionless indicator value.

## 4.2.4. Empirical study

(1) Empirical sample selection.

We checked the list of WeChat official accounts provided by Qingbo Big Data, taking the theme of official accounts classification “health” as the standard, and extracted the top 100 WeChat official accounts of “health-preserving”, “hospital” and “psychology” in the monthly list (from September 1 to September 30, 2021) under this category. The top 250 WeChat official accounts in the monthly list (September 1- September 30, 2021) of the "Medical" category were sampled, excluding duplicate health-related WeChat official accounts and official accounts whose content had nothing to do with health. Finally, the sample of this study included 498 health-related WeChat official ac counts. The specific evaluation objects include the platform information and the tweet information carried by each official account, and for the evaluation of the tweet information, two tweets are randomly selected from each official account to evaluate the quality of the tweet content, and the evaluation indexes related to the tweet content are evaluated by the sum of the evaluation results of the two tweets as the final results of the evaluation indexes. The final sample included in this study consists of 498 WeChat official accounts and a total of 996 tweets they contain.

(2) Sample Evaluation Process.

The evaluation was conducted by 10 researchers. The inclusion criteria for the 10 raters were 1) disciplinary backgrounds from clinical medicine, nursing, medical informatics, and other related fields; and 2) all had at least 6 years of work experience. The rating scale for WeChat health official accounts was designed based on the mobile social media health information evaluation system constructed in the previous sec tion. Before the scoring of the WeChat official accounts, 10 scorers gathered together for a research training and exchange session, which consisted of unified training and discussion of the scoring sheet’s questions, unified analysis of the purpose and meaning of the question setting and assignment classification by the scoring sheet designer, se lection of a public number for collective on-site scoring, and on-site Q&A to unify the scoring standards and ideas of the 10 scorers and reduce individual differences. After the training session, the 10 raters were divided into 5 groups for scoring work, and every two people evaluated the same sample, that is, two evaluators independently evaluated the same sample at the same time. In case of disputes on the assessment results, they were finally determined through negotiation.

## 6. Discussion

## 6.1. Scientificity of the evaluation system

The creation of the mobile social media health information quality evaluation system in this study involved four key steps. In the first step, through literature research, potential mobile social media health infor mation quality indicators are listed using qualitative analysis methods combined with existing research. In the second step, the hierarchical clustering method is used to categorize the indicators of user question naire data and conduct the study of mobile social media health infor mation quality evaluation indicators. The third step involved considering the unique characteristics of mobile social media and the professional characteristics of health information quality. The evalua tion indexes of mobile social media health information quality were further adjusted and screened using the expert consultation method; subsequently, the second round of user questionnaire survey was designed based on the adjusted mobile social media health information quality evaluation framework, and factor analysis (exploratory factor analysis and validation factor analysis) was used to evaluate the indexes Then, the second round of user questionnaire survey was designed based on the adjusted mobile social media health information evaluation framework. In the fourth step, based on the user perception weights of the index system calculated by the factor analysis method, the objective weights of the secondary indicators in the index system were calculated by the entropy method based on the empirical research data of WeChat Public, and the weights of the secondary indicators were corrected by using the combination assignment of the entropy correction G1 method. The final version of the mobile social media health information quality evaluation system was constructed, which contains four primary eval uation indicators; in order of weight, they are health information con tent value (0.383), reliability of mobile social media (0.228), the trustworthiness of health information content (0.206), and interactivity of mobile social media (0.182).

## 6.2. The current situation of health information quality of WeChat official accounts

WeChat official accounts have features such as timely information push notifications, content related to daily life, and a light-hearted and humorous writing style [72]. Health-related WeChat official accounts are in a rapid development stage [73], and as of end of April 2019, the top 100 health WeChat official accounts ranked by the WeChat Communication Index (WCI) have published more than 11,000 articles with total visits of more than 247 million. This shows that the content of health WeChat official accounts is widely noticed by the public and has an important impact on public health education and health promotion. However, WeChat’s official account owners include companies, government agencies, nonprofit organizations, and individuals. WeChat official accounts have simplified the process of accessing health-related information for the public. However, there is a growing sense of skep ticism regarding the accuracy of the health information shared through these accounts [74]. Tencent provides WeChat official accounts authentication service to ensure the authenticity and safety of WeChat official accounts. For verified WeChat official accounts, the authenti cation information and the WeChat authentication unique logo are displayed in the authentication details of the account. However, certi fication is not mandatory. Even without official Tencent certification, individuals and organizations can still apply for WeChat official ac counts and publish health-related information.

By analyzing the survey data on the current status of health infor mation quality of WeChat official accounts, quantitative evaluation re sults were given on the quality of WeChat official accounts’ health information and the level of each dimension, and from the results, it is clear that the quality of WeChat official accounts’ health information is low in general, especially the lowest score on the content credibility dimension. A retrospective study [75] showed that half of the studies gave completely negative evaluations of the quality of online health information, and the main reasons for giving negative evaluations included opaque information about website organizers and hosts, inac curate disease descriptions and drug information, and undisclosed in formation sources and authorship. This is more similar to the empirical results of this paper, and the future improvement of the quality of health information on WeChat official accounts should focus on the content credibility aspect to be promoted.

A systematic review by Moorhead et al. [14] found that the main problems with health information communication in foreign social media such as Facebook, Twitter, and MySpace were quality issues and lack of reliability of health information, indicating that despite the benefits of using social media for health communication, the quality and reliability of information need to be monitored with a focus to ensure its positive impact. Orizio et al. [22] also suggested that the issue of health information quality in online social networks is one of the most worri some and complex issues, and their findings indicated that of the 41 online social networks studied, only 15 social networking sites had at least one quality certificate and only 13 social networking sites had HONcode for health information quality. FZ Wang et al. [28] evaluated and analyzed the quality of health-related WeChat public websites using a combination of two tools, the Online Foundation Code of Conduct (HONcode) and the Suitability Assessment of Materials (SAM) rating scale, and found that they had low HONcode consistency. Despite the moderate applicability of health information published by WeChat official accounts, there are still some problems, such as difficulties in tracking information sources. An Chen et al. [76] found that 50 % of the content published by the top four health official accounts did not have a clear source, and only 2 % of the articles made specific recommenda tions for medication use. Although the quality of mobile social media health information investigated by the above domestic and foreign scholars cannot be compared quantitatively, the relevant problems found are consistent with those found in the empirical research con ducted by the evaluation system constructed in this paper.

In this paper, due to the convenience of sampling channel, the top 100 or top 250 of each subcategory (“health-preserving”, “hospital”, “psychology”, “medical”) under the theme of “health” in the list of WeChat official accounts provided by Qingbo Big Data Platform are selected, which indicates that the WeChat communication index WCI of the selected WeChat official accounts is generally high and influential, and their health information quality level is relatively high and has good representativeness. Therefore, the empirical results of this study can help us infer the current problems of mobile social media health infor mation quality, and it is worth drawing stakeholders’ attention and ef forts to mobile social media health information quality.

## 6.3. The relationship between the quality of health information and the degree of dissemination and influence of WeChat official accounts

The research results show that the quality of health information on WeChat official accounts is positively correlated with the WeChat communication index, indicating that improving the quality of health information on WeChat official accounts can effectively promote the communication degree and influence of WeChat health official accounts. From the correlation analysis between the interactivity of WeChat offi. cial accounts and WCI, there is a positive low correlation between the two, indicating that focusing on personalized recommended information services and guaranteeing active interaction with users are among the most important ways to improve the influence of WeChat health infor mation in the construction of the WeChat official accounts platform. From the correlation analysis of the reliability of WeChat official ac counts and the WeChat communication index, there is a positive low correlation between them, which indicates that the reliability of WeChat official accounts will positively affect the influence of WeChat health information communication. Therefore, first, the state should encourage subjects engaged in medical and health-related industries to provide health information services, and second, in the process of health WeChat official accounts, subjects should pay attention to present their con struction goals, and they should be related to health promotion. From the correlation analysis of the content credibility of WeChat official accounts and the WeChat communication index, there is a positive and moderate correlation between them. This indicates that when providing health information. the subiect party of WeChat official accounts should focus on the objectivity of content description and ensure that the author information is open, the editor and reviewer are open and the reference source is open as much as possible, to promote the influence of WeChat official accounts' health information dissemination. The correlation analysis between the information content value of WeChat official ac counts and the WeChat communication index shows that there is no correlation between the two. This may be due to the lack of judgment on the value of health information by users when reading and receiving health information services due to the strong professionalism of health information, which is the main problem in the field of health informa tion dissemination by WeChat official accounts and even mobile social media in China at present.

The above findings are similar to those of Mo Zuying [77] and Jin et al. [42], who showed that microblog information quality, in general, has a positive effect on its dissemination and influence, but the results differed and were not correlated when each quality indicator was analyzed separately. Jin et al. showed that information quality and credibility of sources were positively correlated with health communi cation. However, a study by An Chen et al. [76] showed that the quality of health accounts’ tweets was not significantly correlated with their contribution to health communication in terms of quality.

## 7. Theoretical implications and practical significance

## 7.1. Theoretical value

In this paper, we constructed a mobile social media health infor mation quality evaluation model, we read and borrowed the existing evaluation indicators from a wide variety of literature, adopted the views of experts and users on the indicators from both perspectives and combined them with empirical research to derive the combination weights to make the model more scientific, reasonable and operable; we empirically evaluated the Chinese health WeChat official accounts and carried out empirical tests on the evaluation model, which was found to be reasonable and effective. The combination assignment idea of the evaluation model can reflect the subjective needs and wishes of users and can also be based on the actual sample data to correct the deviation of human factors. It is a more scientific and reasonable method choice and makes the evaluation model more explanatory and differentiated. At the same time, the evaluation index system constructed in this study provides scientific and effective guidance for evaluating the health in formation quality of mobile social media. In the subsequent work, using this evaluation index system and combining it with specific empirical data, the combined assignment idea of the entropy value correction G1 method selected in this study can be extended to the development of the health information quality evaluation system of any mobile social media other than WeChat official accounts and revision.

## 7.2. Realistic value

By investigating the current status and level of health information quality carried out by the most influential mobile social media platforms in China, we provide a reference for the governance of health infor mation quality of mobile social media in China. The results found that the quality of WeChat official accounts’ health information is generally low; especially the content credibility score is the lowest, and the future improvement of mobile social media health information quality should be promoted from the content credibility aspect. Meanwhile, this pa per’s research on mobile social media health information quality eval uation indexes based on user perceptions can be used for subsequent targeted research on different user groups to guide the construction of accurate services for mobile social media health information service platforms. In addition, our study also shows that improving the quality of mobile social media health information can effectively promote the health communication effect. In particular, the interactivity, reliability and health information content trustworthiness of mobile social media have a significant impact on its communication influence.

## 8. Limitations and future research

In this paper, only WeChat official accounts are selected as the empirical investigation object of the study, and empirical research can be conducted for other mobile social media at home and abroad in the future to provide more powerful guidance for the evaluation of mobile social media health information quality. The empirical research based on the mobile social media health information quality evaluation system in this paper adopts manual evaluation, which cannot to a great extent carry out quality assessment due to the limitation of human cost in vestment and may have bias and can be combined with machine learning methods in the future to develop an automatic quality assess ment model based on natural language processing technology con cerning this evaluation system to promote the widespread development of mobile social media health information quality assessment work and precisely improve the quality of mobile social media health information.

## 9. Conclusion

The mobile social media health information quality evaluation sys tem constructed in this paper can reflect the quality of mobile social media health information in a more comprehensive, scientific and effective way, and the proposed mobile social media health information quality evaluation system has a good level of reliability and validity. Using the evaluation system to conduct empirical research, it is found that the current situation of health information quality of Chinese WeChat official accounts is worrisome, especially since the credibility of health information content is not high. Meanwhile, the health infor mation quality of WeChat official accounts has a significant impact on their communication degree and influence, but the value of health in formation content does not correlate with the WeChat communication index, indicating that users lack professional judgment on the value of health information content, and the difficulty and focus of mobile social media health information quality governance lie in the supervision of health information content value.
