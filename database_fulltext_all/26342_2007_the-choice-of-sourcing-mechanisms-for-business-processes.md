# Chemical Modification of Poly (vinyl chloride) Sheet with Thiourea For Cell Study

Monika<sup>a</sup>, R. R. Mishra<sup>b</sup>, S. Jaiswal<sup>a</sup>, G. Kapusetti<sup>a</sup>, and N. Misra<sup>a</sup>

<sup>a</sup>School of Biomedical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University), Varanasi 221005 <sup>b</sup>Department of Microbiology, Institute of Microbiology, Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi-221005 INDIA

Abstract. Plasticized poly (vinyl chloride) has been used as biomaterial to make medical equipment. An appropriate reaction situation by temperature at 60-85<sup>0</sup>C for the surface modification of PVC sheet with sodium thiourea has been investigated. Although, the use of a phase-transfer catalyst (Tetra butyl ammonium hydrogen sulphate-TBHAS) makes it feasible for the nucleophilic substitution reaction to take place, the morphology of the film is changed by the reaction, leading to a loss in both surface smoothness and transparency of the film. According to this study, the reaction with sodium thiourea occurs consistently through the sheets, and modified surface have antibacterial capacity.

Keywords: Poly (vinyl chloride), Tetra butyl ammonium hydrogen sulphate, Nucleophile substitution. PACS: 87.85-d, 87.85.J

## INTRODUCTION

Poly (vinyl chloride) is widely used for making medical device [1] etc for medical cures. Bacterial infection occur after the long term use of medical devices in patient which has deep consequences on the patient uneasiness and on the expenditure of the medical cures [2].Several method has been exposed for protection of these implants from bacterial infection [3].In these respect several different surface modification methods have been reported to alter the physicochemical properties of substrates [4].The methods like, surface modification by nucleophilic substitution [5], solvent and non- solvent mixtures [6] have been reported. While this paper presents the improvement of an approach to reduce bacterial adhesion based on the surface and chemical modification of the polymer and their difference.

## EXPERIMENTAL

## Materials and Methods

Medical grade PVC sheets were obtained from Hindustan Latex, Ltd., Varanasi, India. Sodium thiourea has obtained from Merck Ltd., Mumbai, India. Tetrahydrofuron (THF) from Glaxo Ltd. Mumbai, India. Different pathogens were used for bacterial adhesion studies.

PVC sheet has been kept with the aqueous solution of sodium thiourea under the room temperature. Tetra butyl hydrogen sulphate has been added when reaction mixture achieve the $6 5 ^ { \mathrm { { 0 } } } \mathrm { { C } }$ temperature and allowed the reaction has been kept for 6 hours. After the reaction, they have been washed with double distilled water followed with methanol and dried under the air oven.

## Characterizations

## FT-IR

FTIR was performed in absorbance mode at room temperature from 400 to 4000 cm<sup>-1</sup> wave number using SHIMADZU 8400S FTIR with a resolution of 4 cm<sup>-1</sup>.

## Contact angle

Kruss F-100 tensiometer was used for contact angle measurement for thin strip of modified PVC sheet at room temperature in water medium.

## Microbial adhesion assessment

Representative nosocomial Gram positive and Gram negative bacteria were selected for microbial adhesion examination. At 2 optical densities (OD) and ${ 1 0 } ^ { 6 }$ colony forming unit (CFU) of McFarland, strains of Staphylococcus aureus, Escherichia coli, and Pseudomonas aeruginosa has been sub culture on Mueller Hinton agar plates were inoculated for 3 hour to reach log phage. The control and modified sheets has been placed on the media surface and incubated at $3 7 ^ { 0 } \mathrm { C }$ for overnight. The zone of inhibition was examined on next day.
