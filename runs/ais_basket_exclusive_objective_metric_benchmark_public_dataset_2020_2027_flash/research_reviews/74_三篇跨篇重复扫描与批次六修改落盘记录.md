## 1. 扫描方法与本轮模板基准

扫描方法。以十六字连续子串为窗口对三篇全文做重复统计，辅以句级相似度扫描（占位与工件名归一化后两两比较），定位篇内逐字重复与跨篇近义句，再逐处回到模板原文核对。

RADAR 结论对框架目的的回望不复用标题短语。原句为 this study draws on RO and RL theories in the design of a novel RADAR framework for improving the robustness of cyber defense AI agents，且 Via rigorous evaluation, we showed that under RADAR, emulating adversarial attacks improves the robustness of AI agents。

ACAA 对设计理由前提的原句。a sound design rationale, as a foundation for artifact design and evaluation, is important for establishing a design science contribution (Gregor and Hevner 2013)。三篇的设计理由句均为该句的翻译，本轮按三篇各自理论构成分化句式。

RADAR 引言与理论节对同一攻击过程的两处表述换词。引言 P3 为 As adversarial attacks often involve taking a sequence of actions to modify the malicious input，RL 节为 emulating the steps (i.e., the sequence of actions) an adversary takes to craft adversarial attacks。

RADAR Experiment Design 以表格一次性定义基线集合，结果节以类别指称基线。原句为 To rigorously compare RADAR to other alternatives。

RADAR Research Gaps 的缺口陈述以指称回指构念，不重复定义性列举。
