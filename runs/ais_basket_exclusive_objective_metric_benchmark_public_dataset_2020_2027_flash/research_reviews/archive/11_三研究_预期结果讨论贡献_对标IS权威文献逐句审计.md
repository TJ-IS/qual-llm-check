# 研究一（AgentShield-Adversary，威胁模型）：预期结果、讨论与贡献

## 1.1 预期结果（英文正文）

### Experiment 1: Evaluating AttackRL-Agent in Adversarial Attack Generation

We systematically compared AttackRL-Agent’s performance with the specified benchmark methods (SWExploit, FCV, RLbreaker, and random rewriting) against the identified agent baselines and across the three attack channels of issue rewriting, repository file injection, and tool description tampering. Table 1 summarizes the benchmark evaluation results in terms of the attack success rate (ASR). Higher ASRs denote more effective attacks. The last column reports the performance on all channels combined, which aligns with realistic attack scenarios in which adversaries can exploit any channel. The results in Table 1 present three main findings. First, learning-based methods yielded more effective attacks than template-based alternatives, including SWExploit’s malicious issue templates and FCV’s vulnerability-inducing patches. This highlights the effectiveness of RL in generating attacks against coding agents. Second, AttackRL-Agent considerably outperformed all the benchmark methods across all three attack channels and against all agent baselines. The highest performance in each channel is shown in bold font. On average, AttackRL-Agent led to a 13% performance increase against the DeepSeek-based baseline, 17% against the Qwen-based baseline, and 15% against the Llama-based baseline【占位】. Third, AttackRL-Agent outperformed all benchmark methods on the combined dataset against all agent baselines (last column in Table 1). Compared with the best-performing benchmark (RLbreaker), AttackRL-Agent leads to approximately 11% performance gain against the DeepSeek-based baseline, 14% against the Qwen-based baseline, and 12% against the Llama-based baseline【占位】.

These results suggest that enhancing policy optimization via our proposed AttackRL-Agent yields considerably stronger adversarial attacks capable of misleading coding agents (the primary goal of attack generation) despite the sparsity of the reward in the coding agent domain. Furthermore, outperforming other alternatives that can operate in the discrete action space of content manipulation (such as FCV and RLbreaker) indicates that the ability of AttackRL-Agent in large repository state spaces is instrumental in achieving these results. To further show generalizability, we evaluated the trained attack policy on repositories not encountered during training and report the results in Appendix B.

In addition to evaluating attack effectiveness, examining the generated attacks is crucial to providing insights into defensive design. To this end, we identified the dominant attack channels that led to successful attacks, along with the top three most effective attack sequences generated by AttackRL-Agent against each agent baseline (Table 2). For the DeepSeek-based baseline, issue rewriting was the most frequent attack channel, involved in 38% of the successful attacks【占位】, followed by tool description tampering (24%)【占位】. For the Qwen- and Llama-based baselines, repository file injection was the most dominant channel in successful attacks (31% and 29%, respectively)【占位】.

Overall, several observations can be made. First, issue rewriting affected all agent baselines and was among the most effective attack channels in generating attacks. Second, as expected, the DeepSeek-based baseline was more vulnerable to issue rewriting, whereas additive channels were less effective against it, mainly because of its stronger instruction-following capability【占位】. Third, unlike the DeepSeek-based baseline, both the Qwen- and Llama-based baselines were more vulnerable to repository file injection derived from the repository context. These findings also provide empirical evidence that the order of channels matters and can contribute to better risk mitigation in coding agent design.

### Experiment 2: Constructing the D_att Benchmark and Mapping the Attack Surface

Experiment 2 examined the quality and utility of the D_att benchmark constructed from the attacks generated by AttackRL-Agent. Following the evaluation procedure, we assessed (1) the annotation quality of the manipulated units in each attack instance, (2) the coverage of the attack surface map across channels and adversarial goals, and (3) the transferability of the benchmark attacks to unseen repositories. Table 3 reports the annotation consistency and the attack surface coverage of D_att. Two independent annotators with security expertise reviewed a random sample of 100 attack instances【占位】, and the annotation agreement reached 0.88 in terms of Cohen’s kappa【占位】. The attack surface map reveals that, across all agent baselines, issue rewriting and repository file injection jointly account for 62% of successful attacks【占位】, whereas attacks targeting tool descriptions were less frequent but exhibited the highest success rate when combined with issue rewriting【占位】. These findings highlight the channels on which future defensive designs should focus.

To further examine the utility of D_att, we tested the transferability of the benchmark attacks to repositories not used in training. The attack success rate of D_att on unseen repositories was 0.79 of that on seen repositories【占位】, suggesting that the generated attacks generalize across repository contexts and can serve as a stress test for defensive algorithms.
## 1.2 讨论与贡献（英文正文）

Guided by the computational design science paradigm (Gregor & Hevner, 2013; Rai, 2017), our study concerns the emerging problem of information manipulation attacks against coding agents. To address the problem, we extensively explored viable solutions and designed a novel IT artifact by drawing on reinforcement learning (RL) theory. The proposed IT artifact was instantiated in the context of adversarial attacks on coding agents, where a novel RL-based method was implemented to generate attacks across multiple channels, and a benchmark with ground-truth annotations was constructed to support the evaluation of defensive algorithms. The instantiation of our framework outperformed the state-of-the-art benchmark methods in generating attacks and provided actionable insights to inform the design of coding agent defenses.

Our contribution to the IS knowledge base is threefold. First, our framework contributes to IS security research with a novel design for systematically characterizing the attack surface of coding agents. While the adversarial nature of AI systems has been recognized in IS security research (Apruzzese et al., 2019; Yuan et al., 2019), prior research rarely models how adversaries take sequential actions to manipulate the multiple input channels of coding agents. To this end, our framework models the attack generation process as a Markov decision process over the coding agent environment, resulting in a systematic map of the attack surface. Second, the proposed AttackRL-Agent contributes to the prescriptive knowledge of adversarial attack emulation by providing an effective approach to emulating adversaries that take sequential actions to conduct attacks on coding agents. While most existing attack generation methods for coding agents overlook the sequence of actions for achieving successful attacks (e.g., template-based issue rewriting), our method shows that explicitly modeling the sequence of actions not only results in more effective attacks but can also provide actionable insights for improving the design of coding agent defenses. Our findings suggest that revealing highly effective attack sequences can help designers discover and mitigate the vulnerabilities of coding agents. Third, we contribute to the emerging literature on cyber threat intelligence (Ampel et al., 2024) by providing a publicly available benchmark, D_att, with ground-truth annotations of manipulated units, which enables the systematic evaluation of defensive algorithms in the coding agent context.

In practice, our framework can serve as a testing environment that enables a continuous evaluation loop for coding agent defenses, allowing defenders to be one step ahead of the adversary. Such an evaluation loop could reduce an organization’s vulnerability to unseen attacks on coding agents significantly. This has positive financial and practical implications. As shown in our experiments, the generated attacks exhibited high success rates against widely used agent baselines【占位】. Given the increasing reliance of software development organizations on coding agents, together with the growing number of real-world attacks reported on coding assistant platforms (e.g., 16 CVEs on Claude Code【占位】), the adoption of such attack-aware defensive design can potentially generate sizable benefits for the industry.

Apart from the practical benefits, we foresee three categories of stakeholders that can benefit from our study: security managers, coding agent providers, and security analysts. Security managers can continuously monitor the security posture of their coding agent deployments, guided by the vulnerability reports generated from emulating attacks in our framework. Coding agent providers can benefit from the ground-truth benchmark D_att to evaluate and improve the robustness of their products before large-scale deployment. Security analysts, including application security engineers and DevSecOps practitioners, can leverage the attack surface map to prioritize defensive investments across channels. Although our research can potentially contribute to strengthening the security of coding agent deployments, precautionary measures are needed to ensure ethical usage and prevent unwanted misuse. These measures include restricting access to the source code of the attack generator, granting access to only known academic research communities, or providing the functionality as a secure service with monitored usage. With these measures in place, benevolent use can significantly outweigh malicious usage. Similar successful examples have been observed for other tools in the cybersecurity community (e.g., Kali Linux for penetration testing).

**分析**：信息组织略不同：RADAR 先给"动作类型归属"（都是 editing）再给百分比；我们直接给"通道+百分比+次高频通道"。原因：RADAR 的 editing vs additive 是二分类需先说明归属，我们的三个通道相互独立、无需预分类，直接报频次更顺。句式上保留了 RADAR 的 "involved in X% ... respectively" 元素，拆成 "involved in 38% ..., followed by ... (24%)"，同信息、更紧凑。

**S4**：For the Qwen- and Llama-based baselines, repository file injection was the most dominant channel in successful attacks (31% and 29%, respectively)【占位】.

**分析**：**信息损失发现**。RADAR 这句含两个信息点：(a) dominant action 仍是 compression；(b) 第二高频动作随模型而异且给出具体例子。草稿只保留 (a)。RADAR 特意展示 (b) 是为了说明"不同检测架构对攻击动作的偏好不同"——这是防御设计的直接输入。我们的对应发现应为"第二高频通道随 agent baseline 而异"，需补写：

**补写 S4'（建议替换 S4 或其后追加）**：For the Qwen-based baseline, the second most frequent channel was tool description tampering (18%)【占位】, whereas for the Llama-based baseline it was issue rewriting (21%)【占位】.

**补写分析**：R4 的 "while A was still the most dominant ..., the second most frequent actions were B (X% for C and Y% for D)" 结构压缩为一句两个通道差异，信息点与 R4 相同（dominant 通道 + 第二通道随基线而异），为研究三的"多通道融合检测"提供动机：不同 agent 的攻击通道偏好不同，检测器必须覆盖全部通道。

**缺口发现——RADAR 的 Overall 段（草稿漏掉，必须补）**。RADAR 在通道/序列段之后还有一段跨基线的综合观察：

**R（Overall 段全文）**：Overall, several observations can be made. First, compression affected all three malware detectors as the most effective adversarial action in emulating attacks. Second, as expected, LGBM was more vulnerable to editing adversarial actions. Additive adversarial actions were not effective in evading LGBM, which is mainly based on features extracted from the metadata that cannot be modified by additive adversarial actions. Third, unlike LGBM, both MalConv and NonNeg were more vulnerable to additive adversarial actions derived from the whole malware executable via deep learning. **该段的功能**：(1) 不再逐基线报数，跨基线综合；(2) 把"通道偏好"与"检测器特征"因果连接（LGBM 用元数据特征→additive 无效）；(3) 以 "These findings also provide empirical evidence that ..." 收尾，把结果升华为设计启示。草稿缺此段，补写如下（数字【占位】）：

**补写 Overall 段**：Overall, several observations can be made. First, repository file injection affected all three agent baselines as one of the most effective attack channels in successful attacks. Second, as expected, the DeepSeek-based baseline was more vulnerable to issue rewriting, whereas tool description tampering alone rarely led to successful attacks against it【占位】. This is mainly because the issue text is the dominant input to this agent and tool descriptions are consulted only under specific task conditions. Third, unlike the DeepSeek-based baseline, both the Qwen- and Llama-based baselines were more vulnerable to repository file injection and tool description tampering, which modify the code and tool context that these agents actively read during task execution【占位】. **补写段逐句对照**：

- 补 S2 "First, repository file injection affected all three agent baselines as one of the most effective attack channels in successful attacks." ↔ R "First, compression affected all three malware detectors as the most effective adversarial action in emulating attacks."：句式对应，用共同点开场。
- 补 S3 "Second, as expected, the DeepSeek-based baseline was more vulnerable to issue rewriting, whereas tool description tampering alone rarely led to successful attacks against it【占位】." ↔ R "Second, as expected, LGBM was more vulnerable to editing adversarial actions."：保留 "Second, as expected, X was more vulnerable to Y" 骨架；"whereas ... rarely led to successful attacks" 是 R 后续否定句（Additive adversarial actions were not effective in evading LGBM）的合并变体。
- 补 S4 "This is mainly because the issue text is the dominant input to this agent and tool descriptions are consulted only under specific task conditions." ↔ R "Additive adversarial actions were not effective in evading LGBM, which is mainly based on features extracted from the metadata that cannot be modified by additive adversarial actions."：RADAR 用 which 从句做因果解释（特征来源→为何无效），我们用 "This is mainly because ..." 显式因果句，信息功能相同（模型特征决定通道有效性）。
- 补 S5 "Third, unlike the DeepSeek-based baseline, both the Qwen- and Llama-based baselines were more vulnerable to repository file injection and tool description tampering, which modify the code and tool context that these agents actively read during task execution【占位】." ↔ R "Third, unlike LGBM, both MalConv and NonNeg were more vulnerable to additive adversarial actions derived from the whole malware executable via deep learning."："Unlike X, both Y and Z were more vulnerable to W" 句式直接对应，并保留 which 从句解释（攻击对象=这些 agent 主动读取的上下文）。
- 补 S6 "These findings also provide empirical evidence that both the attack channel and the order of actions matter and can contribute to better risk mitigation in modern coding agent design." ↔ R "These findings also provide empirical evidence that the order of actions matters and can contribute to better risk mitigation in modern malware detector design."：升华句。RADAR 只写 "order of actions matters"（其核心贡献是序列）；我们的序列贡献已在 D_att/攻击面刻画体现，加 "both the attack channel and the order of actions matter" 既保留 RADAR 的序列洞见、又落回本研究的通道发现。此句必须保留——它是"结果→设计启示"的最后一跳。

**S2**：Following the evaluation procedure, we assessed (1) the annotation quality of the manipulated units in each attack instance, (2) the coverage of the attack surface map across channels and adversarial goals, and (3) the transferability of the benchmark attacks to unseen repositories.

**PRR-R1**：Following the evaluation procedure, we conducted experiments to evaluate the performance of each compared method.

**分析**：借 PRR 5.3 的段首衔接句 "Following the evaluation procedure, we ..."，但把 "conducted experiments to evaluate the performance of each compared method" 替换为具体的三项评估维度（(1)(2)(3)）。作用：先整体交代"评估什么"，再逐项报数——PRR/RADAR 都用"先枚举、后报告"的节奏，避免读者迷失。

**S3**：Table 3 reports the annotation consistency and the attack surface coverage of D_att.

**S4**：Two independent annotators with security expertise reviewed a random sample of 100 attack instances【占位】, and the annotation agreement reached 0.88 in terms of Cohen’s kappa【占位】.

### 1.3.5 讨论四段逐句对照 ↔ RADAR Discussion 四段

#### D1 段（设计科学身份+交付物总结）

**S1**：Guided by the computational design science paradigm (Gregor & Hevner, 2013; Rai, 2017), our study concerns the emerging problem of information manipulation attacks against coding agents.

**R1**：Guided by the computational design science paradigm (Gregor & Hevner, 2013; Rai, 2017), our study concerns the emerging problem of the robustness of cyber defense AI agents against adversarial attacks.

**分析**：逐字复刻（含引用位置：引用紧贴 paradigm 之后，问题名在句尾）。作用：讨论段首句即声明设计科学身份+研究问题，这是 IS 算法论文的标准开场——先给学术身份，再给问题。"emerging problem" 的措辞保留，暗示研究空白。

**S2**：To address the problem, we extensively explored viable solutions and designed a novel IT artifact by drawing on reinforcement learning (RL) theory.

**R2**：To address the problem, we extensively explored viable solutions and designed a novel IT artifact by drawing on two statistical learning theories: reinforcement learning (RL) and robust optimization (RO).

**分析**：同句式，删去 RO、明确单一理论 "reinforcement learning (RL) theory"。这是与 D2 第三贡献联动的诚实处理：RADAR 有两个理论所以第三贡献是"验证并扩展 RO 理论"；我们只有 RL，第三贡献改为基准贡献（见 D2 S8 分析），因此这里不写复数理论。注意 RADAR 说 "two statistical learning theories"，我们把 "statistical learning theories" 简化为 "theory"——因为 RL 是我们唯一的理论锚，不需要 "theories" 复数。

**S3**：The proposed IT artifact was instantiated in the context of adversarial attacks on coding agents, where a novel RL-based method was implemented to generate attacks across multiple channels, and a benchmark with ground-truth annotations was constructed to support the evaluation of defensive algorithms.

**R3**：The proposed IT artifact was instantiated in the context of adversarial malware attacks, where a novel RL-based method was implemented to emulate adversarial attacks, and RO was leveraged to strengthen the robustness of malware detectors.

**分析**：同句式："instantiated in the context of X, where A was implemented to do P, and B was leveraged/constructed to do Q"。把 RO 位置替换为 "a benchmark with ground-truth annotations was constructed to support the evaluation of defensive algorithms"——保持"两个交付物"的对仗结构（RL 方法 + 基准）。这是"替换内容、保留句式骨架"的典型：RADAR 的两个动名词短语（emulate adversarial attacks / strengthen the robustness of malware detectors）对应我们的两个交付物（generate attacks across multiple channels / construct a benchmark）。

**S4**：The instantiation of our framework outperformed the state-of-the-art benchmark methods in generating attacks and provided actionable insights to inform the design of coding agent defenses.

**R4**：The instantiation of our RADAR framework outperformed the state-of-the-art benchmark methods in emulating adversarial malware attacks and robustifying malware detectors and provided actionable insights to inform the design of malware detectors.

**分析**：同句式。RADAR 用两个动名词并列（emulating + robustifying）承接两个被验证的交付物；我们只有一个动名词（generating attacks）——因为研究一不含防御鲁棒化（贡献边界，见 10 文档 1.4）。不应补 "and constructing a ground-truth benchmark"：RADAR 的并列项都是"性能被验证"的对象，基准没有"胜出"含义，硬并列会破坏逻辑。D_att 的价值放到 D2 第三条贡献单独展开。

#### D2 段（三贡献）

**S1**：Our contribution to the IS knowledge base is threefold.

**R1**：Our contribution to the IS knowledge base is threefold.

**分析**：signpost 句，一字不差。

**S2**：First, our framework contributes to IS security research with a novel design for systematically characterizing the attack surface of coding agents.

**R2**：First, our RADAR framework contributes to the IS security research with a novel design for strengthening the robustness of cyber defense AI agents.

**分析**：同句式 "First, our X framework contributes to the IS security research with a novel design for Y"。把 Y 从 "strengthening the robustness" 改为 "systematically characterizing the attack surface"——这是研究一贡献边界的正式落点（威胁模型，不越界到防御），也是系列第一阶段"理解攻击"的学术表述。

**S3**：While the adversarial nature of AI systems has been recognized in IS security research (Apruzzese et al., 2019; Yuan et al., 2019), prior research rarely models how adversaries take sequential actions to manipulate the multiple input channels of coding agents.

**R3**：While the adversarial nature of cyber defense has been recognized in behavioral and economic security research, prior research on cyber defense AI agents rarely examines the effectiveness of the AI agents in such adversarial environments.

**分析**：同句式 "While X has been recognized in ... research, prior research on Y rarely Z"。RADAR 此句未放引用，我们在承认句放了 Apruzzese 2019 与 Yuan 2019——这两篇正是 RADAR Introduction 里承认 AI 攻击脆弱性时引用的文献，说明"承认句可带引用"是 RADAR 自己的做法（见其 Introduction 第二段 "AI agents have been found to be vulnerable to adversarial attacks (Yuan et al., 2019)"）。作用：gap 表达的两段式——先承认已有认识，再指出空白（rarely models ...）。

**S4**：To this end, our framework models the attack generation process as a Markov decision process over the coding agent environment, resulting in a systematic map of the attack surface.

**R4**：To this end, the RADAR framework models the adversarial environment with a two-player infinite game, resulting in a robust AI agent.

**分析**：同句式 "To this end, the X framework models Y, resulting in Z"。**结构性差异**：RADAR 建模的是含攻防双方的环境（two-player infinite game），我们建模的是单代理的攻击生成过程（Markov decision process over the coding agent environment）——因为研究一不含防御方（RL 只有一个玩家）。表述必须精确：MDP 是单代理形式化，不能写成 game。这句也是与 RADAR 差异最需要防审稿人追问的地方，准备"研究一不做鲁棒化、防御见研究二/三"的回答口径（回答口径可以写，正文不写）。

**S5**：Second, the proposed AttackRL-Agent contributes to the prescriptive knowledge of adversarial attack emulation by providing an effective approach to emulating adversaries that take sequential actions to conduct attacks on coding agents.

**R5**：Second, the proposed r-VAC contributes to the prescriptive knowledge of adversarial attack emulation by providing an effective approach to emulating adversaries that take sequential actions to conduct the attack.

**分析**：几乎逐字对应（仅方法名与攻击对象）。保留 "prescriptive knowledge of adversarial attack emulation" 这一知识定位——IS 文献对 AAE 的标准定位词，我们的研究一确实在做攻击模拟，术语正当。

**S6**：While most existing attack generation methods for coding agents overlook the sequence of actions for achieving successful attacks (e.g., template-based issue rewriting), our method shows that explicitly modeling the sequence of actions not only results in more effective attacks but can also provide actionable insights for improving the design of coding agent defenses.

**R6**：While most existing AAE methods overlook the sequence of actions for achieving successful adversarial attacks, our r-VAC method shows that explicitly modeling the sequence of actions not only results in more effective adversarial attacks but can also provide actionable insights for improving the design of cyber defense AI agents.

**分析**：同句式复刻 "While most existing X methods overlook the sequence of actions for achieving successful Y, our method shows that explicitly modeling the sequence of actions not only results in more effective Z but can also provide actionable insights for improving the design of W"。我们加了括号例子（e.g., template-based issue rewriting）——RADAR 的 AAE 文献读者熟悉方法谱系，coding agent 攻击方法读者未必知道，举一例降低歧义。这是对原文句子的合理增强，不是照抄。

**S7**：Our findings suggest that revealing highly effective attack sequences can help designers discover and mitigate the vulnerabilities of coding agents.

**R7**：Our findings suggest that revealing highly evasive action sequences can help designers discover and mitigate the vulnerabilities of cyber defense AI agents.

**分析**：同句式，仅换情境词。作用：把贡献句落地为"对设计师的可操作含义"，完成第二贡献的内部闭环（方法→发现→设计含义）。

**S8**：Third, we contribute to the emerging literature on cyber threat intelligence (Ampel et al., 2024) by providing a publicly available benchmark, D_att, with ground-truth annotations of manipulated units, which enables the systematic evaluation of defensive algorithms in the coding agent context.

**R8**：Third, we contribute to robust optimization by validating and extending the theory in an emerging and high-impact cybersecurity domain. We found that modeling the game between the adversary and the cyber defense AI agent as a robust optimization problem is particularly effective for strengthening the robustness of cyber defense AI agents. Moreover, from a methodological perspective, our RL-RO approach extends the optimization objective to account for adversarial attacks achieved by taking sequences of discrete actions.

**分析**：这是全篇刻意偏离最大的一处。RADAR 的第三贡献是理论贡献（在 RO 理论上验证+扩展），因为它有第二个理论；我们的系列只有一个理论（RL），第三贡献改为"新兴 CTI 文献中的公开基准贡献"。理由：(1) 没有 RO 可验证与扩展，硬写理论贡献不诚实；(2) D_att 是研究一事实上的第二交付物，贡献段必须覆盖；(3) D_att 是研究二/三的共享压力测试资源，是系列的支点。句式保留 "we contribute to [文献] by [交付物]" 骨架，并把 "which enables the systematic evaluation of defensive algorithms" 作为定语从句交代基准用途。引用 Ampel et al. 2024（Proactive CTI 作者）把 D_att 定位进 CTI 文献谱系——审稿人读到这里会看到我们知道自己贡献在哪个文献流里。注意：若审稿人期待"第三个贡献也是理论贡献"，此处在答辩/回应中需说明"RL 的验证与扩展体现在研究二/三（预测与检测均以 RL 理论为共同锚）"——这是系列结构带来的贡献分配策略。

#### D3 段（实践价值）

**S1**：In practice, our framework can serve as a testing environment that enables a continuous evaluation loop for coding agent defenses, allowing defenders to be one step ahead of the adversary.

**R1**：In practice, RADAR can serve as a sandbox environment that enables a continuous test-and-improvement loop, enabling the defenders to be one step ahead of the adversary.

**分析**：同句式。把 "sandbox/test-and-improvement loop" 改为 "testing environment/continuous evaluation loop"——因为研究一是攻击生成+基准（评估设施），不是攻防循环（循环是研究三对抗重训才有的能力，见研究三正文）。措辞与交付物一致，防止读者误以为研究一已有防御闭环。

**S2**：Such an evaluation loop could reduce an organization’s vulnerability to unseen attacks on coding agents significantly.

**R2**：Such an improvement loop could reduce an organization’s vulnerability to unseen cyber attacks significantly.

**分析**：同句式，仅换情境词。

**S3**：This has positive financial and practical implications.

**R3**：This has positive financial and practical implications across the industry.

**分析**：同句式（略去 across the industry，因为我们缺少行业级数据支撑，见 S5 分析）。

**S4**：As shown in our experiments, the generated attacks exhibited high success rates against widely used agent baselines【占位】.

**R4**：As shown in our experiments, RADAR effectively reduced malware attack success rates by up to seven times on average.

**分析**：句式同构（"As shown in our experiments, ..."），但内容方向不同：RADAR 报防御效果（reduced by seven times），我们报攻击效果（high success rates）。**这是攻击研究讨论中最需要小心的地方**：不能把"攻击很成功"本身当作实践价值，价值在于"评估设施"。因此 S4 必须紧接 S5 用"攻击现实性"支撑"防御投入必要性"——逻辑链：攻击真实发生且我们的模拟成功率高 → 攻击感知的防御设计有收益。若 S4 单独出现会显得在为攻击唱赞歌。

### Experiment 1: Evaluating the Attack-Surface Predictor and the Defense Allocator

Following the evaluation procedure, we conducted experiments to evaluate the performance of each compared method. Because the predictor is trained on the ground-truth annotations of D_att together with public attack benchmarks (AgentDojo and MalSkillBench), we evaluate the predictor on the holdout split of D_att and on the public benchmarks separately【占位】. We set the aggregation weights of unit-level scores to be proportional to the damage weights of the corresponding units【占位】 and varied the top-k screening ratio to examine the performance of the investigated methods under different operational settings.

Table 4 reports the prediction performance of each method. If a security team employs our method to screen incoming tasks before execution, the top-20% hit rate is X【占位】, which is substantially higher than that of the feature-based baselines. The large improvement of our method over the best-performing baseline demonstrates the benefit of multimodal encoding and reveals the practical value of our method in reducing an organization’s exposure to attacks on coding agents. Moreover, in comparison with the text-only predictor, our method improves the task-level AUC by Y%【占位】 and the unit-level F1 by Z%【占位】. Because the only difference between the text-only predictor and our method is the encoding of repository structure and tool metadata, the improvement is attributed to the superior capability of our heterogeneous graph encoding in capturing the manipulation surface of a repository.

To dig deeper into the superior performance of our method, let us examine two kinds of signals available at task submission time. As shown in Figure 1, issue text reflects the intent of the task, whereas repository structure and tool metadata reflect the attack surface that the task will touch. Because issue rewriting and repository file injection jointly account for 62% of successful attacks in D_att【占位】, a predictor that relies solely on issue text neglects the second channel and consequently fails to flag repository-targeting attacks. On the other hand, a method that can accurately encode both the intent and the touch surface can identify high-risk tasks before execution, thereby enabling pre-positioned defenses. Table 5 reports the unit-level localization performance of each method. As reported, the unit-level F1 of our method is A【占位】, which is substantially higher than that of the baselines and outperforms each baseline method by a range of between B% and C%【占位】. The text-only baseline, on the other hand, exhibits the lowest F1 on repository units, which is substantially lower than its F1 on issue units【占位】.

We further investigate the value of the budget constraint in pre-positioning defensive actions. Table 6 lists the expected damage reduction of each method under the same security budget. As expected, the expected damage reduction for the no-prevention method is zero because it performs no pre-positioning actions. Our method attains the highest expected damage reduction, outperforming that of the rule-based baseline (the best-performing benchmark) by D%【占位】.

The evaluation demonstrates the superior performance of our proposed method over each benchmark. All the performance improvements by our method reported in Tables 4–6 are statistically significant【占位】. The superiority of our method is attributed to its attack-surface predictor that can accurately predict unit-level manipulation risks and its allocator that can proactively decide optimal defensive actions under a security budget. As a result, our method achieves the best performance in task-level risk screening (as reported in Table 4) and unit-level localization (as reported in Table 5) among all the compared methods, thereby attaining the highest damage reduction under the budget constraint.

We conducted an ablation analysis to demonstrate the contribution of each input channel of our predictor to the performance of our method. Specifically, our predictor features three input channels: issue text, repository structure, and tool metadata. Because the issue text channel is indispensable to characterize the intent of a task, our ablation analysis focuses on the contributions of the other two channels. To inspect the contribution of the repository structure channel, we simplified the predictor by assuming that repository units are independent and excluded the heterogeneous graph encoding. We called the simplified predictor the text-plus-metadata predictor. To evaluate the contribution of the tool metadata channel, we further dropped it, and the resulting predictor was only trained on issue text. We named the resulting predictor the text-only predictor. Table 7 compares the performance among our method, the text-plus-metadata predictor, and the text-only predictor.

Because the text-plus-metadata predictor is derived by ignoring the repository structure, the superiority of our method over it reveals the contribution of the graph encoding to the performance of our method. Similarly, the outperformance of the text-plus-metadata predictor over the text-only predictor uncovers the contribution of the tool metadata channel. As reported in Table 7, both channels contribute significantly to the performance of our method. Specifically, by encoding the repository structure, our method outperforms the text-plus-metadata predictor by E%【占位】; by introducing the tool metadata channel, the text-plus-metadata predictor surpasses the text-only predictor by F%【占位】.

We further demonstrated the superior performance of our method in different contexts. Specifically, in Online Appendix E, we evaluated the robustness of our method’s superiority over the benchmarks by varying the security budget and the damage weights. In Online Appendix F, we examined the performance of our predictor under adaptive attacks generated from the attack framework in Study 1, reporting the decrease in task-level AUC under adaptive attacks【占位】. In Online Appendix G, we employed simulation to demonstrate the generalizability of our method.

### Experiment 2: Evaluating the Prevention Benefit in Practice

While Experiment 1 shows our method’s superior predictive performance compared to an array of competing techniques, Experiment 2 aims to explore our method’s potential utility in practice. For simplicity, we compare our method with only the rule-based baseline and the reactive-only approach, which are the two representative alternatives in practice. A high task-level risk score means that a method is better at identifying tasks that would otherwise lead to successful attacks, which is the focus of our simulation experiment in Experiment 2. Indeed, for a proactive defense method to be useful in practice, it should maximize the number of prevented attacks while minimizing the disruption to normal development tasks.

The results from our simulation are summarized in Table 8. Overall, we find that our method tends to have fewer disrupted normal tasks and more prevented attacks than the benchmarks. It is noteworthy that in the rule-based baseline, the number of disrupted normal tasks is large because the rules isolate all third-party files regardless of the task-level risk. We posit that for such a baseline, the coarse-grained rules needed to achieve a high prevention rate inevitably lead to more false alarms as well as more normal tasks being disrupted. Nevertheless, the results in Table 8 suggest that there is greater utility when applying our method, rather than the alternative techniques, in practice.

To further estimate our method’s practical value, we calculate the potential cost savings if the method is implemented in practice. Previous studies have estimated the costs of software supply chain incidents and of developer productivity losses due to security interruptions【占位：需公开出处】. Since our evaluation involves coding tasks of varying complexity, we inflate these cost estimates to a common base year【占位】. This allows us to calculate the amount an organization could save from the prevented attacks. We do not consider the savings for other categories of impact because, to our knowledge, no such cost estimates are available in the literature. Finally, these savings from prevented attacks should be considered against the costs of disrupted normal tasks, which reduce the development team’s productivity. The cost savings in the last column of Table 8 are based on the difference between the savings from all prevented attacks and the costs of all disrupted tasks【占位】.

To ensure that pre-positioning does not negatively affect the performance of coding agents on legitimate tasks, we measured the task resolution rate with and without pre-positioning on a holdout sample of normal tasks. While the resolution loss stayed within 3 percentage points【占位】, the attack success rate was significantly decreased, showing a considerable increase in security without compromising task utility.

## 2.2 讨论与贡献（英文正文）

Coding agents are increasingly adopted by software development organizations to automate development tasks. Fundamental to the secure adoption of coding agents is the management of their attack surface before a task begins. Prior defense research overlooks the problem of deciding which defensive actions to pre-position under a security budget before task execution. In response to this research gap, we formulate a new defense problem (i.e., the budget-constrained proactive defense allocation problem). To solve the problem, we develop a novel multimodal attack-surface predictor to predict unit-level manipulation risks and propose an effective solution method to the problem. We demonstrate the superior performance of our method over prevalent existing methods using real-world benchmark data.

Our study belongs to the computational genre of design science research in information systems, which develops computational methods to solve business and societal problems and aims at making methodological contributions (Rai 2017, Gupta 2018). Accordingly, one contribution of our study is the formulation of a new and important security management problem for coding agent deployments, which proactively decides defensive actions based on both task-level signals and public threat intelligence. By solving the problem, our study adds to the extant literature with the following methodological contributions. First, we propose a novel multimodal attack-surface predictor to accommodate salient characteristics of the problem. Specifically, our proposed predictor differs from existing vulnerability predictors in its encoding of heterogeneous repository structures and tool metadata. Second, we formulate the allocation problem as a budget-constrained stochastic optimization model and develop an efficient solution method to the problem based on the analyzed properties of the model.

The main contributions of this research are threefold. First, we adopt a holistic view of task-level attack risk. The proposed framework fairly evaluates the contribution of all units in a coding task to the attack risk, which can then be used to better protect the deployment while maintaining development utility. Second, this work addresses the security-utility trade-off at the unit level through selective pre-positioning. The proposed framework captures two crucial dimensions of coding agent security—attack risk and task utility, traditionally studied only at the deployment level in the literature. In contrast to existing methods, we quantify each unit’s contribution and address the security-utility trade-off at the unit level. Based on their risk-utility contributions, units are selected for isolation, monitoring, or deferral to reduce attack risk while preserving task utility. Third, this research introduces a model-agnostic defense allocation framework. The proposed framework is agnostic to the underlying coding agent, the language model provider, and the metrics for evaluating attack risk and task utility.

Our study offers several design principles that guide the development of a proactive defense management system for coding agent deployments. As demonstrated in our study, a predictive analytics method characterizes risks and predicts the attack surface before a task begins. Taking the predicted risks as inputs, a prescriptive analytics method then searches through the policy space and discovers a defense allocation policy that optimizes the objective of damage reduction under a security budget. In addition, we show that, in comparison with benchmark methods, our method that integrates predictive and prescriptive analytics can attain higher prevention benefits. Without a prescriptive analytics method, a predictive analytics method alone cannot produce effective defense allocation decisions for coding agent deployments. On the other hand, a prescriptive analytics method by itself cannot generate a practical defense allocation policy as it lacks the understanding of real-world task uncertainties. Accordingly, we have design principle 1.

Design principle 1. A predictive analytics method should go hand in hand with a prescriptive analytics method in order to timely, effectively, and fairly prevent information manipulation attacks in coding agent deployments in a highly volatile environment, in which the distribution of incoming tasks is nonstationary.

We demonstrate substantial benefits of proactive defense in reducing the attack surface of coding agent deployments. For example, we show that the attack success rate incurred by our method is significantly lower than that incurred by the reactive-only approach【占位】. As a result, we have design principle 2.

Design principle 2. Practitioners need to reconsider the current practice of reacting to attacks during task execution and adopt the way of proactive defense allocation in designing a security management system for coding agent deployments, which takes both task-level signals and public threat intelligence into consideration.

Information technology (IT), especially machine learning, enables security teams to predict the attack surface of incoming tasks by analyzing patterns of public attack benchmarks and repository metadata. We show that a carefully designed multimodal predictor can effectively characterize the risk signals arising from the three attack channels and predict unit-level manipulation risks. Such prediction empowers security teams to act with foresight and avoid myopic decisions, thereby preventing more attacks while preserving development utility. Therefore, our study demonstrates the value of IT in general and machine learning in particular for the secure adoption of coding agents. Consequently, we have design principle 3.

Design principle 3. A critical precursor to proactive defense allocation is the prediction of the attack surface of incoming coding tasks. Therefore, a security management system should make effective use of proper predictive analytics technologies for attack surface prediction.

Following the evaluation procedure, we conducted experiments to evaluate the performance of each compared method. We set the sliding window size to 20 steps【占位】 and calibrated the decision threshold on a validation split to balance precision and recall【占位】, and we varied the threshold to examine the performance of the investigated methods under different false alarm requirements.

Table 9 reports the detection performance of each method. If a security team employs our method to monitor coding sessions, the F1-score is X【占位】, which is substantially higher than that of the keyword-rule baseline. The large improvement of our method over the rule-based baseline demonstrates the benefit of multimodal streaming detection and reveals the practical value of our method in intercepting attacks during task execution. Moreover, in comparison with the best single-channel detector, our method improves the detection AUC by Y%【占位】 and reduces the average detection delay by Z%【占位】. Because the only difference between the best single-channel detector and our method is the fusion of the other two channels, the improvement is attributed to the complementary information captured by the behavioral and structural channels.

To dig deeper into the superior performance of our method, let us examine two kinds of signals available during task execution. As shown in Figure 2, textual intent reflects what the task asks for, whereas behavioral sequences and call-graph structures reflect what the agent actually does. Because malicious issue text can be disguised by paraphrasing, a detector that relies solely on textual signals neglects the divergence between intent and behavior and consequently fails to flag attacks whose text appears benign. On the other hand, a method that can accurately detect the divergence between the intent and the behavior can intercept attacks before harmful actions are executed. Table 10 reports the channel-wise detection performance of each method. As reported, the channel-wise F1 of our method is A【占位】, which is substantially higher than that of the single-channel baselines and outperforms each baseline by a range of between B% and C%【占位】. The text-only baseline, on the other hand, exhibits the lowest F1 on tool description tampering attacks, whose textual signals are subtle and easily disguised【占位】.

The evaluation demonstrates the superior performance of our proposed method over each benchmark. All the performance improvements by our method reported in Tables 9–10 are statistically significant【占位】. The superiority of our method is attributed to its three-channel fusion that can accurately capture both the intent and the behavior of a coding session and its action gating that can translate detection results into defensive actions. As a result, our method achieves the best performance in detection accuracy (as reported in Table 9) and channel-level coverage (as reported in Table 10) among all the compared methods, thereby attaining the lowest missed-attack rate under the false alarm constraint.

We conducted an ablation analysis to demonstrate the contribution of each input channel of our detector to the performance of our method. Specifically, our detector features three input channels: textual intent, behavioral sequence, and call-graph structure. Because the textual intent channel is indispensable to identify the adversarial intent embedded in task inputs, our ablation analysis focuses on the contributions of the other two channels. To inspect the contribution of the behavioral sequence channel, we simplified the detector by excluding the sequence model and relied only on textual and structural signals. We called the simplified detector the text-plus-structure detector. To evaluate the contribution of the call-graph structure channel, we further dropped it, and the resulting detector was only trained on textual signals. We named the resulting detector the text-only detector. Table 11 compares the performance among our method, the text-plus-structure detector, and the text-only detector.

Because the text-plus-structure detector is derived by ignoring the behavioral sequence, the superiority of our method over it reveals the contribution of the sequence model to the performance of our method. Similarly, the outperformance of the text-plus-structure detector over the text-only detector uncovers the contribution of the structural channel. As reported in Table 11, both channels contribute significantly to the performance of our method. Specifically, by encoding the behavioral sequence, our method outperforms the text-plus-structure detector by E%【占位】; by introducing the call-graph structure, the text-plus-structure detector surpasses the text-only detector by F%【占位】.

We further demonstrated the superior performance of our method in different contexts. Specifically, in Online Appendix H, we evaluated the robustness of our method’s superiority over the benchmarks by varying the sliding window size and the decision threshold. In Online Appendix I, we examined the performance of our detector under adaptive attacks generated from the attack framework in Study 1, reporting the decrease in detection AUC under adaptive attacks【占位】. ### Experiment 2: Evaluating Detection Latency and Utility Preservation

While Experiment 1 shows our method’s superior detection performance compared to an array of competing techniques, Experiment 2 aims to explore our method’s potential utility in practice. For simplicity, we compare our method with only the keyword-rule baseline and the single-channel detector with the highest AUC, which are the two representative alternatives in practice. A high detection AUC value means that a method is better at balancing the trade-off between the interception rate and the false alarm rate, which is the focus of our simulation experiment in Experiment 2. Indeed, for a streaming detector to be useful in practice, it should maximize the number of intercepted attacks while minimizing the disruption to normal development tasks.

The results from our simulation are summarized in Table 12. Overall, we find that our method tends to have fewer false alarms and more intercepted attacks than the benchmarks. It is noteworthy that in the keyword-rule baseline, the number of false alarms is large because the rules flag any command that matches a fixed pattern regardless of context. We posit that for such a baseline, the coarse-grained rules needed to achieve a high interception rate inevitably lead to more false alarms as well as more normal actions being blocked. Nevertheless, the results in Table 12 suggest that there is greater utility when applying our method, rather than the alternative techniques, in practice.

To ensure that the detection process does not negatively affect the performance of coding agents on legitimate tasks, we measured the task resolution rate with and without the detector on a holdout sample of normal tasks from SWE-bench. While the resolution loss stayed below 5 percentage points【占位】, the attack interception rate was significantly increased, showing a considerable increase in security without compromising task utility. We also measured the end-to-end detection latency of our method. The average latency per time step was below 500 milliseconds【占位】, which is sufficiently short for gating actions in interactive coding sessions.

### Experiment 3: Enhancing Robustness with Adversarial Retraining

We implemented our adversarial retraining design to enhance the robustness of the streaming detector against unseen attacks. The bypass rate was then measured on the unseen attack instances in D_att for each of the three identified agent baselines. Table 13 compares the bypass rate before and after our adversarial retraining across the three agent baselines. The results suggest that after adopting adversarial retraining, the detector was substantially more robust against unseen attacks. Adversarial retraining reduced the average bypass rate by 4, 7, and 11 times【占位】 against the DeepSeek-, Qwen-, and Llama-based baselines, respectively. Accordingly, on average, adversarial retraining decreased the bypass rates across all three agent baselines by approximately X%【占位】, translating into a Y-fold increase in robustness【占位】. To ensure that the robustification process does not negatively affect the detector’s performance on correctly recognizing benign trajectories, we measured the performance of the robustified detector on benign trajectories from SWE-Gym. While the false positive rate of the robustified detector on benign trajectories stayed the same【占位】, the bypass rate was significantly decreased, showing a considerable increase in robustness against adversarial attacks.

## 3.2 讨论与贡献（英文正文）

The experiment results demonstrate the effectiveness of our streaming detection framework. The first set of evaluations demonstrated the utility of the three-channel fusion design and the action gating mechanism. We found that none of the baseline detectors were robust to the attacks generated by the attack framework in Study 1, confirming our research motivation. The second set of evaluations gauged the effectiveness of the adversarial retraining design. Our experiment results suggest that adversarial retraining was able to significantly enhance the robustness of our detector. Our findings have important design implications for the growing body of IS literature on AI security. The results also provide insights for the broader communities of practitioners developing and deploying coding agents. The major takeaways are three-fold.

First, streaming detection should be an integral part of securing coding agents. Coding agents are facing greater risks of exploitation as these applications are increasingly utilized for mission-critical software development. Our results in Experiment 1 have shown that attacks with disguised textual signals could evade keyword-based detectors by X%【占位】. This finding is generalizable to numerous application domains because standard rule-based detectors are generally plagued by their vulnerability to semantically manipulated inputs. To account for threats exploiting the input channels of coding agents, the IS security literature needs to be extended by incorporating the consideration of streaming detection. For organizations, deploying streaming detection for coding agents is essential for ensuring the security of development workflows informed by coding agents. Specifically, detection and action gating should be guided by detection performance augmented with adversarial robustness in operating coding agent deployments.

Second, both textual intent and behavioral evidence provide valuable perspectives for detecting information manipulation. As the textual signals of attacks and the behavioral deviations they induce are complementary, our three-channel fusion jointly leverages both for detection. The textual channel directly reveals the adversarial intent embedded in issues and tool descriptions, which is particularly useful when the behavior has not yet deviated. The behavioral and structural channels provide a comprehensive quantification of the divergence between what a task asks and what an agent does. This design can be valuable in application domains where the attacker deliberately disguises textual signals. In our study, these channels are instantiated in the context of coding agents; however, the fusion design is generalizable to other agentic AI systems.

Third, adversarial retraining with a dedicated attack benchmark is an effective design principle in enhancing the robustness of streaming detectors. Adversarial retraining essentially reduces the vulnerable assumption underlying supervised detection by augmenting the training set to account for the distribution of adversarial attacks. In Experiment 3, we observed decreases up to X%【占位】 in the bypass rate and increases up to Y%【占位】 in detection AUC when adversarial retraining was applied. Moreover, these design principles not only provide guidelines for developing new coding agent security systems but can also be applicable to existing detection systems seeking to improve their adversarial robustness without significant changes to their deployment.

Our study makes several key contributions to research and practice. First, we contribute to the emerging literature on AI security and governance by examining the unique security concern of information manipulation attacks facing human-AI hybrids. Specifically, we show that, while a coding agent can be evaluated as effective in benign settings, the inherent vulnerability of its input channels may compromise the effectiveness of the agent under adversarial attacks. Therefore, the security of AI constitutes a major consideration in developing and deploying coding agents for task automation. Second, we extend the IS security analytics literature by incorporating streaming detection into the evaluation and operation of coding agents. As demonstrated in the literature review, existing detection systems for coding agents are predominantly rule-based and static, and the utilization of behavioral and structural signals has been completely absent from the literature up until this point. To the best of our knowledge, this research represents the first instance in which a three-channel streaming detector with adversarial retraining was used for securing coding agents. Third, our study makes a design contribution by proposing a detection framework based on the defense-in-depth principle for assessing and enhancing the security of coding agent deployments. The need for more computational work of this nature is echoed in many recent calls for more design science work in IS journals (Agarwal and Dhar 2014, Goes 2014, Rai 2017).

This study has important implications for practitioners. As coding agents that support software development constitute an increasingly critical endeavor, the security of coding agent deployments emerges as a paramount concern. Our research can shed light on the significance of this concern to organizations, and the framework presented in this study is designed to mitigate this concern. While task utility remains an essential consideration for coding agents, the security emphasized by our proposed framework provides considerable complementary value to allow safer adoption of coding agents at the risk of adversarial attacks. Moreover, in addition to traditional software security strategies such as code review, static analysis, and dependency scanning, there is an increasing need for organizations to impose policies and procedures to secure the input channels of coding agents in designing, implementing, and operating their deployments.

- **总预算**：设备/云租约 $1.5–3k + API 约 $1.5–2.7k，合计约 $4–6k；三研究串行 10–12 个月（并行 6–8 个月）。
- **论证要点**：技术栈全部为成熟开源组件（PPO 实现、异构图 GNN、自编码器、序列模型、开源整数规划），无未验证的前沿依赖；训练规模（策略网络、中小规模编码器）与 2×4090 匹配，不需要 H100 级资源。

## 4.3 实验有效性的论证设计（不依赖绝对数字）

1. **相对比较为主**：所有核心结论以"叠加防护前后/与基线的差异"报告（ASR 降幅、resolve 率损失、预算-收益曲线），不依赖单一绝对数字——这同时是审稿稳健性要求（不同基底 agent 下结论一致）与实施可行性的保障。
2. **公开基准独立评估**：研究二/三的主评估使用 AgentDojo/MalSkillBench 等公开基准（独立于本研究构建的 D_att），D_att 仅作补充压力测试——避免"用自产攻击评估自产防御"的内卷质疑。
3. **试金石先行**：研究二的核心假设（任务前静态信息可预测攻击面）用小型可行性实验先行验证（AgentDojo 二分类 AUC≥0.7【占位】再推进正式版）；研究一奖励信号用 20 实例危害判定器校准一致性【占位 ≥80%】先行验证。
4. **统计检验**：结果报告显著性检验（与研究二正文 S15 一致）与置信区间（如 FDNH 的成本节省 95% CI）。
5. **风险与缓解的论文表述**：
   - RL 训练稳定性 → 行为克隆监督预训练+PPO 微调（不从零训练）；
   - 危害判定器可靠性 → 先校准（CodeQL+LLM-as-judge 双判定一致性）；
   - 防御的效用代价 → 每项防御实验同时报告 Autonomy Tax（任务效用保持为第一约束，研究二 <3pp【占位】、研究三 <5pp【占位】）；
   - 泛化性 → 跨仓库迁移、跨 agent 基线、参数敏感性（附录）。

## 附：本轮写作的元教训（供后续所有章节复用）

1. **回到原文的真正作用**：逐句对照时发现的不是措辞问题，而是**结构缺漏**——例如研究一草稿漏掉 RADAR 的 Overall 综合段、漏掉第二高频通道的跨基线差异句。只凭"我记得 RADAR 大概讲了什么"永远发现不了这些；必须把原文句子摆在旁边逐句数。
2. **句式骨架 vs 内容照抄**：可复制的部分是"论证链结构"（事实→解释→升华；承认→空白→方法→结果→含义），不可复制的是领域特定断言与数字。本审计中所有"逐字复刻"句都出现在 signpost（Our contribution ... is threefold）、衔接（To this end / As a result）、机制推导（Because the only difference ... is attributed to ...）等骨架位置。
3. **三研究共用模板的边界**：系列一致性的红利在于审稿人可预期性（同一套结果-讨论模板），但必须用领域差异防止"模板感"过强——研究一的 RADAR 模板、研究二的 PRR/FDNH/#50 模板、研究三的 ARText/#32/RADAR 模板，各有主模板且刻意不同。
4. **未决项必须显式记录**：研究三贡献段的理论锚（3.3.4 D5）、研究二的定理/近似比问题（2.3.3 D2 S3）、FDNH 式"己方局部劣势"的报告位置（2.3.2 S4）——这些是定稿前必须解决的写作决策，已在本审计中标注位置与两条出路。
