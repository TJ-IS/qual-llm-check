# Changes from the completed pilot prompt

1. Precision is explicitly prioritized over recall.
2. `moderate` has been removed from the positive strength levels; only `exemplary` and `strong` qualify.
3. The earlier five-gate chain is expanded to seven gates separating:
   - constitutive IS phenomenon,
   - prospective knowledge-to-design derivation,
   - identifiable distinctive component,
   - direct benchmark test of that component,
   - failed generic rewrite,
   - return to reusable IS knowledge.
4. The generic rewrite must change at least two article-level elements.
5. Theory citation is explicitly insufficient; prospective design translation and evidence pointers are required.
6. A technical benchmark disconnected from the IS artifact/design claim is explicitly excluded.
7. Generic analytical economics/OR is excluded unless digital actor relations or organizational processes materially alter the design and objective comparison.
8. Standard CS benchmarks are explicitly accepted; benchmark style is not used as a proxy for disciplinary distinctiveness.
9. “Beyond a transient score advantage” is integrated into the main contribution gate rather than treated as a separate durability classifier.
10. No coding-agent analogy, metric generation, or thesis design appears anywhere in the prompt.
11. Previous decisions are not provided, preventing anchoring on the pilot output.
