# 20 篇核心编程研究的 Scopus 施引检索式
## 字段说明
新版 Scopus 的施引检索字段是 `REFEID(EID)`：它返回参考文献中包含该 EID 的文献，即“引用了该种子论文的文献”。实际界面验证表明，旧写法 `CITEID(EID)` 在当前 Scopus 中不能正确完成这个任务。
20 篇种子的本地 `Cited by` 数相加为 932，但同一篇后续文献可能同时引用多篇种子，不能把 932 当成唯一施引文献数。下面的组合检索由 Scopus 去重后得到 807 条 EID 唯一的记录。
## 全部 20 篇的组合检索式
```text
REFEID(2-s2.0-85088022397) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-84994330775) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-77954305944) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-77954266297) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-60649093502) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-57049159852) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33747759674) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33749575086) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33750169459) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33744808509) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0035501733) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0042633688) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0033277397) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0032043170) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0032223745) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0346613452) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-21444451960) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-4244025449) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-21344452166) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0346032623)
```
## 年代分段
将上面的组合检索式放进括号，再分别追加：
```text
AND PUBYEAR > 2020
AND PUBYEAR > 2015 AND PUBYEAR < 2021
AND PUBYEAR > 2005 AND PUBYEAR < 2016
AND PUBYEAR < 2006
```
四段分别对应 2021 年及以后、2016–2020、2006–2015、2005 年及以前。
## 与现代编程智能体/生成式 AI 的直接连接
```text
(REFEID(2-s2.0-85088022397) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-84994330775) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-77954305944) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-77954266297) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-60649093502) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-57049159852) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33747759674) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33749575086) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33750169459) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-33744808509) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0035501733) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0042633688) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0033277397) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0032043170) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0032223745) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0346613452) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-21444451960) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-4244025449) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-21344452166) OR REFEID(2-s2.0-0346032623))
AND TITLE-ABS-KEY("generative AI" OR "generative artificial intelligence" OR "large language model*" OR LLM OR ChatGPT OR Copilot OR Codex OR CodeGen OR InCoder OR "AI pair programmer*" OR "coding agent*" OR "programming agent*" OR "AI-assisted programming" OR "AI-assisted coding" OR "AI code generation")
```
注意：该检索式只找“引用这 20 篇旧种子且使用现代 AI 术语”的交集，不能代表全部现代编程智能体研究。大量新文献不会引用这些旧种子，应另做独立的现代主题检索。
