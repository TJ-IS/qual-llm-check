# Corporate Blogging and Job Performance: Effects of Work-related and Nonwork-related Participation




# 企业博客和工作绩效：工作相关和非工作相关参与的影响


BENJIANG LU, XUNHUA GUO, NIANLONG LUO, AND GUOQING CHEN




卢本江、郭循华、罗年龙、陈国庆

ABSTRACT: Corporate blogs are expected to facilitate communication, knowledge sharing, and collaborative innovation within organizations. However, empirical evidence has yet to be found illustrating whether and how such applications have affected job performance. Drawing upon social network theory, we postulate a conceptual model suggesting that employees’ online social relationships accumulated through work- and nonwork-related blog participation will engender different effects on job performance. The model is empirically tested using digital trace and archival data collected from two in-practice systems of a large telecommunications company. The results reveal that, in the workrelated blog network, the structural and cognitive dimensions of social relationships positively affect job performance, whereas the relational dimension shows a negative influence. Meanwhile, participation in nonwork-related blog network benefits job performance for employees with a high level of performance in the previous time period, but is detrimental for other employees. The findings uncover the influencing mechanism of corporate blogging on job performance and offer practical advice for managers to better exploit the value of intraorganizational social media.




摘要：企业博客有望促进组织内部的沟通、知识共享和协作创新。然而，尚未找到经验证据来说明此类应用程序是否以及如何影响工作绩效。借鉴社交网络理论，我们假设了一个概念模型，表明员工通过工作和非工作相关的博客参与积累的在线社交关系将对工作绩效产生不同的影响。该模型使用从一家大型电信公司的两个实际系统收集的数字跟踪和档案数据进行了实证测试。结果表明，在工作相关的博客网络中，社会关系的结构和认知维度对工作绩效产生积极影响，而关系维度则表现出消极影响。同时，参与与工作无关的博客网络有利于前一段时间表现较高的员工的工作绩效，但对其他员工不利。研究结果揭示了企业博客对工作绩效的影响机制，并为管理者更好地挖掘组织内社交媒体的价值提供了实用建议。


KEY WORDS AND PHRASES: blogs, corporate blogs, digital traces, job performance, organizational social media, social capital, social network.




关键词和短语：博客、企业博客、数字痕迹、工作绩效、组织社交媒体、社会资本、社交网络。


The widespread fusion of social media applications in companies has reshaped the traditional business environment and caused phenomenon of social economy to boom [22, 63]. The economic effect of the fusion is expected to exceed \$1 trillion, based on McKinsey’s evaluation [22]. Efficient communication and collaboration within and across organizations are crucial antecedents of such success [52]. In terms of academic research, the effects of social media on organizations have become an important area of interest during the past few years [15, 17, 22, 52].




社交媒体应用在企业中的广泛融合重塑了传统的商业环境，引发了社会经济的繁荣现象[22, 63]。根据麦肯锡的评估，核聚变的经济效应预计将超过 1 万亿美元[22]。组织内部和组织之间的有效沟通和协作是这种成功的关键前提[52]。在学术研究方面，社交媒体对组织的影响在过去几年中已成为一个重要的关注领域[15,17,22,52]。


As one of the earliest and most classical social media applications, blogs are considered a suitable technology for building an agile communication platform that can effectively facilitate communication, knowledge sharing, and innovation within companies [15, 17, 27, 41]. A notable number of companies such as IBM, Google, and Microsoft are spending considerable resources on constructing internal blogging platforms to facilitate employees’ communication and collaboration with the expectation of enhancing their job outcomes [13, 55]. However, little empirical evidence has been found to illustrate whether and how such applications have actually aided or hindered job performance [58]. The relationship between corporate blogs and job performance remains underexplored.




作为最早、最经典的社交媒体应用之一，博客被认为是构建敏捷沟通平台的合适技术，可以有效促进企业内部的沟通、知识共享和创新[15,17,27,41]。 IBM、谷歌和微软等许多公司正在花费大量资源构建内部博客平台，以促进员工的沟通和协作，以期提高他们的工作成果[13, 55]。然而，几乎没有发现经验证据来说明此类应用程序是否以及如何实际上帮助或阻碍了工作绩效[58]。企业博客与工作绩效之间的关系仍未得到充分探索。


As a kind of social media application, employees’ activities on corporate blogs lead to the formation of diverse online social relationships. Employees’ involvement in different kinds of blog activities and their heterogeneity in using corporate blogs may engender a complicated influence on job performance. On the one hand, the nature of such social media allows users to engage in both work- and nonwork-related activities [79], which could potentially engender different effects on job performance. For example, employees’ work-related activities, such as reading work-related posts or exchanging opinions and advice via corporate blogs, may positively affect their task execution and performance. In contrast, nonwork-related activities are not directly related to task execution and can sometimes even negatively influence job improvements.




作为一种社交媒体应用，员工在企业博客上的活动导致了多样化的在线社交关系的形成。员工参与不同类型的博客活动以及使用企业博客的异质性可能会对工作绩效产生复杂的影响。一方面，此类社交媒体的性质允许用户参与与工作和非工作相关的活动[79]，这可能会对工作绩效产生不同的影响。例如，员工与工作相关的活动，例如阅读与工作相关的帖子或通过公司博客交换意见和建议，可能会对他们的任务执行和绩效产生积极影响。相反，与工作无关的活动与任务执行没有直接关系，有时甚至会对工作改进产生负面影响。


On the other hand, as the use of a corporate blog is usually voluntary, different employees may use it in different ways, which has varied consequences [42, 79]. In other words, employees’ heterogeneity may affect their participation in corporate blogs. For example, if the employees are more efficient in their daily work, they may be more aware of what they need and will use a corporate blog in a more controllable manner. In contrast, when employees who are not so efficient or autonomic engage in corporate blogging, they can easily get “lost” and conduct blog loafing activities, especially for nonwork-related blog activities. For these two kinds of employees, the same blog activities can generate different effects on their performance. However, scarce previous research emphasizes the above two important issues when investigating the influence of organizational social media. Aiming to fill in such a gap, our research questions are:




另一方面，由于企业博客的使用通常是自愿的，不同的员工可能会以不同的方式使用它，从而产生不同的后果[42, 79]。换句话说，员工的异质性可能会影响他们对企业博客的参与。例如，如果员工的日常工作效率更高，他们可能会更清楚自己需要什么，并会以更可控的方式使用企业博客。相反，当效率或自主性不高的员工参与企业博客时，他们很容易“迷失”并进行博客闲逛活动，尤其是与工作无关的博客活动。对于这两类员工来说，相同的博客活动会对他们的绩效产生不同的影响。然而，以往的研究在调查组织社交媒体的影响时很少强调上述两个重要问题。为了填补这一空白，我们的研究问题是：


RQ1: What are the influences of employees’ work-related corporate blog participation on their job performance?




RQ1：员工参与与工作相关的企业博客对其工作绩效有何影响？


RQ2: What are the influences of employees’ nonwork-related corporate blog participation on their job performance?




RQ2：员工参与与工作无关的企业博客对其工作绩效有何影响？


RQ3: How does the employees’ heterogeneity shape the influences of their corporate blog participation on job performance?




RQ3：员工的异质性如何影响他们的企业博客参与对工作绩效的影响？


Drawing on social network theory, this paper develops a conceptual model that illustrates the relationship between employees’ work- and nonwork-related corporate blog participation on job performance via integrating employees’ heterogeneity. Our findings reveal that the various dimensions of employees’ work- and nonworkrelated corporate blog participation could engender different effects on job performance, and as a reflection of employees’ heterogeneity, employees’ preperformance is an important moderator that could reshape the influence of corporate blogging on job performance.




本文借鉴社交网络理论，建立了一个概念模型，通过整合员工的异质性来说明员工工作相关和非工作相关的企业博客参与与工作绩效之间的关系。我们的研究结果表明，员工工作相关和非工作相关的企业博客参与的不同维度会对工作绩效产生不同的影响，并且作为员工异质性的反映，员工的预绩效是一个重要的调节因素，可以重塑企业博客对工作绩效的影响。

## 相关文献


## Job Performance




## 工作表现


Job performance is a classical research topic in the area of organizational behavior, and cumulative studies have engaged in investigating the factors that influence job performance. One research stream focuses mainly on individual internal factors. For example, researchers have found that an employee’s individual personality derived from the Big Five Model can affect job performance [35, 77]; meanwhile, other research has argued that employees’ knowledge, ability, and skill (KAS) influence their performance [14, 44]. Another stream investigates the influence of external factors on job performance [70, 87], such as organizational culture, working environment, task characteristics, external incentives, and social influence. Among these factors, social influence has aroused extensive attention in recent years, especially against the background of social media applications. Social network theory has been regarded as suitable for explaining performance variation. In the abundant performance research in the lens of social network theory, two perspectives have emerged. The first one is the view based on “network resource,” which focuses on investigating the influence of resource flow in the network, such as advice [81, 91], friendship [8, 20], hindrance [89], and so on. The second stream is the view based on “network configuration,” which pays more attention to the effects of network configurations, such as centrality [2, 5], structural holes [11, 12, 76], and network density [32, 66].




工作绩效是组织行为领域的一个经典研究课题，累积的研究致力于调查影响工作绩效的因素。一个研究方向主要关注个人的内部因素。例如，研究人员发现，源自大五模型的员工个人性格可以影响工作绩效 [35, 77]；与此同时，其他研究认为员工的知识、能力和技能 (KAS) 会影响他们的绩效 [14, 44]。另一流研究外部因素对工作绩效的影响[70, 87]，例如组织文化、工作环境、任务特征、外部激励和社会影响。其中，社会影响力近年来引起了广泛关注，特别是在社交媒体应用的背景下。社交网络理论被认为适合解释绩效变化。在社交网络理论视角下的大量绩效研究中，出现了两种观点。第一种是基于“网络资源”的观点，重点考察网络中资源流动的影响，如建议[81, 91]、友谊[8, 20]、阻碍[89]等。第二个流派是基于“网络配置”的观点，它更关注网络配置的影响，例如中心性[2, 5]、结构洞[11, 12, 76]和网络密度[32, 66]。


For the network resource-based view, Ibarra’s [47] conceptual framework, which makes a distinction between instrumental and expressive network ties, is referenced most frequently. Instrumental network ties are those developed for information, advice, and resource exchanges that are needed for work. Expressive ties are affective ties carrying either positive or negative emotions that are not necessarily work-related [47, 48]. For example, Sykes et al. [81] focused on the influence of advice networks on job performance after enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems implementation, and found that influences vary between the workflow advice network and software advice network. Zhang and Venkatesh [91] characterized network ties as direct and indirect ties in both online and offline workflow networks, and they found that employees’ online and offline networks act as complementary resources that interact to influence their job performance.




对于基于网络资源的观点，最常引用的是 Ibarra 的 [47] 概念框架，该框架区分了工具性网络联系和表达性网络联系。工具性网络联系是为工作所需的信息、建议和资源交换而建立的。表达性联系是情感联系，带有积极或消极的情绪，不一定与工作相关[47, 48]。例如，赛克斯等人。 [81]重点研究了企业资源规划（ERP）系统实施后建议网络对工作绩效的影响，发现工作流建议网络和软件建议网络的影响有所不同。张和文卡特什[91]将网络联系描述为线上和线下工作流网络中的直接和间接联系，他们发现员工的线上和线下网络作为互补资源，相互作用影响他们的工作绩效。


For the network configuration-based view, numerous studies have found that centrality is tightly related to individual performance in both online and offline settings [2, 5, 8, 20]. For example, Bowler and Brass [8] found that job characteristics, which include skill variety, task identity, task significance, autonomy, and task feedback, mediate the relationship between centrality and job performance. Structural holes is another important indicator of network configurations. Generally speaking, when two separate clusters possess nonredundant information, a structural hole exists between them [9]. Some studies have shown that structural holes are related to job performance [11, 12]. For instance, Burt et al. [12] found that the relationship between structural holes and job performance is contingent on rank and gender. Other researchers also found that network density, which describes the connectedness of a network, influences job performance [32, 66, 76].




对于基于网络配置的观点，大量研究发现中心性与在线和离线环境中的个人表现密切相关[2,5,8,20]。例如，Bowler 和 Brass [8] 发现工作特征，包括技能多样性、任务同一性、任务重要性、自主性和任务反馈，调节中心性和工作绩效之间的关系。结构洞是网络配置的另一个重要指标。一般来说，当两个独立的簇拥有非冗余信息时，它们之间就存在结构空洞[9]。一些研究表明结构洞与工作绩效相关[11, 12]。例如，伯特等人。 [12]发现结构漏洞和工作绩效之间的关系取决于职位和性别。其他研究人员还发现，描述网络连通性的网络密度会影响工作绩效[32,66,76]。


Besides considering social network through resource and configuration view, as one of the major branches of social network theory, social capital theory provides an integrated view that includes the structural, relational, and cognitive dimensions of social relationships. We infer that such a multidimensional view is appropriate for our research scenario and we provide a review of social capital theory below.




除了从资源和配置的角度考虑社会网络之外，作为社会网络理论的主要分支之一，社会资本理论提供了一个包括社会关系的结构、关系和认知维度的综合视图。我们推断这种多维观点适合我们的研究场景，并且我们在下面对社会资本理论进行了回顾。


## Corporate Blogs




## 企业博客


Corporate blogs share attributes with public blogs. Employees can write diversified content via blogs, including topics such as working experience, skills, expert knowledge, and so on, which are work-related; and personal stories, reviews, feelings, and emotions, and the like, which are nonwork-related [42]. Employees can form social links with those who share the same interests and further aggregate blog rings through constant interaction [17]. All these social links form a complex network. Information, ideas, and opinions flow and spread throughout the organization via such a network [37, 69]. Employees are expected to read, write, link, and comment on others’ posts, and abundant research has discussed the value of such participation behaviors on blog platforms [27, 42, 74, 86].




企业博客与公共博客共享属性。员工可以通过博客撰写多元化的内容，包括工作经验、技能、专业知识等与工作相关的话题；以及与工作无关的个人故事、评论、感受和情感等[42]。员工可以与具有相同兴趣的人形成社交联系，并通过不断的互动进一步聚合博客圈[17]。所有这些社会联系形成了一个复杂的网络。信息、想法和意见通过这样的网络在整个组织中流动和传播[37, 69]。员工应该阅读、撰写、链接和评论他人的帖子，大量研究讨论了博客平台上此类参与行为的价值[27,42,74,86]。


Among all these blog participation behaviors, writing, which represents a type of knowledge sharing and contribution activities, has been widely discussed [16, 33]. Meanwhile, some researchers have focused on studying commenting or linking behavior in blog communities [39, 62]. Furthermore, some studies have engaged in learning blog reading behavior [79]. However, there is still little research on the relationship between corporate blog activities and job performance, and our research aims to fill this gap to some extent.




在所有这些博客参与行为中，写作作为一种知识共享和贡献活动受到了广泛的讨论[16, 33]。与此同时，一些研究人员专注于研究博客社区中的评论或链接行为 [39, 62]。此外，一些研究致力于学习博客阅读行为[79]。然而，目前关于企业博客活动与工作绩效关系的研究还很少，我们的研究旨在在一定程度上填补这一空白。


## Social Capital Theory




## 社会资本理论


As a major branch of social network theory, social capital theory has developed for decades, and the definition of social capital has been broadly refined [1, 7, 11, 23, 68, 72]. As part of this development, three major views of social capital have emerged: an external perspective [7, 11, 73], an internal perspective [23], and a combined perspective [68]. The classical external perspective defined social capital as “friends, colleagues and more-general contacts through whom you receive opportunities to use your financial and human capital” [59, p. 9], focusing on the external relation foregrounds that have been called the “bridging” forms of social capital [10, 34, 73]. The internal perspective proposed that “social capital is defined by its function. It is not a single entity, but a variety of different entities having two characteristics in common: they all consist of some aspect of social structure, and they facilitate certain actions of individuals who are within the structure” [23, p. 302]. This definition mainly focused on internal relationships within collective foregrounds—the “bonding” forms of social capital. Combining the internal and external views, Nahapiet and Ghoshal proposed their definition of social capital: “the sum of actual and potential resources embedded within, available through and derived from the network of relationships possessed by an individual or social unit” [68, p. 243]. They defined social capital from a multidimensional perspective that includes structural, relational, and cognitive dimensions. The structural dimension includes structural links or connections among individuals or collectives, and includes mainly network ties, network configuration, and appropriable organization; the relational dimension means that individual or collective relationships have strong, positive characteristics that refer to trust, norms, obligations, and identification; and the cognitive dimension illustrates that individuals or collectives have the cognitive capability to understand and apply common knowledge represented as shared codes, language, and narratives. Based on this framework, various constructs have been introduced. We list some of the most widely discussed constructs in Table 1.




作为社会网络理论的一个主要分支，社会资本理论已经发展了几十年，社会资本的定义也得到了广泛的细化[1,7,11,23,68,72]。作为这一发展的一部分，出现了三种主要的社会资本观点：外部视角[7,11,73]、内部视角[23]和综合视角[68]。经典的外部视角将社会资本定义为“朋友、同事和更一般的联系人，通过他们你有机会使用你的财务和人力资本”[59，第 17 页]。 9]，重点关注被称为社会资本“桥接”形式的外部关系前景[10,34,73]。内部视角提出“社会资本是根据其功能来定义的。它不是一个单一的实体，而是各种不同的实体，它们具有两个共同的特征：它们都由社会结构的某些方面组成，并促进结构内个体的某些行为”[23，p.11]。 302]。这个定义主要关注集体前景中的内部关系——社会资本的“纽带”形式。结合内部和外部的观点，Nahapiet 和 Ghoshal 提出了他们对社会资本的定义：“嵌入在个人或社会单位所拥有的关系网络中、可通过其获得并衍生自该网络的实际和潜在资源的总和”[68，第 17 页]。 243]。他们从多维角度定义了社会资本，包括结构、关系和认知维度。结构维度包括个人或集体之间的结构性联系或联系，主要包括网络联系、网络配置和适当组织；关系维度是指个人或集体关系具有强烈、积极的特征，包括信任、规范、义务和认同；认知维度表明个人或集体具有理解和应用以共享代码、语言和叙述为代表的共同知识的认知能力。基于该框架，引入了各种构造。我们在表 1 中列出了一些讨论最广泛的结构。


Social capital theory has been proved to be a suitable theoretical foundation for studies related to collective actions [1, 85], career success [11, 36], knowledge creation [68], knowledge sharing and transfer [15, 19, 49, 64], resource exchange [26, 30], and performance [30, 53, 71], where research settings are all networkbased, including both online and offline. In our research context of corporate blogging, employees form an online social network through various blog activities. Hence, we propose that social capital theory also corresponds to our research background.

## Research Model and Hypotheses




## 研究模型和假设


Employees’ diverse corporate blogging activities lead to the formation of online social relationships. In order to justify these online relationships, we refer to the typical framework of social capital theory proposed by Nahapiet and Ghoshal [68]. In the model, they define structural, relational, and cognitive dimensions of social capital for understanding intellectual knowledge creation and sharing within organizations, which are in correspondence to corporate blog activities. For example, employees may construct structural advantages on the blogging network through diversified linking strategies [62]; they can develop social norms and trust, and accelerate cooperation and coordination with each other; they can also foster common understanding with others and form blog rings through their specific posting behaviors [15, 16, 17].




员工多样化的企业博客活动导致了在线社交关系的形成。为了证明这些在线关系的合理性，我们参考了 Nahapiet 和 Ghoshal 提出的社会资本理论的典型框架[68]。在该模型中，他们定义了社会资本的结构、关系和认知维度，以理解组织内知识知识的创造和共享，这与企业博客活动相对应。例如，员工可以通过多样化的链接策略在博客网络上构建结构性优势[62]；他们可以建立社会规范和信任，并加速彼此的合作与协调；他们还可以通过特定的发帖行为与他人建立共识并形成博客圈[15,16,17]。


Meanwhile, employees may engage in both work- and nonwork-related activities on the blog platform, which engenders the typical division of blog networks into instrumental networks and expressive networks [47, 48]. We propose that employees’ involvement in these two different networks will engender different consequences for their performance. Besides considering the effects of blog participation on job performance, we also consider employees’ heterogeneity in using a corporate blog. As discussed above, employees’ efficiency or autonomy can reshape the influences of blog participation. Hence, we adopt employees’ preperformance as a reflection of their self-efficacy [4, 61, 80]. Meanwhile, we postulate that moderation effects exist between employees’ preperformance and corporate blog participation, in both work- and nonwork-related networks.




同时，员工可以在博客平台上从事与工作和非工作相关的活动，这导致博客网络典型地分为工具性网络和表达性网络[47, 48]。我们认为，员工参与这两个不同的网络将会对其绩效产生不同的影响。除了考虑博客参与对工作绩效的影响外，我们还考虑员工使用企业博客的异质性。如上所述，员工的效率或自主权可以重塑博客参与的影响。因此，我们采用员工的预绩效作为他们自我效能感的反映[4,61,80]。同时，我们假设在工作相关网络和非工作相关网络中，员工的预表现和企业博客参与之间都存在调节效应。

## Structural Dimension




## 结构维度


In studies focusing on individuals as the unit of analysis, we adopt the egocentric conceptualization of social networks that refers to an individual’s online interactions with others. We first consider in-degree centrality, which is defined as the total number of units from which a focal unit has received resources [83]. For example, employees may receive work-related information, experience, and knowledge through incoming comments, which can be reflected in in-degree centrality. Correspondingly, out-degree centrality is defined as the total number of units with which a focal unit has actively exchanged resources [24]. For example, employees may attempt to share information with others through reading and commenting on their peers’ blogs.




在以个体为分析单位的研究中，我们采用以自我为中心的社交网络概念，指的是个体与他人的在线互动。我们首先考虑度中心性，它被定义为焦点单元已从其接收资源的单元总数[83]。例如，员工可以通过传入的评论接收与工作相关的信息、经验和知识，这可以反映在度中心性中。相应地，出度中心性被定义为与焦点单元主动交换资源的单元总数[24]。例如，员工可能会尝试通过阅读和评论同事的博客来与其他人分享信息。


With respect to job performance improvements, correct and timely access of workrelated resources plays a significant role [2, 5, 10, 81, 91]. In/out-degree centralities in the work-related network serve this goal. From the resource perspective, the workrelated network is constituted by employees’ formal routine activities online and serves as a complementary channel providing work-related information, advice, experience, and knowledge. Employees’ structural advantages can largely support the access of these resources when they encounter problems in their work. More concretely, if employees hold a high in/out-degree centrality in a work-related blogging network, it can be more convenient and easier for them to receive and acquire specific resources from their peers and integrate them to obtain higher effectiveness. Meanwhile, these employees can also find different ways of thinking through feedback from their peers, which could be potentially useful for their work. Hence, we propose H1 and H2:




对于工作绩效的提高，正确、及时地访问工作相关资源起着重要作用[2,5,10,81,91]。工作相关网络中的入/出度中心性服务于这一目标。从资源角度看，工作相关网络是由员工在网上进行的正式日常活动构成的，是提供工作相关信息、建议、经验和知识的补充渠道。员工的结构性优势很大程度上可以支持他们在工作中遇到问题时获取这些资源。更具体地说，如果员工在与工作相关的博客网络中拥有较高的进/出度中心性，他们就可以更方便、更容易地从同事那里接收和获取特定资源并进行整合，从而获得更高的效率。同时，这些员工还可以通过同事的反馈找到不同的思维方式，这可能对他们的工作有用。因此，我们提出H1和H2：


Hypothesis 1: Employees’ in-degree centrality in a work-related network has a positive influence on job performance;




假设1：员工在工作相关网络中的度中心性对工作绩效有正向影响；


Hypothesis 2: Employees’ out-degree centrality in a work-related network has a positive influence on job performance.




假设2：员工在工作相关网络中的外度中心性对工作绩效有正向影响。


## Relational Dimension




## 关系维度


Relational dimension indicates that individuals’ relationships have strong, positive characteristics that are usually bound to trust, norms, obligations, and identification [68]. In our research, we adopt reciprocity as a measure of individuals’ relational dimension of online interactive relationships. In general, reciprocity conveys that favorable past interactions lead to expectations about positive future interactions. Many researchers have found that reciprocity is related to electronic networks of practice, information sharing, group performance, and so on [23, 64]. However, we propose that the influencing mechanism of reciprocity may vary across work- and nonwork-related networks.




关系维度表明个人的关系具有强烈、积极的特征，通常与信任、规范、义务和认同相关[68]。在我们的研究中，我们采用互惠作为衡量个体在线互动关系维度的指标。一般来说，互惠表明过去有利的互动会导致对未来积极互动的预期。许多研究人员发现互惠与电子实践网络、信息共享、群体绩效等有关[23, 64]。然而，我们认为互惠的影响机制可能在工作相关网络和非工作相关网络中有所不同。


In a work-related blog network, employees’ reciprocal interactions could bring benefits for performance improvements through exchanging resources, fostering trust, and cultivating collaboration. First, reciprocal interactions among employees can enhance work-related exchanges of resources, which can strengthen employees comprehension and assimilation of specific work-related information, advice, and knowledge, and further bring benefits for performance improvements [50]; second, employees’ cooperation and coordination have become tremendously important in modern corporations, and if an employee wants to improve his/her performance, he/ she needs to cooperate with others to achieve synergy improvements [56, 57]. In our research, employees’ reciprocal interactions can foster trust among employees [23, 49, 64] and cultivate opportunities for collaboration and cooperation [56, 57], consequently improving their performance. Hence, we propose the following hypothesis:




在与工作相关的博客网络中，员工的相互互动可以通过交换资源、培养信任和培养协作来提高绩效。首先，员工之间的相互互动可以增强与工作相关的资源交换，可以加强员工对具体工作相关信息、建议和知识的理解和吸收，进一步为绩效改进带来好处[50]；其次，员工的合作与协调在现代企业中变得非常重要，如果员工想要提高自己的绩效，他/她需要与他人合作以实现协同改进[56, 57]。在我们的研究中，员工的相互互动可以培养员工之间的信任[23,49,64]，并培养协作与合作的机会[56,57]，从而提高他们的绩效。因此，我们提出以下假设：


Hypothesis 3: Employees’ reciprocity with other members in a work-related network has a positive influence on job performance.




假设3：员工与工作相关网络中其他成员的互惠对工作绩效有正向影响。


## Cognitive Dimension




## 认知维度


The cognitive dimension provides common understanding, shared values, and common representatives among individuals or collectives, which further boosts cooperation and coordination [6, 68]. The cognitive dimension involves constructs such as shared codes and language, shared narratives, shared culture, tenure in the field, and self-expertise. At the micro level, shared language and codes enhance the capability for sharing, transferring, and combining knowledge, contribute to accumulating intellectual capital [68], result in mutual knowledge [25], and facilitate individuals’ full use of shared understanding to enhance their efficiency. At the macro level, shared narratives provide powerful means for creating, exchanging, and preserving different forms of knowledge [64]. In our research, we propose a new construct—“shared topics”—as a proxy for the cognitive dimension based on a blog’s diversified topics and blog ring characteristics. From a macro perspective, shared topics have some common characteristics with shared narratives, in that the topics existing on the platform may not only arouse some classical narratives but aggregate to form a specific shared atmosphere among employees. From a micro perspective, shared topics must be based on shared language, in that the shared topics could hardly be formed if language barriers exist.




认知维度提供了个人或集体之间的共同理解、共同价值观和共同代表，从而进一步促进合作与协调[6, 68]。认知维度涉及诸如共享代码和语言、共享叙述、共享文化、领域任期和自我专业知识等结构。在微观层面，共享语言和代码增强了知识共享、转移和组合的能力，有助于积累智力资本[68]，产生相互知识[25]，并有利于个体充分利用共享理解来提高效率。在宏观层面上，共享叙事为创造、交换和保存不同形式的知识提供了强大的手段[64]。在我们的研究中，我们基于博客的多元化主题和博客环特征，提出了一种新的构造——“共享主题”，作为认知维度的代理。从宏观上看，共享话题与共享叙事有一些共同的特点，即平台上存在的话题不仅可以唤起一些经典的叙事，而且聚合起来在员工之间形成特定的共享氛围。从微观角度看，共享话题必须建立在共享语言的基础上，如果存在语言障碍，共享话题就很难形成。


In a work-related network, shared topics bring benefits for job performance. First, shared topics represent a common language among employees that can facilitate cooperation and coordination [6, 68]. Second, to some extent, shared topics represent employees’ scope of knowledge. When an employee has a high level of shared topics with others, he/she may initially possess a wide scope of knowledge that can support him/her in achieving better job performance [14, 44]. Third, when an employee has a high level of shared topics with others, he/she can more easily and accurately seek targeted information or resources when necessary. For example, if an employee shares the same topic of “programming skills” with several other employees, then when he/ she encounters some practical problems in programming, he/she can easily identify where to find corresponding solutions and seek specific posts in a timely manner. Employees who do not have such a shared topic with others might waste more time and energy in seeking specific information or even fail in such a process. Fourth, shared topics can help to cultivate a better working atmosphere among employees, which will benefit job performance. Hence, we propose the following hypothesis:




在与工作相关的网络中，共享主题会给工作绩效带来好处。首先，共享主题代表员工之间的共同语言，可以促进合作与协调 [6, 68]。其次，共享话题在某种程度上代表了员工的知识范围。当员工与他人有高水平的共享话题时，他/她最初可能拥有广泛的知识，可以支持他/她取得更好的工作绩效[14, 44]。第三，当员工与他人有较高的共同话题时，他/她在必要时可以更容易、更准确地寻找有针对性的信息或资源。例如，如果一名员工与其他几名员工分享相同的“编程技巧”主题，那么当他/她在编程中遇到一些实际问题时，他/她可以很容易地识别到哪里找到相应的解决方案，并及时寻求特定的职位。如果员工没有与他人有这样的共同话题，他们可能会浪费更多的时间和精力来寻找特定的信息，甚至会失败。第四，共享话题有助于在员工之间培养更好的工作氛围，有利于工作绩效。因此，我们提出以下假设：


Hypothesis 4: Employees’ shared topics with other members in a work-related network have a positive influence on job performance.




假设4：员工与工作相关网络中其他成员的共享话题对工作绩效有正向影响。


## Preperformance as a Moderator




## 作为主持人预演


We adopt employees’ preperformance as a reflection of employees’ self-efficacy, which could reshape the influence of employees’ corporate blog participation on their performance. More concretely, if an employee has a high level of preperformance, then he/she can be regarded as a more efficient and autonomic employee [4, 61, 80]. When these employees encounter problems or obstacles in their daily work, they are better able to solve them via their own capabilities and they can gather needed resources using diverse approaches; in contrast to high preperformance employees, employees with a low level of preperformance may not be so capable and autonomic; when they encounter problems or obstacles in their work, they need more related information or advice to resolve them, and a corporate blog acts as an efficient tool for them to acquire related resources. In other words, their degree of dependence on a corporate blog may be higher than that of employees whose preperformance is high, and the influence of their corporate blog participation on performance may be stronger than that of high preperformance employees. Hence, we propose hypotheses H1a, H2a, H3a, and H4a:




我们采用员工的预绩效作为员工自我效能感的反映，这可以重塑员工企业博客参与对其绩效的影响。更具体地说，如果员工的预绩效水平较高，那么他/她可以被视为更有效率和自主性的员工[4,61,80]。当这些员工在日常工作中遇到问题或障碍时，他们能够更好地通过自己的能力来解决，并可以通过多种方式聚集所需的资源；与高绩效员工相比，低绩效员工可能没有那么有能力和自主性；当他们在工作中遇到问题或障碍时，他们需要更多的相关信息或建议来解决，而企业博客则是他们获取相关资源的有效工具。换句话说，他们对企业博客的依赖程度可能高于绩效高的员工，其企​​业博客参与对绩效的影响可能强于绩效高的员工。因此，我们提出假设 H1a、H2a、H3a 和 H4a：


Hypothesis 1a: Compared with high preperformance employees, employees’ indegree centrality in a work-related network has a stronger influence on the performance of those with low preperformance;




假设1a：与高绩效员工相比，员工在工作相关网络中的入度中心性对低绩效员工的绩效影响更大；


Hypothesis 2a: Compared with high preperformance employees, employees’ out-degree centrality in a work-related network has a stronger influence on the performance of those with low preperformance;




假设2a：与高绩效员工相比，员工在工作相关网络中的出度中心性对低绩效员工的绩效影响更大；


Hypothesis 3a: Compared with high preperformance employees, employees’ reciprocity with other members in a work-related network has a stronger influence on the performance of those with low preperformance;




假设3a：与高绩效员工相比，员工与工作相关网络中其他成员的互惠对低绩效员工的绩效有更强的影响；


Hypothesis 4a: Compared with high preperformance employees, employees’ shared topics with other members in a work-related network have a stronger influence on the performance of those with low preperformance.




假设4a：与高绩效员工相比，员工与工作相关网络中其他成员的共享话题对低绩效员工的绩效有更大的影响。


## Nonwork-related network




## 非工作相关网络


In contrast to a work-related network, the influencing mechanism of a nonworkrelated network may differ, and we infer that contradictory effects exist among employees with a different level of preperformance.




与工作相关网络相比，非工作相关网络的影响机制可能有所不同，我们推断不同预绩效水平的员工之间存在矛盾效应。


## Structural Dimension




## 结构维度


A nonwork-related network resembles an expressive network, which is defined as a channel to provide emotional and social support as well as entertainment. Employees’ access to these resources may engender different effects on performance according to their preperformance. From the perspective of self-efficacy, employees with high preperformance are more efficient and autonomic [4, 61]. For these employees, a complementary effect exists between their work- and nonwork-related structural advantages. Previous research has noted that “short and unobtrusive breaks, such as a quick surf of the Internet, enable the mind to rest itself, leading to a greater total net concentration for a day’s work, and as a result, increased productivity” [28, p. 1]. These employees tend to participate in a nonwork-related blog in a more disciplined manner. They get a feeling of entertainment and emotional support via a nonwork-related network, which mainly releases pressure and adjusts the working environment. In/out-degree centralities in a nonwork-related network serve this goal. Hence, we propose H5a and H6a:




与工作无关的网络类似于表达网络，它被定义为提供情感和社交支持以及娱乐的渠道。根据员工的预绩效，他们对这些资源的访问可能会对绩效产生不同的影响。从自我效能的角度来看，绩效高的员工更有效率和自主性[4, 61]。对于这些员工来说，他们的工作相关结构优势和非工作相关结构优势之间存在互补效应。先前的研究指出，“短暂且不引人注目的休息，例如快速上网，可以使大脑得到休息，从而使一天的工作更加集中，从而提高生产力”[28，第 17 页]。 1]。这些员工倾向于以更有纪律的方式参与与工作无关的博客。他们通过与工作无关的网络获得娱乐感和情感支持，主要是释放压力、调整工作环境。与工作无关的网络中的入/出度中心性服务于这一目标。因此，我们提出H5a和H6a：


Hypothesis 5a: For employees with a high level of preperformance, employees’ in-degree centrality in a nonwork-related network has a positive influence on job performance.




假设5a：对于前绩效水平较高的员工，员工在与工作无关的网络中的度中心性对工作绩效有正向影响。


Hypothesis 6a: For employees with a high level of preperformance, employees’ out-degree centrality in a nonwork-related network has a positive influence on job performance.




假设6a：对于前绩效水平较高的员工，员工在与工作无关的网络中的出度中心性对工作绩效有正向影响。


However, for employees with a low level of preperformance, a contradictory effect exists between structural advantages in work- and nonwork-related blog networks. As the use of a corporate blog is usually voluntary, the phenomenon of “blog loafing” will probably occur among these employees. First, after the blogging platform is deployed, employees with a low level of preperformance might behave in a less disciplined manner. They may get easily addicted to it. Second, the sociability of the blog makes it possible for employees to choose posts that arouse their interest and satisfy their curiosity. If the reader unit has access to too many superstars in the blogosphere, employees in this unit may feel bored because those superstars may be mostly homogeneous. To some extent, this resembles other social media platforms such as movie- or music-recommending websites. Most of the movies or music enjoyed on these websites are less well-known ones. This phenomenon has been explained as the “Long Tail” [3, 40]. In a corporate blogosphere, a similar “long tail” may also exist. Under such a mechanism, employees with low self-control may overuse leisure blogging and waste too much time. In summary, if low preperformance employees are highly embedded within a nonwork-related network, their time and energy will be dispersed and their performance will be harmed. Hence, we propose H5b and H6b:




然而，对于预绩效水平较低的员工来说，工作相关博客网络和非工作相关博客网络的结构优势之间存在矛盾效应。由于企业博客的使用通常是自愿的，因此这些员工很可能会出现“博客闲逛”的现象。首先，部署博客平台后，绩效水平较低的员工可能会表现得不太自律。他们可能很容易对此上瘾。其次，博客的社交性使员工可以选择引起他们兴趣并满足他们好奇心的帖子。如果读者单位接触到太多博客圈的超级明星，该单位的员工可能会感到无聊，因为这些超级明星可能大多是同质的。在某种程度上，这类似于其他社交媒体平台，例如电影或音乐推荐网站。在这些网站上欣赏的大多数电影或音乐都是不太知名的。这种现象被解释为“长尾”[3, 40]。在企业博客圈中，也可能存在类似的“长尾”。在这样的机制下，自制力差的员工可能会过度使用休闲博客，浪费太多时间。总之，如果绩效不佳的员工高度融入与工作无关的网络中，他们的时间和精力就会被分散，他们的绩效也会受到损害。因此，我们提出H5b和H6b：


Hypothesis 5b: For employees with a low level of preperformance, employees’ in-degree centrality in a nonwork-related network has a negative influence on job performance.




假设5b：对于前绩效水平较低的员工，员工在与工作无关的网络中的度中心性对工作绩效有负面影响。


Hypothesis 6b: For employees with a low level of preperformance, employees’ out-degree centrality in a nonwork-related network has a negative influence on job performance.




假设6b：对于前绩效水平较低的员工，员工在与工作无关的网络中的出度中心性对工作绩效有负面影响。


## Relational Dimension




## 关系维度


In a nonwork-related network, the influence of reciprocity may vary among employees with different levels of preperformance. For employees with a high level of preperformance, their strong self-efficacy may help them to gain the benefits of reciprocal relationships in a nonwork-related network. They may focus on maintaining relationships and cultivating emotional support via such reciprocal interactions [47, 48]. Therefore, their nonwork-related reciprocal relationships may be an efficient complement to their formal work-related relationships, allowing them to gain proper emotional and social support from their peers in their daily work process, which could potentially benefit their performance. However, for employees with a low level of preperformance, their self-efficacy may be less strong, and they might easily get trapped in building and maintaining nonwork-related reciprocal relationships. When an employee devotes much time and energy to activities that maintain informal relationships, their formal work may be negatively affected. Hence, we propose H7a and H7b:




在与工作无关的网络中，互惠的影响可能因不同预绩效水平的员工而异。对于预绩效水平较高的员工来说，他们强烈的自我效能感可能有助于他们在与工作无关的网络中获得互惠关系的好处。他们可能会专注于通过这种相互互动来维持关系和培养情感支持 [47, 48]。因此，他们与工作无关的互惠关系可能是对正式工作相关关系的有效补充，使他们能够在日常工作过程中从同事那里获得适当的情感和社会支持，这可能有利于他们的绩效。然而，对于预绩效水平较低的员工来说，他们的自我效能感可能不太强，他们很容易陷入建立和维持与工作无关的互惠关系的困境。当员工投入大量时间和精力来维持非正式关系的活动时，他们的正式工作可能会受到负面影响。因此，我们提出H7a和H7b：


Hypothesis 7a: For high preperformance employees, employees’ reciprocity with other members in a nonwork-related network has a positive influence on job performance.




假设7a：对于高绩效员工，员工与非工作相关网络中其他成员的互惠对工作绩效有积极影响。


Hypothesis 7b: For low preperformance employees, employees’ reciprocity with other members in a nonwork-related network has a negative influence on job performance.




假设7b：对于低绩效员工，员工与非工作相关网络中其他成员的互惠对工作绩效有负面影响。


## Cognitive Dimension




## 认知维度


In a nonwork-related network, shared topics may also affect job performance differently in terms of employees’ preperformance. For high preperformance employees, their shared topics with others may cultivate a better working environment for them. As Van Oech has noted, “A fun working environment is much more productive than a routine environment” [84, p. 110]. Shared topics among employees in a nonwork-related network foster an entertaining climate, which may benefit employees’ productivity [43, 60, 65]. However, for employees with a low level of preperformance, when they have more shared topics with others, they are more likely to become addicted and their performance may decrease. We therefore propose H8a and H8b:




在与工作无关的网络中，共享主题也可能对员工的工作绩效产生不同的影响。对于高绩效员工来说，与他人分享的话题可能会为他们创造更好的工作环境。正如 Van Oech 所指出的，“有趣的工作环境比常规环境更有效率”[84，第 14 页]。 110]。在与工作无关的网络中，员工之间共享的话题营造了一种有趣的氛围，这可能有利于员工的生产力[43,60,65]。然而，对于预绩效水平较低的员工来说，当他们与他人分享的话题越多时，他们就越容易上瘾，绩效可能会下降。因此我们提出H8a和H8b：


Hypothesis 8a: For high preperformance employees, employees’ shared topics with other members in a nonwork-related network have a positive influence on job performance;




假设8a：对于高绩效员工，员工与非工作相关网络中其他成员的共享话题对工作绩效有正向影响；


Hypothesis 8b: For low preperformance employees, employees’ shared topics with other members in a nonwork-related network have a negative influence on job performance.




假设8b：对于低绩效员工，员工与非工作相关网络中其他成员的共享话题对工作绩效有负面影响。

$$




$$

$$

Therefore, we take “scope of sharing” into consideration. We penalize those employees who have a high degree of sharing while having few connections with others. We denote $N _ { r j }$ as the number of employees read by reader unit r. The penalty can be calculated as:




然而，共享主题的程度仅衡量员工之间的主题重叠。如果直接使用话题共享度，结果可能会有偏差。因此，我们考虑“共享范围”。我们惩罚那些分享程度高但与他人联系较少的员工。我们将 $N _ { r j }$ 表示为读取器单元 r 读取的员工数量。罚金可按下式计算：

## Control and Moderating Variables




## 控制和调节变量


In our research, we control the influences of employees’ age, gender, position, and preperformance. Among all these control variables, age, gender, and position are derived from employees’ basic demographic information. Preperformance is a yearly evaluated indicator that represents an employee’s whole year performance, which is a more stable illustration of employees’ capability, efficiency, and productivity. In the company, employees’ preperformance is noted on a seven-point scale indicator. If an employee’s preperformance is above 6, he/she is noted as a “star employee.” As proposed in the model, preperformance not only affects the monthly evaluated performance in the subsequent year but also reshapes the influences of blogging on job performance. Preperformance data for the year 2012 were used in our study.




在我们的研究中，我们控制了员工年龄、性别、职位和绩效表现的影响。在所有这些控制变量中，年龄、性别和职位来自员工的基本人口统计信息。 Preperformance是年度评估指标，代表员工全年的绩效，更稳定地体现员工的能力、效率和生产力。在公司里，员工的绩效表现是通过七分制指标来衡量的。如果员工的预绩效高于 6，他/她就会被称为“明星员工”。正如模型中提出的，预绩效不仅影响随后一年的月度评估绩效，而且重塑博客对工作绩效的影响。我们的研究中使用了 2012 年的预绩效数据。

In a work-related network, we apply a stepwise PLS to test our hypotheses, and we treat preperformance as a continuous moderator [18], as the main effects of employees’ corporate blogging on performance are positive consistent before and after the moderation of preperformance, namely, the influencing direction of main effects would not change. Next, we test the influence of employees’ corporate blog participation on performance, and we find that employees’ structural dimension and cognitive dimension (in/out-degree centrality and shared topics) show a highly positive influence on job performance; however, the relational dimension, namely, reciprocity, has a significant negative influence on job performance. ## Discussion




＃＃ 讨论


As the results have revealed (summarized in Table 7), the distinction between workand nonwork-related blog networks is essential and meaningful. Meanwhile, the inclusion of preperformance as a moderator variable uncovers the hidden mechanism of the influence of employees’ corporate blogging on job performance in different kinds of networks.




结果表明（表 7 中总结），工作相关博客网络和与工作无关的博客网络之间的区别至关重要且有意义。同时，将预绩效作为调节变量，揭示了不同类型网络中员工的企业博客对工作绩效影响的隐藏机制。


In a work-related network, employees’ in- and out-degree centralities and shared topics show significantly positive influences on job performance. Therefore, H1, H2, and H4 are supported, indicating that employees’ correct and timely access to workrelated resources and sharing a common understanding with others in a work-related network will promote improved performance. However, the relational dimension (reciprocity) shows a significantly negative influence on job performance, which is contradictory to our initial H3. As discussed, in a work-related network, reciprocity represents interactive relationships among employees. Through such constant interactions, more efficient resource exchanges and opportunities for further collaboration and cooperation can be cultivated. However, employees’ interaction patterns can also affect the process of exchanging resources. As Burt [11] has argued, a densely connected network will hinder efficient resource exchange, especially when actors within the network form a strong identification and sense of belonging. Such detrimental effects of closed networks have also been discussed in other studies [31, 67]. In order to explore the patterns of employees’ interaction, we draw a distribution graph of indegree centrality. As shown in Figure 3, a large proportion of employees constantly interact with only a few other employees. In other words, most employees are trapped in a closed network. Under such a condition, employees’ constant reciprocal interactions may hinder their resource exchange with outsiders. Consequently, high reciprocity has a negative influence on their performance in this context.




在工作相关网络中，员工的进出度中心性和共享主题对工作绩效表现出显着的正向影响。因此，H1、H2、H4得到支持，表明员工正确、及时地获取工作相关资源，并在工作相关网络中与其他人分享共同的理解，将促进绩效的提高。然而，关系维度（互惠）对工作绩效表现出显着的负面影响，这与我们最初的H3相矛盾。正如所讨论的，在与工作相关的网络中，互惠代表了员工之间的互动关系。通过这种不断的互动，可以培育更有效的资源交流和进一步协作与合作的机会。然而，员工的互动模式也会影响资源交换的过程。正如 Burt [11] 所指出的，密集连接的网络将阻碍有效的资源交换，特别是当网络内的参与者形成强烈的认同感和归属感时。其他研究也讨论了封闭网络的这种有害影响 [31, 67]。为了探索员工互动的模式，我们绘制了入度中心性分布图。如图 3 所示，大部分员工不断地与少数其他员工互动。换句话说，大多数员工都被困在一个封闭的网络中。在这种情况下，员工不断的互动可能会阻碍他们与外界的资源交换。因此，高互惠会对他们在这方面的表现产生负面影响。

Notably, we use in- and out-degree centralities to illustrate employees’ access to related resources, which are all directly related to employees’ concrete manipulation of resources. In contrast, for the relational and cognitive dimensions, we use reciprocity and shared topics to illustrate employees’ cooperation and common understanding in the work-related network, which are not directly related to the manipulation of work resources. Hence, the moderation effects of preperformance on reciprocity and shared topics are less strong.

In nonwork-related network, contradictory effects exist between star employee groups and other groups. For star employee groups, structural and cognitive dimensions show significantly positive effects on job performance, thus supporting our H5a, H6a, and H8a. It can be inferred that high preperformance employees tend to use a nonwork-related blog network as complementary for their work-related activities. For the other group, we find that employees’ structural and relational dimensions all have significant negative influences on job performance, thus supporting H5b, H6b, and H7b. ## Conclusion




＃＃ 结论


Our research makes some theoretical and methodological contributions. Theoretically, we uncover the influencing mechanism of employees’ corporate blogging on job performance from a novel perspective. First, drawing upon social capital theory, our proposed framework provides a new theoretical lens that will be effective for systematically analyzing diversified intraorganizational social media activities in similar research contexts. Second, our findings suggest that the distinction between work- and nonwork-related blog networks is critical when considering the organizational outcomes of social media use. Third, by considering preperformance as a reflection of employees’ heterogeneity in using a corporate blog, we find that the effects of employees’ work- and nonwork-related blogging activities on their performance are conditionally varied. Specifically, the impacts of work-related blogging are negatively moderated by employees’ preperformance. In contrast, the effects of nonwork-related blogging are contradictory between star employees and other employees. Star employees can benefit from nonwork-related blogging, whereas the effects are detrimental for low preperformance employees.




我们的研究做出了一些理论和方法论贡献。从理论上讲，我们从一个新颖的角度揭示了员工企业博客对工作绩效的影响机制。首先，借鉴社会资本理论，我们提出的框架提供了一个新的理论视角，可有效系统地分析类似研究背景下的多元化组织内社交媒体活动。其次，我们的研究结果表明，在考虑社交媒体使用的组织结果时，工作相关博客网络和非工作相关博客网络之间的区别至关重要。第三，通过将预绩效视为员工使用企业博客的异质性的反映，我们发现员工与工作相关和与工作无关的博客活动对其绩效的影响是有条件变化的。具体来说，与工作相关的博客的影响会受到员工绩效的负面影响。相比之下，与工作无关的博客的效果在明星员工和其他员工之间是矛盾的。明星员工可以从与工作无关的博客中受益，而这种影响对于绩效不佳的员工来说是有害的。


Methodologically, we measured all the independent variables using record data collected from an in-practice blogging system, in an attempt to overcome the inherent defects of self-report methods. We believe this is a meaningful exploration for new methodologies to analyze social capital.




在方法上，我们使用从实际博客系统收集的记录数据来测量所有自变量，试图克服自我报告方法的固有缺陷。我们相信这是对社会资本分析新方法的一次有意义的探索。


Our research also contributes to practical management areas. When managers aim to improve job performance via implementing a corporate blog, they are advised to consider the different effects engendered via work- and nonwork-related blogging. For a work-related blog network, encouraging employees to actively participate is meaningful. Especially for those with a low level of preperformance, a corporate blog can act as a useful working assistant for these employees to acquire and gather related resources to accomplish their tasks. Meanwhile, managers may encourage employees to break the boundary of “small groups” in a work-related network by reading from a diversified selection of employees and interacting online with employees from different departments. Furthermore, managers need to create a shared common understanding by cultivating more shared topics among employees on the blogging platform to enhance job performance. For nonwork-related blogging, high preperformance employees benefit from the participation, but low preperformance employees do not. Therefore, managers need to tactfully encourage employees to engage in nonwork-related blogging, taking the employees’ preperformance as a reference.




我们的研究也为实际管理领域做出了贡献。当管理者旨在通过实施企业博客来提高工作绩效时，建议他们考虑与工作和非工作相关的博客所产生的不同效果。对于与工作相关的博客网络来说，鼓励员工积极参与是有意义的。特别是对于那些预绩效水平较低的员工来说，企业博客可以充当这些员工的有用工作助手，帮助他们获取和聚集相关资源来完成他们的任务。同时，管理者可以鼓励员工打破工作网络中“小群体”的界限，通过多元化选择的员工阅读、与不同部门的员工在线互动。此外，管理者需要通过在博客平台上培养员工之间更多的共享话题来建立共识，以提高工作绩效。对于与工作无关的博客，高绩效员工会从参与中受益，但低绩效员工则不会。因此，管理者需要巧妙地鼓励员工写与工作无关的博客，并以员工的绩效为参考。


There are also some limitations to be noted. First, the measurement of job performance is based on a single indicator. This aggregate rating restricts our ability to develop a nuanced view of relationships between individual online social capital and dimensions of performance. The second limitation relates to shared topics. In our research, shared topics is seemingly a new construct we built to measure the cognitive dimension of social relationships; it is reasonable for interpreting some phenomena. Based on this first attempt, future work should be done, such as evaluating its internal and external validities. The third limitation is that the distinction of a high- and low preperformance group in a nonwork-related blog network analysis is subjectively conducted according to the company; nevertheless, our results should be interpreted with caution, and further improvements are still needed.




还有一些需要注意的限制。首先，工作绩效的衡量基于单一指标。这种综合评级限制了我们对个人在线社会资本与绩效维度之间关系进行细致观察的能力。第二个限制与共享主题有关。在我们的研究中，共享主题似乎是我们为衡量社会关系的认知维度而构建的新结构。对某些现象的解释是合理的。基于这第一次尝试，未来的工作应该做，例如评估其内部和外部的有效性。第三个限制是，在与工作无关的博客网络分析中，高绩效组和低绩效组的区分是根据公司主观进行的；尽管如此，我们的结果应该谨慎解释，并且仍然需要进一步改进。


Future research may extend our current work in several directions. First, the proposed framework may be applied and adapted in other scenarios, in order to further theorize the organizational impacts of various social media applications. Second, the cross effects of multiple social media can be examined. Third, the value of a nonwork-related social media network deserves further investigation, considering that nonwork-related activities may contribute to other organizational outcomes such work–life balance, communication satisfaction, and organizational commitment.




未来的研究可能会在几个方向上扩展我们目前的工作。首先，所提出的框架可以在其他场景中应用和调整，以便进一步理论化各种社交媒体应用程序的组织影响。其次，可以检查多种社交媒体的交叉影响。第三，考虑到与工作无关的活动可能有助于其他组织成果，例如工作与生活的平衡、沟通满意度和组织承诺，与工作无关的社交媒体网络的价值值得进一步研究。
