# 软件流程多样性：概念化、测量以及对项目性能影响的分析<sup>1</sup>

This article investigates software process diversity, defined as the project condition arising out of the simultaneous use of multiple software development process frameworks within a single project. Software process diversity is conceptualized as the response of a project team to such contingencies as requirements volatility, design and technological novelty, customer involvement, and the level of organizational process compliance enforced on the project. Moreover, we conceptualize that the degree of fit (or match) between a project’s software process diversity and the level of process compliance enforced on the project impacts overall project performance. This conceptualization was empirically tested by utilizing data collected from 410 large commercial software projects of a multinational firm. The results show that higher levels of requirements volatility, design and technological novelty, and customer involvement increased software process diversity within a project. However, software process diversity decreased relative to increases in the level of process compliance enforced on the project. A higher degree of fit between the process diversity and process com pliance of a project, rather than the effects of those variables independently, was found to be significantly associated with a higher level of project performance, as measured in terms of project productivity and software quality. These results indicate that increasing software process diversity in response to project-level contingencies improves project performance only when there is a concomitant increase in organizational process compliance efforts. The implications of these results for research are discussed and prescriptive guidelines derived to manage the fit between process diversity and process compliance for improving software project performance.




本文研究了软件过程多样性，定义为在单个项目中同时使用多个软件开发过程框架而产生的项目条件。软件过程多样性被概念化为项目团队对诸如需求波动、设计和技术新颖性、客户参与以及项目上执行的组织过程合规性水平等突发事件的响应。此外，我们认为项目的软件流程多样性与项目执行的流程合规性水平之间的契合（或匹配）程度会影响整体项目绩效。这一概念化利用从一家跨国公司的 410 个大型商业软件项目收集的数据进行了实证检验。结果表明，更高水平的需求波动性、设计和技术新颖性以及客户参与增加了项目内软件流程的多样性。然而，相对于项目执行的流程合规性水平的提高，软件流程多样性有所下降。人们发现，项目的流程多样性和流程合规性之间的更高程度的契合度，而不是这些变量独立的影响，与更高水平的项目绩效显着相关，根据项目生产力和软件质量来衡量。这些结果表明，只有当组织流程合规性工作随之增加时，为响应项目级突发事件而增加软件流程多样性才能提高项目绩效。讨论了这些结果对研究的影响，并得出了规范性指南，以管理流程多样性和流程合规性之间的配合，从而提高软件项目性能。

## Introduction




＃＃ 介绍


Pursuing firm-wide process standardization has traditionally been prescribed as a “best practice” for software organizations aiming to address the challenges of developing high-quality software in a cost-effective way (Harter et al. 2000; Humphrey 1989; Ramasubbu et al. 2008; Van der Pijl et al. 1997). Until recently, such standardization efforts have been typically implemented through a firm-wide adoption of a single plan-based or agile normative software process framework<sup>2</sup> (Krishnan and Kellner 1999; Lycett et al. 2003; Ramasubbu 2014). Accordingly, a majority of prior research on software development processes implicitly treats project-level processes as being uniformly derived from a single normative process framework (Agarwal and Chari 2007; Jiang et al. 2004; Krishnan et al. 2000). However, project-level processes in software development can be divisible, and project teams can customize their processes by selecting and combining elements from multiple plan-based and agile normative frameworks (Fitzgerald et al. 2006; Napier et al. 2008). Practitioner reports from the field also suggest that firms are increasingly adopting multiple process frameworks for their projects and embracing software process diversity (Anderson 2005; Bella et al 2008). Software process diversity refers to the condition of project-level processes being composed of elements drawn from multiple normative software process frameworks that could differ in their underlying attributes and philosophies (Deck 2002; Lindvall and Rus 2000).




追求全公司范围的流程标准化传统上被规定为软件组织的“最佳实践”，旨在以经济高效的方式应对开发高质量软件的挑战（Harter et al. 2000；Humphrey 1989；Ramasubbu et al. 2008；Van der Pijl et al. 1997）。直到最近，此类标准化工作通常是通过在公司范围内采用单一的基于计划或敏捷的规范性软件流程框架<sup>2</sup>来实施的（Krishnan 和 Kellner 1999；Lycett 等人 2003；Ramasubbu 2014）。因此，大多数先前关于软件开发过程的研究都隐含地将项目级过程视为统一源自单个规范过程框架（Agarwal 和 Chari 2007；Jiang 等人 2004；Krishnan 等人 2000）。然而，软件开发中的项目级流程是可以分割的，项目团队可以通过从多个基于计划的敏捷规范框架中选择和组合元素来定制其流程（Fitzgerald 等人，2006 年；Napier 等人，2008 年）。来自该领域的从业者报告还表明，公司越来越多地在其项目中采用多种流程框架并拥抱软件流程多样性（Anderson 2005；Bella 等人 2008）。软件过程多样性是指项目级过程由来自多个规范软件过程框架的元素组成的情况，这些框架的基本属性和理念可能有所不同（Deck 2002；Lindvall 和 Rus 2000）。


There is growing recognition that software process diversity could aid teams in overcoming the limitations imposed by standardized processes based on a single normative process framework that advocates strict adherence to either plan-based processes or agile processes (Magdaleno et al. 2012; Napier et al. 2008; Vinekar et al. 2006). Past research studies have highlighted that the combination of plan-based and agile processes within a single project, and the resulting increase in software process diversity, could help software teams to better adapt to changing user requirements and design specifications (Harris et al. 2009; Ramesh et al. 2012). Capabilities gained from embracing process diversity could also help teams address the conflicting demands in a project such as the need to be efficient versus the need to be flexible (Subramanyam et al. 2012). By increasing process diversity, software teams that traditionally use plan-based frameworks could infuse flexibility into their context by adopting certain components of agile process frameworks such as accepting requirement changes and by submitting to more frequent testing (Boehm 2003; Ramasubbu and Balan 2009). Similarly, by embracing process diversity, agile software teams could incorporate some elements of structure and formal communication associated with plan-based process approaches to enhance efficiency and overall predictability of outcomes (Ramesh et al. 2012).




人们越来越认识到，软件流程多样性可以帮助团队克服基于单一规范流程框架的标准化流程所带来的限制，该框架主张严格遵守基于计划的流程或敏捷流程（Magdaleno 等人，2012 年；Napier 等人，2008 年；Vinekar 等人，2006 年）。过去的研究强调，单个项目中基于计划的流程和敏捷流程的结合以及由此带来的软件流程多样性的增加，可以帮助软件团队更好地适应不断变化的用户需求和设计规范（Harris 等人，2009 年；Ramesh 等人，2012 年）。通过拥抱流程多样性获得的能力还可以帮助团队解决项目中相互冲突的需求，例如高效的需求与灵活性的需求（Subramanyam 等人，2012）。通过增加流程多样性，传统上使用基于计划的框架的软件团队可以通过采用敏捷流程框架的某些组件（例如接受需求变更和提交更频繁的测试）将灵活性注入到他们的环境中（Boehm 2003；Ramasubbu 和 Balan 2009）。同样，通过拥抱流程多样性，敏捷软件团队可以纳入与基于计划的流程方法相关的一些结构元素和正式沟通，以提高效率和结果的整体可预测性（Ramesh 等人，2012）。


While examining the impacts of software process diversity on the performance of custom software development projects, it is important to recognize the presence of another countervailing force in the form of the organizational process compliance enforced in those projects. Stringent compliance to established process standards is considered to be an important firm capability in the custom software development industry (Ethiraj et al. 2005; Ramasubbu 2014). Software firms are known to voluntarily seek compliance verification and certification from third-party standards organizations in order to gain the reputation of “high process maturity” firms (Gopal and Gao 2009; Van der Pijl et al. 1997). These firms allocate significant resources to monitor project teams for their compliance to organizational process standards (McGarry and Decker 2002; Ramasubbu et al. 2008; Staples and Niazi 2008). Enforcing such organizational process compliance on project teams can be expected to influence both the levels of process diversity pursued by individual project teams and their subsequent performance. However, there is a dearth of research examining the impact of software process diversity on the performance of projects executed in an environment of stringent process compliance mandates. We address this gap through this study.




在检查软件流程多样性对定制软件开发项目性能的影响时，重要的是要认识到在这些项目中强制执行的组织流程合规性形式的另一种抵消力量的存在。严格遵守既定流程标准被认为是定制软件开发行业的一项重要的公司能力（Ethiraj 等人，2005 年；Ramasubbu，2014 年）。众所周知，软件公司会自愿寻求第三方标准组织的合规性验证和认证，以获得“高流程成熟度”公司的声誉（Gopal 和Gao 2009；Van der Pijl 等1997）。这些公司分配大量资源来监控项目团队是否遵守组织流程标准（McGarry 和 Decker 2002；Ramasubbu 等人 2008；Staples 和 Niazi 2008）。在项目团队中强制执行此类组织流程合规性预计会影响各个项目团队所追求的流程多样性水平及其后续绩效。然而，缺乏研究来检验软件流程多样性对在严格流程合规性要求的环境中执行的项目性能的影响。我们通过这项研究解决了这一差距。


We raise the following research questions: What factors contribute to an increase in software process diversity in real world projects that are also actively monitored for compliance to mandated process standards? What is the joint effect of software process diversity and process compliance on project performance? To answer these research questions, we proceeded as follows. First, we conceptualized the software process diversity construct and developed its empirical formulization. Then, by theorizing software process diversity as a risk response mechanism to project-level contingencies, we developed a research model of the antecedents and performance implications of software process diversity. Subsequently, we partnered with a leading multinational software firm in order to conduct field observations of software projects and data collection. We tested and verified our conceptualization by utilizing archival data from 410 software projects completed at the research site. Finally, based on the results from the analysis and on follow-up discussions with project managers from the field, we developed a set of prescriptive guidelines for effectively balancing process diversity and process compliance in software projects.




我们提出以下研究问题：哪些因素有助于增加现实世界项目中的软件流程多样性，并且还积极监控其是否符合强制性流程标准？软件流程多样性和流程合规性对项目绩效的共同影响是什么？为了回答这些研究问题，我们进行了如下操作。首先，我们概念化了软件过程多样性构造并开发了其经验公式。然后，通过将软件过程多样性理论化为项目级突发事件的风险响应机制，我们开发了软件过程多样性的前因和性能影响的研究模型。随后，我们与一家领先的跨国软件公司合作，对软件项目进行实地观察并收集数据。我们利用研究现场完成的 410 个软件项目的档案数据测试并验证了我们的概念。最后，根据分析结果以及与现场项目经理的后续讨论，我们制定了一套规范性指南，用于有效平衡软件项目中的流程多样性和流程合规性。


## A Conceptualization of Software Process Diversity




## 软件过程多样性的概念化


When software teams adopt and fuse multiple normative process frameworks within a single project, they increase the overall software process diversity of the project. While there have been anecdotal and case descriptions of software process diversity in prior research (e.g., Boehm 2003; Jakobsen and Jhonson 2008; Jakobsen and Sutherland 2009), a rigorous analysis of the concept of software process diversity, its relationship with organizational process compliance, and their joint impact on project performance is absent in the literature.




当软件团队在单个项目中采用并融合多个规范流程框架时，他们会增加项目的整体软件流程多样性。虽然在先前的研究中已有软件过程多样性的轶事和案例描述（例如，Boehm 2003；Jakobsen 和 Jhonson 2008；Jakobsen 和 Sutherland 2009），但文献中缺乏对软件过程多样性的概念、其与组织过程合规性的关系以及它们对项目绩效的共同影响的严格分析。


To address this gap, we draw on the broader management literature, which has focused on various forms of organizational diversity, such as demographic differences and differences in values, functional skills, and personality types. Harrison and Klein (2007) reviewed the literature on organizational diversity and provided guidelines for its conceptualization and measurement. They recommended conceptualizing diversity as an amalgamation of the differences in the beliefs and values of team members (diversity as separation); the differences in the knowledge, experience, and perspectives of team members (diversity as variety); and the differences in the influences and resources held by team members (diversity as disparity). To build on this, we theoretically define software process diversity as the composition of differences in separation, variety, and disparity of key process areas implemented in a software project.




为了解决这一差距，我们借鉴了更广泛的管理文献，这些文献重点关注各种形式的组织多样性，例如人口差异以及价值观、职能技能和人格类型的差异。 Harrison 和 Klein (2007) 回顾了有关组织多样性的文献，并为其概念化和测量提供了指导。他们建议将多样性概念化为团队成员信仰和价值观差异的融合（多样性即分离）；团队成员的知识、经验和观点的差异（多样性即多样性）；以及团队成员所拥有的影响力和资源的差异（多样性即差异）。在此基础上，我们从理论上将软件过程多样性定义为软件项目中实现的关键过程领域的分离性、多样性和差异性的差异的组合。


A key process area (KPA) is a cluster of related activities (or tasks) in a project that, when performed collectively, achieves a set of goals for successful software development (Ramasubbu et al. 2008). Normative software process frameworks prescribed by industry-based standards organizations clearly specify the number and scope of KPAs that they support. For example, the CMMI framework for software development consists of a total of 22 KPAs covering the entire lifespan of a development project.<sup>3</sup> When a software organization adopts a normative process framework such as the CMMI, a process template covering the KPAs of the framework is instituted. This process template provides a predefined collection of artifacts that can be used to organize the workflow spanning the KPAs throughout a project’s life cycle. In organizations that allow project teams to simultaneously use multiple normative frameworks, process templates corresponding to the different frameworks are made available to the software project team. Software teams typically activate specific components of each of those templates for use in their projects (Fitzgerald et al. 2006; Ramasubbu et al. 2008). Thus, in this scenario, multiple process templates collectively implement the entire set of KPAs of a project. Since the use of process templates by a software team is logged and traceable, longitudinal observations of the membership of a project’s KPAs to the different process templates provide an empirical basis to reliably measure software process diversity and the underlying dimensions of separation, variety, and disparity of the KPAs implemented in a project.




关键过程域 (KPA) 是项目中的一组相关活动（或任务），当共同执行时，可以实现成功软件开发的一组目标（Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年）。行业标准组织规定的规范性软件流程框架明确规定了它们支持的 KPA 的数量和范围。例如，软件开发的 CMMI 框架总共由 22 个 KPA 组成，覆盖了开发项目的整个生命周期。<sup>3</sup>当软件组织采用 CMMI 等规范性流程框架时，就会建立一个涵盖该框架 KPA 的流程模板。此流程模板提供了预定义的工件集合，可用于组织跨越整个项目生命周期的 KPA 的工作流程。在允许项目团队同时使用多个规范框架的组织中，与不同框架相对应的流程模板可供软件项目团队使用。软件团队通常会激活每个模板的特定组件以在其项目中使用（Fitzgerald 等人，2006 年；Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年）。因此，在这种情况下，多个流程模板共同实现项目的整套 KPA。由于软件团队对流程模板的使用是有记录且可追溯的，因此对项目 KPA 与不同流程模板的成员资格的纵向观察为可靠地衡量软件流程多样性以及项目中实施的 KPA 的分离、多样性和差异的基本维度提供了经验基础。


The separation dimension of software process diversity measures the composition of differences among KPAs in a software project belonging to process templates as derived from a plan-based versus an agile normative process framework. This is analogous to assessing diversity in a project team by acknowledging the differences in knowledge bases, perspectives, and deeply held values and beliefs among the team members (Kilduff et al. 1995; Homan et al. 2007). Planbased and agile process frameworks vary in their overall values, beliefs, and practice attitudes (Boehm 2002; Boehm and Turner 2003). For example, while plan-based frameworks such as the CMMI favor structured planning processes and comprehensive documentation for traceability, agile frameworks such as extreme programming tend to emphasize “individuals and interactions over tools, working software over documentation, customer collaboration over contract negotiation, and responding to change over following a plan” (Beck et al. 2001). Measuring the composition of KPAs belonging to the plan-based and agile process templates in a project captures the extent to which a project’s KPAs are diverse in their process values and beliefs.




软件流程多样性的分离维度衡量软件项目中属于流程模板的 KPA 之间差异的构成，流程模板源自基于计划的流程框架与敏捷的规范流程框架。这类似于通过承认团队成员之间知识基础、观点以及根深蒂固的价值观和信仰的差异来评估项目团队的多样性（Kilduff 等人，1995 年；Homan 等人，2007 年）。基于计划的敏捷流程框架在整体价值观、信念和实践态度方面有所不同（Boehm 2002；Boehm 和 Turner 2003）。例如，虽然基于计划的框架（如 CMMI）偏向结构化规划流程和全面的可追溯性文档，但敏捷框架（如极限编程）往往强调“个人和交互胜过工具、工作软件胜过文档、客户协作胜过合同谈判、响应变化胜过遵循计划”（Beck 等，2001）。衡量项目中属于基于计划的敏捷流程模板的 KPA 的组成可以捕获项目 KPA 在流程价值观和信念方面的多样性程度。


The variety dimension of software process diversity measures the composition of differences in the spread of KPAs across the different process templates used in a project. This accounts for differences among the varieties of process frameworks used in a project even when they are not separated in their overall philosophy (e.g., plan versus agile). For example, a software project might utilize templates from two plan-based frameworks such as the CMMI and rational unified process (RUP), and allocate to each 50 percent of the total project KPAs. Another project in the firm might utilize the same two process templates, but allocate 25 percent of its KPAs to the CMMI process template and 75 percent of its KPAs to the RUP template. The separation diversity score for these two projects would be zero, as both CMMI and RUP impose a plan-based development paradigm and there is no separation of values in this case. However, the two projects still differ in the extent to which they allocate KPAs to the two varieties of process templates (CMMI and RUP). Thus, there is a need to capture differences among software projects with respect to the number of process templates that they use and the way they allocate KPAs to those different varieties of process templates. The variety dimension of software process diversity accomplishes this by capturing the spread of the KPAs among the different varieties of process templates used in a project.




软件流程多样性的多样性维度衡量项目中使用的不同流程模板中 KPA 分布差异的构成。这解释了项目中使用的各种流程框架之间的差异，即使它们在总体理念上没有分开（例如，计划与敏捷）。例如，软件项目可能会利用来自两个基于计划的框架（例如 CMMI 和合理统一流程 (RUP)）的模板，并分配给总项目 KPA 的各 50%。公司中的另一个项目可能会使用相同的两个流程模板，但将 25% 的 KPA 分配给 CMMI 流程模板，将 75% 的 KPA 分配给 RUP 模板。这两个项目的分离多样性得分为零，因为 CMMI 和 RUP 都强加了基于计划的开发范例，并且在这种情况下没有价值分离。然而，这两个项目在将 KPA 分配给两种流程模板（CMMI 和 RUP）的程度上仍然存在差异。因此，需要捕获软件项目之间在使用的流程模板数量以及将 KPA 分配给这些不同类型的流程模板的方式方面的差异。软件流程多样性的多样性维度通过捕获项目中使用的不同流程模板之间的 KPA 分布来实现这一点。


The disparity dimension of software process diversity measures the composition of differences in the resource allocation to the KPAs belonging to the different varieties of process templates utilized in a project. Measuring disparity in resource allocation is important, because it captures the extent to which the diverse elements in a process were actually utilized in project activities. Resource allocation in software projects has been typically measured as the percentage of total project effort allocated to a particular activity (Krishnan et al. 2000; Ramasubbu et al. 2008). Accordingly, the disparity dimension of process diversity accounts for resource allocation differences by considering the differences in the project effort allocated to the different KPAs and the process templates with which they are associated.




软件过程多样性的差异维度衡量了属于项目中使用的不同过程模板种类的 KPA 的资源分配差异的构成。衡量资源分配的差异很重要，因为它反映了项目活动中实际利用流程中不同要素的程度。软件项目中的资源分配通常以分配给特定活动的项目总工作量的百分比来衡量（Krishnan 等人，2000 年；Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年）。因此，流程多样性的差异维度通过考虑分配给不同 KPA 的项目工作量以及与其关联的流程模板的差异来解释资源分配差异。


In summary, rather than assuming that the KPAs of a project are homogenously distributed with respect to the underlying normative process framework(s), the construct of software process diversity allows us to account for the separation, variety, and disparity of software processes within a project. Next, we develop a research model of the antecedents and consequences of software process diversity, and formulate our key hypotheses.




总之，软件过程多样性的构造使我们能够解释项目内软件过程的分离、多样性和差异，而不是假设项目的 KPA 相对于底层规范过程框架是均匀分布的。接下来，我们开发了软件过程多样性的前因和后果的研究模型，并制定了我们的关键假设。

## Research Model




## 研究模型


Keeping in mind the dual forces of software process diversity and process compliance in high process maturity environments, we began our model formulation by building on prior research findings of “controlled-flexible” process designs (Harris et al. 2009). Controlled-flexible process designs aim for both process improvisation and the establishment of disciplined management controls to enforce compliance.




牢记高流程成熟度环境中软件流程多样性和流程合规性的双重力量，我们基于“受控灵活”流程设计的先前研究成果开始构建模型（Harris 等人，2009 年）。受控灵活的流程设计旨在实现流程即兴创作和建立严格的管理控制以强制合规。


Using case studies, Harris et al. (2009) established evidence for the superior performance of controlled-flexible process designs when compared with pure plan-based designs. Those case studies revealed cross-project variations in the way controlled-flexible process designs were enacted by different project teams under the influence of technological and market uncertainties. Addressing how teams could adapt to such uncertainties, Vidgen and Wang (2009) studied software process design through the lens of complex adaptive systems and discussed the coevolution of software processes along with the business environment. In the software engineering literature, project-level contingencies such as requirements volatility, degree of customer involvement, and code size have been reported as influencing specific process design choices made by a software team (Boehm and Turner 2003; Magdaleno et al. 2012; Maruping et al. 2009). Finally, prior research has also established that high process maturity organizations, such as those assessed at CMM level-5 process maturity, enforce stringent process compliance mechanisms to ensure minimal deviance from prescribed standards (Ethiraj et al. 2005; Ramasubbu et al. 2008). Process compliance efforts tend to reduce process variations within a project, and they have been associated with the benefits of minimizing schedule deviation and lowering the number of defects in the delivered software (Harter et al. 2000; Harter et al. 2012; Krishnan and Kellner 1999).




哈里斯等人通过案例研究。 (2009) 证实了与纯粹基于计划的设计相比，受控灵活的流程设计具有优越的性能。这些案例研究揭示了在技术和市场不确定性的影响下，不同项目团队制定的受控灵活流程设计方式存在跨项目差异。为了解决团队如何适应这种不确定性，Vidgen 和 Wang（2009）通过复杂自适应系统的视角研究了软件流程设计，并讨论了软件流程与业务环境的共同演化。在软件工程文献中，项目级意外事件（例如需求波动性、客户参与程度和代码大小）已被报告为影响软件团队做出的特定流程设计选择（Boehm 和 Turner 2003；Magdaleno 等人 2012；Maruping 等人 2009）。最后，先前的研究还表明，高流程成熟度组织（例如那些在 CMM 5 级流程成熟度评估的组织）会执行严格的流程合规机制，以确保与规定标准的偏差最小化（Ethiraj 等人，2005 年；Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年）。流程合规性工作往往会减少项目内的流程变化，并且它们与最大限度地减少进度偏差和减少交付软件中的缺陷数量有关（Harter 等人，2000 年；Harter 等人，2012 年；Krishnan 和 Kellner，1999 年）。


The above research findings motivated our model development as follows: (1) depending on specific project-level contingencies, software teams make choices regarding the underlying normative process frameworks for their projects, which could result in increased levels of software process diversity within the projects; and (2) process diversity and process compliance efforts expended on a project jointly impact project performance outcomes. We build on these to develop specific hypotheses on the antecedents of withinproject software process diversity and on the joint influence of organizational process compliance and process diversity on project performance.




上述研究结果推动我们的模型开发如下：（1）根据具体的项目级突发事件，软件团队对其项目的底层规范流程框架做出选择，这可能会导致项目内软件流程多样性水平的提高； (2) 项目上的流程多样性和流程合规性努力共同影响项目绩效结果。在此基础上，我们针对项目内软件流程多样性的前因以及组织流程合规性和流程多样性对项目绩效的共同影响提出了具体假设。


As noted earlier, software process variations within a project reflect the choices made by the software team in response to specific contingencies encountered during the project life cycle. Prior research on software project risk has examined how specific project-level contingencies (or project risk factors) contribute to overall project risk and impact performance outcomes (Barki et al. 1993; Keil et al. 2000). Much of this work characterizes project risks as stemming from various types of uncertainties related to project requirements, end user involvement, underlying technological and design complexity, project team composition, and the overall organizational environment (Wallace et al. 2004). We draw on the risk framework proposed by Wallace et al. (2004) to develop specific hypotheses that relate project risk factors to software process diversity. In doing so, we characterize the extent of process diversity observed in a project as the collective response of the project team to the perceived project risks through a key mechanism they control, namely, process design. However, as noted earlier, a countervailing choice exercised by managers in the form of process compliance enforcements attempts to rein in the within-project process diversity. Software process diversity is, therefore, the result of both project-specific risk factors and the extent of organizational process compliance enforced on the project.




如前所述，项目内的软件流程变化反映了软件团队为响应项目生命周期中遇到的特定突发事件而做出的选择。先前对软件项目风险的研究已经研究了特定项目级意外事件（或项目风险因素）如何影响总体项目风险并影响绩效结果（Barki 等人，1993 年；Keil 等人，2000 年）。这项工作的大部分内容将项目风险描述为源于与项目需求、最终用户参与、潜在技术和设计复杂性、项目团队组成以及整体组织环境相关的各种类型的不确定性（Wallace 等人，2004 年）。我们借鉴了 Wallace 等人提出的风险框架。 (2004) 提出将项目风险因素与软件过程多样性联系起来的具体假设。在此过程中，我们将项目中观察到的流程多样性程度描述为项目团队通过他们控制的关键机制（即流程设计）对感知到的项目风险的集体反应。然而，如前所述，管理者以流程合规性强制措施的形式进行的反补贴选择试图控制项目内流程的多样性。因此，软件过程多样性是项目特定风险因素和项目上执行的组织过程合规程度的结果。


In contrast to prior studies that have examined project risks in relation to overall project performance, we focus on the intermediate outcome of software process diversity, which in our conceptualization is a key conduit through which project risk factors impact project performance. Accordingly, among the six dimensions of project risk identified by Wallace et al.,<sup>4</sup> we develop hypotheses pertaining to requirements risk, design and technology risk, and end-user (customer) involvement risk. We account for the remaining risk factors in our empirical models through appropriate control variables, such as software project size, team size, and personnel experience.




与先前研究项目风险与整体项目绩效相关的研究相比，我们关注软件过程多样性的中间结果，在我们的概念化中，这是项目风险因素影响项目绩效的关键渠道。因此，在 Wallace 等人确定的项目风险的六个维度中，<sup>4</sup>我们提出了与需求风险、设计和技术风险以及最终用户（客户）参与风险相关的假设。我们通过适当的控制变量（例如软件项目规模、团队规模和人员经验）来解释实证模型中的其余风险因素。


The final component of our conceptualization is the joint impact of process diversity and process compliance on project performance. In hypothesizing this effect, we consider two project performance dimensions widely examined in the information systems development literature: productivity and quality (Harter et al. 2000; Krishnan et al. 2000; Ramasubbu et al. 2008). We utilize the “fit as matching” perspective (Venkatraman 1989) to characterize the fit (or match) between process diversity and process compliance and consider its impact on project performance. Conceptually, project performance improves with a good fit. That is, when a higher (lower) level of process diversity is matched with a higher (lower) level of investment in process compliance, there is an improvement in productivity and quality. ## Requirements Volatility




## 需求波动性


Requirements volatility refers to the extent to which the functional and nonfunctional requirements for a software system under development change during the project’s life span (Agarwal and Chari 2007; Barry et al. 2006). Requirement changes and subsequent alterations of software code have been reported to create ripple effects of rework in a project (Harter et al. 2000; Mookerjee and Chiang 2002). Rework might result due to legitimate changes in a client’s needs, but can also stem from ambiguous, inadequate, or incorrect articulation of requirements in the first place (Wallace et al. 2004).




需求波动性是指正在开发的软件系统的功能性和非功能性需求在项目生命周期内发生变化的程度（Agarwal 和 Chari 2007；Barry 等人 2006）。据报道，需求变更和随后的软件代码变更会在项目中产生返工的连锁反应（Harter 等人，2000 年；Mookerjee 和Chiang，2002 年）。返工可能是由于客户需求的合理变化而导致的，但也可能源于最初对需求的模糊、不充分或不正确的表述（Wallace et al. 2004）。


Plan-based and agile processes vary in their approaches to handling requirements volatility, the underlying causes, and the subsequent ripple effects of rework. Plan-based approaches primarily work toward minimizing requirements volatility through a combination of formal contracts that evoke client commitments and partitioning mechanisms that freeze requirements over certain stages of the life cycle of a project. In contrast, agile processes tend to respond to requirements volatility through improvisation, rapid adaptation, and incremental delivery of software (Boehm 2002; Maruping et al. 2009). The rapid adaptation and social approach inherent in the agile process framework, however, have been noted as unscalable due to the inefficiencies of frequent informal meetings (Begel and Nagappan 2007; Dyba and Dingsoyr 2008). Thus, in the presence of requirements volatility, there are tradeoffs to be made when any single framework is used exclusively in a project.




基于计划的流程和敏捷流程在处理需求波动、根本原因以及返工的后续连锁反应方面的方法有所不同。基于计划的方法主要通过结合引起客户承诺的正式合同和在项目生命周期的某些阶段冻结需求的分区机制来最大限度地减少需求波动。相比之下，敏捷流程倾向于通过即兴创作、快速适应和增量交付软件来应对需求的波动（Boehm 2002；Maruping 等人 2009）。然而，由于频繁的非正式会议效率低下，敏捷流程框架中固有的快速适应和社会方法被认为是不可扩展的（Begel 和 Nagappan 2007；Dyba 和 Dingsoyr 2008）。因此，在存在需求波动的情况下，当在项目中专门使用任何单一框架时，需要进行权衡。


One way to remove the constraints of such tradeoffs, then, is to adopt both plan-based and agile process frameworks within a project. Using multiple process frameworks would help project teams to better handle fluidity in requirements, as they could cordon off parts of the project with different levels of volatility and then tackle them through different process approaches. For example, teams could partition a project into different phases (or iterations) of high and low volatility in order to use different process frameworks for these phases (MacCormack and Verganti 2003). Teams could also partition the software into different modules according to the requirements volatility levels and then suitably apply different varieties of processes to them (MacCormack et al. 2001).




因此，消除此类权衡限制的一种方法是在项目中采用基于计划的敏捷流程框架。使用多个流程框架将帮助项目团队更好地处理需求的流动性，因为他们可以隔离项目中具有不同程度波动的部分，然后通过不同的流程方法来解决它们。例如，团队可以将项目划分为高波动性和低波动性的不同阶段（或迭代），以便为这些阶段使用不同的流程框架（MacCormack 和 Verganti 2003）。团队还可以根据需求波动级别将软件划分为不同的模块，然后对它们适当地应用不同种类的流程（MacCormack 等人，2001）。


Using different varieties of process templates from both planbased and agile frameworks creates separation in the overall philosophies toward software development within a project. Also, it has been observed that requirements volatility may not occur homogeneously throughout a project’s life cycle, and it does not affect all parts of a system’s development in a uniform way (Banker and Slaughter 2000; Barry et al. 2006). The heterogeneous patterns of requirements volatility, in turn, cause a disparity in effort expenditure on the different project phases, the parts of partitioned systems developed during those phases, and their associated process templates. Thus, an increase in the requirements volatility of a project tends to increase the variety, separation, and disparity of processes within the project, which leads to an overall increase in software process diversity. ## Design and Technology Novelty




## 设计和技术新颖性


Project teams facing new software design and technologies face a steep learning curve during the development process (Boh et al. 2007; Kemerer 1992). Initially, such teams might employ agile processes with rapid iterations to gain early feedback and accelerate team learning (MacCormack et al. 2001). However, over time, as the new technological features become more familiar and tasks get predictable, the focus can shift to a plan-based approach that facilitates enhanced efficiency and benefits due to the scale economies of longer iterations (Banker and Slaughter 1997). Furthermore, the use of novel design and technologies in a software project could be a significant contributor to project complexity, resulting in higher levels of project management risk (Wallace et al. 2004). Normative process frameworks that prescribe an exclusive plan-based or agile paradigm advocate different approaches for mitigating such risks. Plan-based frameworks often prescribe explicit risk management goals and practices. For example, in the CMMI framework, risk management is a level-3 KPA that prescribes specific steps for preparation, identification, analysis, and mitigation of risks (Chrissis et al. 2011). In contrast, agile process frameworks tend not to formally prescribe explicit templates for risk management, leaving it open for resolution by project teams (Anderson 2005; Moran 2014).




面对新的软件设计和技术的项目团队在开发过程中面临着陡峭的学习曲线（Boh et al. 2007；Kemerer 1992）。最初，此类团队可能会采用快速迭代的敏捷流程来获得早期反馈并加速团队学习（MacCormack 等，2001）。然而，随着时间的推移，随着新技术特征变得更加熟悉并且任务变得可预测，重点可以转移到基于计划的方法，该方法由于较长迭代的规模经济而有助于提高效率和收益（Banker 和 Slaughter 1997）。此外，在软件项目中使用新颖的设计和技术可能会极大地增加项目的复杂性，从而导致更高水平的项目管理风险（Wallace et al. 2004）。规范性流程框架规定了专有的基于计划或敏捷范式，提倡采用不同的方法来减轻此类风险。基于计划的框架通常规定明确的风险管理目标和实践。例如，在 CMMI 框架中，风险管理是 3 级 KPA，规定了准备、识别、分析和缓解风险的具体步骤（Chrissis 等，2011）。相比之下，敏捷流程框架往往不会正式规定明确的风险管理模板，而将其留给项目团队解决（Anderson 2005；Moran 2014）。


Higher levels of design and technological novelty present a demanding requirement on software teams, who are tasked with accommodating quick iterations to both facilitate effective learning and implement a planned approach to manage risks. Teams facing such challenging requirements resort to combining the attributes of multiple varieties of process frameworks, as it helps them to develop ambidextrous capabilities (Magdaleno et al. 2012; Napier et al. 2008; Ramesh et al. 2012). In such a scenario, the variety of process frameworks chosen by a project team would include a number of agile-oriented process components, because rapid iterations facilitated by agile processes aid efficient learning and effective client feedback during early project stages (MacCormack et al. 2001). Inclusion of agile-oriented process components in high process maturity environments, where the use of structured and plan-based approaches is the organizational norm, naturally leads to a separation of values and beliefs within a project.




更高水平的设计和技术新颖性对软件团队提出了苛刻的要求，他们的任务是适应快速迭代，以促进有效学习并实施有计划的方法来管理风险。面临如此具有挑战性的要求的团队诉诸于组合多种流程框架的属性，因为这有助于他们开发灵巧的能力（Magdaleno 等人，2012 年；Napier 等人，2008 年；Ramesh 等人，2012 年）。在这种情况下，项目团队选择的各种流程框架将包括许多面向敏捷的流程组件，因为敏捷流程促进的快速迭代有助于在项目早期阶段进行有效的学习和有效的客户反馈（MacCormack et al. 2001）。在高流程成熟度环境中包含面向敏捷的流程组件，其中使用结构化和基于计划的方法是组织规范，自然会导致项目内价值观和信念的分离。

## Customer Involvement




## 客户参与


There are divergent findings in prior research about the level of customer (end user) involvement in a project and its influence on a project’s processes and performance outcomes. Wallace et al. (2004) categorize the lack of user involvement in a project as a significant project risk and note that such risks could contribute to project failures. Exploring customerdeveloper links in software projects, Keil and Carmel (1995) show that projects with a higher degree of participation from customers tend to perform better. Such project success has been attributed to the ability of software teams to match their processes to customer needs through appropriate changes to development processes (MacCormack and Verganti 2003). On the one hand, when there is a high degree of customer participation in a project, team members might be expected to more actively respond to evolving customer requirements (Maruping et al. 2009; Matook and Maruping 2014; Subramanyam et al. 2010). On the other hand, a higher degree of customer participation might also necessitate the need for more formal communication plans, especially when there are organizational boundaries between the customer and the vendor teams (Pikkarainen et al. 2008; Ramesh et al. 2012).




关于客户（最终用户）参与项目的程度及其对项目流程和绩效结果的影响，先前的研究存在不同的结果。华莱士等人。 (2004) 将用户缺乏参与项目归类为重大项目风险，并指出此类风险可能导致项目失败。 Keil 和 Carmel（1995）在探索软件项目中的客户开发人员联系时表明，客户参与程度越高的项目往往表现更好。此类项目的成功归功于软件团队通过对开发流程进行适当更改来将其流程与客户需求相匹配的能力（MacCormack 和 Verganti 2003）。一方面，当客户高度参与项目时，团队成员可能会更积极地响应不断变化的客户需求（Maruping 等人，2009 年；Matook 和 Maruping，2014 年；Subramanyam 等人，2010 年）。另一方面，更高程度的客户参与也可能需要更正式的沟通计划，特别是当客户和供应商团队之间存在组织边界时（Pikkarainen 等人，2008 年；Ramesh 等人，2012 年）。


To respond to such dual needs in managing customer involvement in a project, software teams could potentially draw appropriate process components from multiple normative frameworks for their projects as customer engagement approaches in plan-based and agile approaches vary significantly. While the plan-based approaches opt for formal and punctuated interactions, the agile approaches prefer continuous, informal, and frequent interactions (Matook and Maruping 2014). Furthermore, as highlighted in prior research, a higher degree of customer involvement positively influences a project team’s orientation toward selecting more agile process components (Matook and Maruping 2014; Ramasubbu and Balan 2009). Naturally, in the context of custom software development executed by high process maturity firms, this leads to a situation in which a software team must grapple with separate value systems enshrined in the different varieties of process templates used in the project.




为了满足管理客户参与项目的双重需求，软件团队可能会从多个规范框架中为其项目抽取适当的流程组件，因为基于计划的客户参与方法和敏捷方法差异很大。虽然基于计划的方法选择正式和间断的交互，但敏捷方法更喜欢连续、非正式和频繁的交互（Matook 和 Maruping 2014）。此外，正如之前的研究所强调的那样，更高程度的客户参与会对项目团队选择更敏捷的流程组件的方向产生积极影响（Matook 和 Maruping 2014；Ramasubbu 和 Balan 2009）。当然，在高流程成熟度公司执行定制软件开发的情况下，这会导致软件团队必须努力处理项目中使用的不同类型的流程模板中包含的单独的价值系统。


Moreover, the degree of client involvement in a project may not be uniform throughout the different phases of a software project (Subramanyam et al. 2010). There are also variations in the expectations of different end users of a customer firm with respect to the formalism required in project-level communications and the corresponding coordination mechanisms (Andres and Zmud 2002; Nidumolu 1995). These variations result in overall heterogeneity in effort expenditures across formal (documents, contracts, etc.) and informal (mutual adjustments, informal interaction, etc.) coordination schemes. In turn, these would cause a corresponding disparity in the effort allocation to the different KPAs belonging to the plan-based and agile process templates that respectively facilitate formal and informal coordination. Thus, we expect the degree of customer involvement to positively influence the variety, separation, and disparity of the processes of a project team. Therefore, our third hypothesis is




此外，客户参与项目的程度在软件项目的不同阶段可能并不统一（Subramanyam 等人，2010）。客户公司的不同最终用户对于项目级沟通所需的形式和相应的协调机制的期望也存在差异（Andres 和 Zmud 2002；Nidumolu 1995）。这些差异导致正式（文件、合同等）和非正式（相互调整、非正式互动等）协调计划的工作支出总体异质性。反过来，这些将导致属于基于计划和敏捷流程模板的不同 KPA 的工作量分配出现相应的差异，这些模板分别促进正式和非正式的协调。因此，我们期望客户参与的程度能够对项目团队流程的多样性、分离性和差异性产生积极影响。因此，我们的第三个假设是


H3. A higher level of customer involvement in a software project is associated with a higher level of software process diversity.




H3。客户对软件项目的参与程度越高，软件流程的多样性就越高。


## Process Compliance




## 流程合规性


Process compliance mechanisms are procedures enacted to ensure the fidelity of the software design processes to the selected process frameworks (such as the adherence of the team to the normative prescriptions of the CMMI framework). Process compliance is typically enforced by quality control groups, who are charged with the responsibility of auditing and evaluating the adherence to quality metrics including the normative prescriptions for process design and documentation (Ogasawara et al. 2006; Ramasubbu 2014). In high process maturity environments, project teams are expected to report all process tailoring activities, and the teams are monitored and evaluated through internal audits and related quality assurance activities. Thus, process compliance efforts contribute to an indirect selection mechanism wherein a deliberate investment is made to monitor the project-level processes and assess their fit with the overall project and firm context. Compliance efforts act as a stabilizing force that tends to limit process diversity by blocking process variations that are deemed unfit, unnecessary, or noncompliant with firm standards, as evaluated by the independent quality assurance personnel.




流程合规机制是为确保软件设计流程对所选流程框架的保真度而制定的程序（例如团队遵守 CMMI 框架的规范性规定）。流程合规性通常由质量控制小组强制执行，质量控制小组负责审核和评估对质量指标的遵守情况，包括流程设计和文档的规范性规定（Ogasawara 等人，2006 年；Ramasubbu，2014 年）。在高流程成熟度环境中，项目团队应报告所有流程定制活动，并通过内部审计和相关质量保证活动对团队进行监控和评估。因此，流程合规性工作有助于建立间接选择机制，其中进行有意的投资来监控项目级流程并评估它们与整个项目和公司环境的契合度。合规工作充当稳定力量，通过阻止独立质量保证人员评估的被认为不适合、不必要或不符合公司标准的流程变化来限制流程多样性。


Apart from pruning project-level process variations, higher levels of process compliance enforcement can also provide learning opportunities for reconciling disparate values among project members (Ramasubbu et al. 2008). This can result in project teams utilizing more cohesive process components that are not too distinct in their underlying value attributes. Also, with a higher level of process compliance, process components that do not receive significant levels of resource commitments from the project team could be identified as potentially less useful variations that can be eliminated without significantly impacting project outcomes. Thus, we expect process compliance to act as a countervailing force to software process diversity by reducing the variety, separation, and disparity of processes in a software project. Hence, we hypothesize




除了修剪项目级流程差异之外，更高级别的流程合规性执行还可以为协调项目成员之间的不同价值观提供学习机会（Ramasubbu 等，2008）。这可能会导致项目团队利用更具凝聚力的流程组件，这些组件的基本价值属性不太明显。此外，随着流程合规性的提高，未从项目团队获得大量资源承诺的流程组件可能会被识别为可能不太有用的变化，可以在不显着影响项目结果的情况下将其消除。因此，我们期望流程合规性能够通过减少软件项目中流程的多样性、分离性和差异性来对抗软件流程多样性。因此，我们假设


H4: A higher level of process compliance effort in a software project is associated with a lower level of software process diversity.




H4：软件项目中较高级别的流程合规性工作与较低级别的软件流程多样性相关。


## Fit Between Process Diversity and Process Compliance




## 流程多样性和流程合规性之间的契合


The discussion up to this point has focused on examining the project-level contingencies that increase software process diversity and the role of process compliance in reining in process diversity. In this section, we examine the fit (or match) between process diversity and process compliance, and examine their joint effects on overall project performance. Following well-established protocols in prior literature for the measurement of software project performance (e.g., Harter et al 2000; Krishnan et al 2000; Ramasubbu et al. 2008), we conceptualize project performance along the two dimensions of the development productivity of a project team and the conformance quality in the software code delivered by the project team.




到目前为止，讨论的重点是检查增加软件过程多样性的项目级意外事件以及过程合规性在控制过程多样性中的作用。在本节中，我们将检查流程多样性和流程合规性之间的契合（或匹配），并检查它们对整体项目绩效的共同影响。遵循先前文献中用于测量软件项目性能的完善协议（例如，Harter 等人 2000；Krishnan 等人 2000；Ramasubbu 等人 2008），我们沿着项目团队的开发生产力和项目团队交付的软件代码的一致性质量两个维度来概念化项目性能。


Development productivity refers to the efficiency of a project team in developing and delivering software code by using resources available to the team (Krishnan et al. 2000). When lower levels of process diversity in a project are matched with a high degree of process compliance investments, the result is suboptimal use of organizational resources. The excessive compliance efforts in the above scenario could have otherwise been used for core project tasks such as software development and verification. Similarly, when a project team is subjected to a lower level of investments in organizational process compliance, but operates using a higher level of process diversity, there is a mismatch and a negative impact on productivity. This is because insufficient process compliance efforts often lead to the propagation of suboptimal or bad practices that cause rework during the late stages of a project (Stamelos 2010). The costs of such rework are difficult to constrain because of complex dependencies that cause ripple effects through all parts of the software system that is under development (Mookerjee and Chiang 2002). Such additional expenditures due to rework ripples dent the overall development productivity of a project. Similarly, when higher levels of process diversity are met with lower levels of process compliance efforts, there are insufficient resources available to detect software errors committed during development (i.e., lower levels of conformance quality). Such undetected development errors are likely to be reported by customers after the delivery of software code; for example, during acceptance testing. Rectifying software errors after delivery is challenging and leads to an overall increase in project rework and cost of quality (Harter et al. 2000).




开发生产力是指项目团队利用团队可用资源开发和交付软件代码的效率（Krishnan et al. 2000）。当项目中较低水平的流程多样性与高度的流程合规性投资相匹配时，结果是组织资源的使用不理想。上述场景中过多的合规工作本来可以用于软件开发和验证等核心项目任务。同样，当项目团队在组织流程合规性方面投入较低水平，但使用较高水平的流程多样性进行操作时，就会出现不匹配并对生产力产生负面影响。这是因为流程合规性工作不足通常会导致次优或不良实践的传播，从而导致项目后期返工（Stamelos 2010）。这种返工的成本很难限制，因为复杂的依赖关系会对正在开发的软件系统的所有部分产生连锁反应（Mookerjee 和Chiang 2002）。由于返工连锁反应而产生的额外支出降低了项目的整体开发效率。类似地，当较高水平的流程多样性与较低水平的流程合规性努力相结合时，没有足够的资源来检测开发期间犯下的软件错误（即较低水平的一致性质量）。这种未被发现的开发错误很可能在软件代码交付后被客户报告；例如，在验收测试期间。交付后纠正软件错误具有挑战性，并且会导致项目返工和质量成本总体增加（Harter 等，2000）。


In contrast, a good fit between process diversity and process compliance enables a project team to optimally balance the increased flexibility resulting from the use of multiple process frameworks and the rigor needed to detect and rectify software development errors before delivery. Such an optimal balance has been reported to engender process-based learning in software development that helps teams to realize improvements in productivity and reduce the number of defects delivered to customers (Ramasubbu et al. 2008; Subramanyam et al. 2012). A good fit between process diversity and process compliance also leads to the development of coevolutionary change mechanisms that help project teams adapt to uncertain business situations in an efficient way (Vidgen and Wang 2009; Volberda and Lewin 2003). Thus, a better fit between process diversity and process compliance endows project teams with capabilities that help them cope with project contingencies in an efficient way and reduce defects (or improve quality) through improved learning and effective quality control mechanisms. Therefore, our final set of hypotheses is




相比之下，流程多样性和流程合规性之间的良好契合使项目团队能够最佳地平衡因使用多个流程框架而增加的灵活性和交付前检测和纠正软件开发错误所需的严格性。据报道，这种最佳平衡可以在软件开发中产生基于流程的学习，帮助团队提高生产力并减少交付给客户的缺陷数量（Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年；Subramanyam 等人，2012 年）。流程多样性和流程合规性之间的良好契合还导致了共同进化变革机制的发展，帮助项目团队以有效的方式适应不确定的业务情况（Vidgen 和 Wang 2009；Volberda 和 Lewin 2003）。因此，流程多样性和流程合规性之间的更好契合赋予项目团队以有效的方式应对项目突发事件的能力，并通过改进的学习和有效的质量控制机制来减少缺陷（或提高质量）。因此，我们的最终假设是


H5a: A higher degree of fit between process diversity and process compliance in a software project is associated with a higher level of project productivity.




H5a：软件项目中流程多样性和流程合规性之间的更高程度的契合度与更高水平的项目生产力相关。


H5b: A higher degree of fit between process diversity and process compliance in a software project is associated with a higher level of software quality.




H5b：软件项目中流程多样性和流程合规性之间的更高程度的契合度与更高水平的软件质量相关。

## Data Collection




## 数据收集


To collect data for this study, we collaborated with a leading multinational software development firm that was a recipient of the IEEE Software Process Achievement Award (IEEE SPA 2010) and had an industry-wide reputation as a leader in process innovation. The firm operated in 55 countries with over 100,000 employees and about \$8 billion in annual revenues at the time of our data collection. All the development centers from which we collected data for this study were assessed as operating at CMM level-5 process maturity by an independent auditing agency. The high process maturity environment at the firm was attractive for our study, as it would facilitate our collection of reliable and finegrained data on project-level software processes.




为了收集本研究的数据，我们与一家领先的跨国软件开发公司合作，该公司是 IEEE 软件流程成就奖 (IEEE SPA 2010) 的获得者，并在流程创新领域享有全行业的声誉。该公司在 55 个国家/地区开展业务，拥有超过 10 万名员工，在我们收集数据时年收入约为 80 亿美元。我们为本研究收集数据的所有开发中心均由独立审计机构评估为按 CMM 5 级流程成熟度运行。该公司的高流程成熟度环境对我们的研究很有吸引力，因为它将有助于我们收集有关项目级软件流程的可靠且细粒度的数据。


We first engaged with the firm to assess the suitability of its business context, processes, and overall environment for our study. We conducted 27 focus group meetings and 28 structured interviews that involved 109 managers and software project personnel. These discussions and interviews involved participants who described their team’s development environment, process design rationale, and lessons learned from their experiences. Through these interactions, we were able to confirm the presence of within-project process design variations involving both plan-based and agile-process approaches at the firm. Practitioners also highlighted the influential role played by the firm’s software engineering process group (SEPG), which was an autonomous organizational unit that conducted rigorous internal audits on project teams to enforce compliance to the firm’s process standards. Those operational characteristics of the firm made it an ideal setting for testing the research model of this study, and we proceeded to the second stage of our data collection.




我们首先与该公司合作，评估其业务背景、流程和整体环境是否适合我们的研究。我们进行了 27 次焦点小组会议和 28 次结构化访谈，涉及 109 名经理和软件项目人员。这些讨论和访谈涉及参与者，他们描述了其团队的开发环境、流程设计原理以及从他们的经验中吸取的教训。通过这些互动，我们能够确认项目内流程设计变化的存在，涉及公司基于计划的方法和敏捷流程方法。从业者还强调了公司软件工程流程小组 (SEPG) 所发挥的影响力作用，该小组是一个自治的组织单位，对项目团队进行严格的内部审计，以强制遵守公司的流程标准。该公司的这些运营特征使其成为测试本研究的研究模型的理想环境，我们进入了数据收集的第二阶段。


In the second stage, we collected archival data from 410 commercial software projects completed by the firm in a recent two-year period. The archival data were collated from multiple databases at the firm, including those maintained by the SEPG, individual project managers, and the human resources division of the firm. The data set utilized in the study was independently audited multiple times by the firm’s external auditors for quality certification purposes, including the annual CMMI level-5 compliance checks and the nomination for the IEEE SPI award. Thus, we were able to utilize a unique and highly reliable data set for our analysis.




第二阶段，我们收集了该公司近两年内完成的410个商业软件项目的档案数据。档案数据是从公司的多个数据库中整理出来的，包括 SEPG、各个项目经理和公司人力资源部门维护的数据库。研究中使用的数据集经过公司外部审计师的多次独立审计，用于质量认证目的，包括年度 CMMI 5 级合规性检查和 IEEE SPI 奖提名。因此，我们能够利用独特且高度可靠的数据集进行分析。

## 数据集中的变量


## Process Diversity




## 流程多样性


As noted earlier, we consider a KPA as the fundamental unit of a software process design. At our research site, a majority of the software development centers used the 22 KPAs prescribed for CMMI level-5<sup>5</sup> development operations. Other normative process frameworks to which project teams subscribed during the time period of this study were IBM’s Rational Unified Process (RUP), Agile RUP, Scrum, and Extreme Programming (XP). The baseline process templates for all of these five normative process frameworks were standardized and controlled by the firm’s centralized SEPG. Individual projects were allowed to implement all of or a subset of the five process templates for their specific use.




如前所述，我们将 KPA 视为软件流程设计的基本单元。在我们的研究地点，大多数软件开发中心都使用为 CMMI 5 级<sup>5</sup> 开发操作规定的 22 个 KPA。在本研究期间，项目团队订阅的其他规范流程框架包括 IBM 的 Rational 统一流程 (RUP)、Agile RUP、Scrum 和极限编程 (XP)。所有这五个规范流程框架的基线流程模板均由公司的集中 SEPG 标准化和控制。各个项目被允许实施五个过程模板的全部或子集以供其特定用途。


Therefore, software teams had the flexibility to both choose a set of relevant process templates for their project and to design the spread of KPAs across the process templates, which resulted in project-specific customizations of planbased and agile frameworks. To be able to monitor and control such customized processes through uniform metrics, the firm’s SEPG teams mapped the KPAs of the normative process frameworks under a seamless and uniform process compliance framework.<sup>6</sup> The project-level process customizations and the spread of KPAs to the different process templates formed the basis for the measurement of the process diversity construct. For each of the KPAs implemented in a project, we observed the membership of the KPA to the corresponding process template and traced its use throughout the project’s life cycle. Then, we calculated the separation, variety, and disparity process diversity scores for each project.




因此，软件团队可以灵活地为其项目选择一组相关的流程模板，并设计跨流程模板的 KPA 分布，从而实现基于计划的敏捷框架的特定于项目的定制。为了能够通过统一的指标来监控此类定制流程，该公司的 SEPG 团队将规范流程框架的 KPA 映射到无缝且统一的流程合规框架下。<sup>6</sup>项目级流程定制以及 KPA 到不同流程模板的传播构成了衡量流程多样性结构的基础。对于项目中实施的每个 KPA，我们观察了 KPA 与相应流程模板的成员关系，并跟踪其在整个项目生命周期中的使用情况。然后，我们计算了每个项目的分离、多样性和差异过程多样性得分。

## Process Compliance




## 流程合规性


Process compliance efforts in a project involve the monitoring, assessment, and auditing of process engineering activities in a project. The process compliance personnel at our research site reported to both the project manager of a software team and the compliance supervisor at the SEPG. Although compliance personnel had sufficient autonomy within a software project team, executing process compliance activities required them to collaborate with individual developers. The extent of resources allocated to process compliance varied across the projects and we observed a corresponding variance in the extent to which the process compliance personnel were able to systematically monitor and assess process variants across projects. We had access to the task-level accounting and work log of all SEPG personnel participating in a software project, and we were able to identify and trace the overall process compliance effort expended on a project. Similar to prior research in which investments in quality management processes were measured as a percentage of total project effort (Ramasubbu et al. 2008), we derived the process compliance variable as the percentage of total project effort spent on process compliance activities.




项目中的流程合规工作涉及对项目中流程工程活动的监控、评估和审核。我们研究点的流程合规人员向软件团队的项目经理和SEPG 的合规主管汇报。尽管合规人员在软件项目团队中拥有足够的自主权，但执行流程合规活动需要他们与各个开发人员协作。分配给流程合规性的资源范围因项目而异，我们观察到流程合规人员能够系统地监控和评估项目间流程变体的程度也存在相应的差异。我们可以访问参与软件项目的所有 SEPG 人员的任务级会计和工作日志，并且能够识别和跟踪项目上花费的整个流程合规工作。与先前的研究类似，其中质量管理流程的投资以项目总工作量的百分比来衡量（Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年），我们将流程合规性变量推导出为流程合规性活动所花费的项目总工作量的百分比。


## Fit Between Process Diversity and Process Compliance




## 流程多样性和流程合规性之间的契合


To derive the fit variable, we follow the “fit as matching” perspective developed in prior literature (see Tang and Rai 2014; Venkatraman 1989). The fit as matching technique enables us to derive the match between process diversity and process compliance without reference to an external criterion variable (such as project performance). The criterionindependent matching perspective is particularly apt for our study because process diversity and process compliance efforts are primarily influenced by different and independent actors in a project who have different goals and incentives. While process diversity is driven by project-level contingencies faced by software teams, process compliance efforts are driven by standards mandated at the organizational level that are enforced by central SEPG agents.




为了导出拟合变量，我们遵循先前文献中提出的“拟合作为匹配”的观点（参见 Tang 和 Rai 2014；Venkatraman 1989）。拟合作为匹配技术使我们能够在不参考外部标准变量（例如项目绩效）的情况下得出流程多样性和流程合规性之间的匹配。标准独立匹配视角特别适合我们的研究，因为流程多样性和流程合规性工作主要受到项目中具有不同目标和激励的不同且独立的参与者的影响。虽然流程多样性是由软件团队面临的项目级突发事件驱动的，但流程合规性工作是由中央 SEPG 代理强制执行的组织级别强制标准驱动的。


We utilized the deviation score and residual score approaches to measure the match between process diversity and process compliance (see Venkatraman 1989). In the deviation score approach, misfit (or mismatch) is derived as the absolute difference between the standardized scores of process diversity and process compliance variables. Inversing the sign of the misfit variable obtained from the deviation score approach reflects fit, and it is used for easier interpretation of the regression coefficients. In the residual score approach, we derive the fit variable using the residuals from the regression of process compliance on process diversity. To verify the robustness of results, we also used the residuals from the regression of process diversity on process compliance to derive the fit variable as recommended in the literature (see Dewar and Werbel 1979).




我们利用偏差分数和残差分数方法来衡量流程多样性和流程合规性之间的匹配度（参见 Venkatraman 1989）。在偏差评分方法中，失配（或不匹配）是作为流程多样性和流程合规性变量的标准化分数之间的绝对差得出的。将偏差得分方法获得的失配变量的符号反转反映了拟合度，它用于更容易地解释回归系数。在残差评分方法中，我们使用流程合规性对流程多样性的回归的残差得出拟合变量。为了验证结果的稳健性，我们还使用过程多样性对过程合规性的回归残差来导出文献中推荐的拟合变量（参见 Dewar 和 Werbel 1979）。


## Project Performance




## 项目绩效


The dependent construct in our model, project performance, is measured using two variables: software productivity and quality. To derive the productivity variable, we first measured project size using function points, which is a programming language-independent measure of software functionality (Kemerer 1993). Then, we calculated productivity as an efficiency measure by treating the total function points produced by a software team as the output of a project and the total project effort as the input. Following methods applied in prior research, we utilized the log-transformed productivity variable for the regression estimations (Harter et al. 2000; Krishnan et al. 2000; Ramasubbu et al. 2008). We derived the second performance measure, software quality, as the inverse of the total number of unique software defects reported by customers (Subramanyam and Krishnan 2003).




我们模型中的依赖结构（项目绩效）是使用两个变量来衡量的：软件生产力和质量。为了导出生产率变量，我们首先使用功能点来测量项目规模，这是一种独立于编程语言的软件功能测量方法（Kemerer 1993）。然后，我们将软件团队产生的总功能点视为项目的输出，将项目的总工作量视为输入，从而计算生产力作为效率衡量标准。按照先前研究中应用的方法，我们利用对数转换的生产率变量进行回归估计（Harter 等人，2000 年；Krishnan 等人，2000 年；Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年）。我们得出了第二个性能指标，软件质量，作为客户报告的独特软件缺陷总数的倒数（Subramanyam 和 Krishnan 2003）。


## Requirements Volatility




## 需求波动性


This variable measures the extent to which requirements changes from customers induced rework in a software project. We calculated requirements volatility as the percentage of effort spent on rework due to change in customer requirements, and we held the planned development effort before the change occurred as the baseline.




该变量衡量客户在软件项目中引起返工而导致需求变化的程度。我们将需求波动性计算为由于客户需求变化而导致返工所花费的工作量的百分比，并以变化发生之前计划的开发工作量作为基准。


## Design and Technology Novelty




## 设计和技术新颖性


The extent to which the software design and technology involved in a project was new to the project team was selfreported by project managers and programmers using a questionnaire adapted from Takeishi (2002). We sought at least two survey responses from each project team: one from a manager and the other from a technical team member. The survey asked participants to rate the extent to which their project involved “design newness” and their familiarity with the technology used to implement the designs. The responses were measured using a 0 to 100 point scale, ranging from old design (0%), some modifications to old design (less than 30%), to radically different from prior designs (100%). Similarly, technological familiarity was measured using scales ranging from being completely familiar to old technologies used (0%) to dealing with a completely new and unfamiliar technology (100%). The responses to the design newness and technology familiarity items were highly correlated (0.94), and we took an average of those item scores to calculate the value of design and technology novelty in a project.




项目经理和程序员使用改编自 Takeishi (2002) 的调查问卷自行报告项目中涉及的软件设计和技术对项目团队来说是新的程度。我们向每个项目团队至少寻求两项调查答复：一项来自经理，另一项来自技术团队成员。该调查要求参与者评价他们的项目涉及“设计新颖性”的程度以及他们对用于实现设计的技术的熟悉程度。响应采用 0 到 100 分制进行衡量，范围从旧设计 (0%)、对旧设计的一些修改 (小于 30%) 到与先前设计完全不同 (100%)。同样，技术熟悉程度的衡量标准包括从完全熟悉所使用的旧技术（0%）到处理全新且不熟悉的技术（100%）。对设计新颖性和技术熟悉度项目的反应高度相关（0.94），我们取这些项目得分的平均值来计算项目中设计和技术新颖性的价值。


## Customer Involvement




## 客户参与


We calculated the extent of customer involvement in a project as the percentage of total project effort spent by a software team in engaging with end users. We systematically parsed the daily time logs submitted by software project members that were utilized for billing and cost accounting purposes of the firm in order to identify the time allocation of individual team members for client-facing activities. Then, we aggregated the total time spent on these client-facing activities at the project level. Next, we identified and accounted for the presence of clients in team meetings, peer reviews, and quality inspection meetings that were not specifically reported as client-facing activities in the time logs by matching the meeting participant logs with the list of project personnel that we obtained from project managers.




我们将客户参与项目的程度计算为软件团队在与最终用户互动方面所花费的总项目工作量的百分比。我们系统地分析了软件项目成员提交的每日时间日志，这些日志用于公司的计费和成本核算目的，以便确定各个团队成员在面向客户的活动中的时间分配。然后，我们汇总了项目级别上这些面向客户的活动所花费的总时间。接下来，我们通过将会议参与者日志与我们从项目经理那里获得的项目人员列表进行匹配，识别并解释了团队会议、同行评审和质量检查会议中客户的出席情况，这些会议在时间日志中没有专门报告为面向客户的活动。


## Control Variables




## 控制变量


As mentioned before, we used software code size measured using function points to derive the productivity variable. Similar to methods used in prior research, we used logtransformed code size in the regression analysis as a control variable (Harter et al. 2000; Krishnan et al. 2000). We utilize the full-time equivalent count of personnel involved in a project as the team-size control variable to account for the extent of the coordination and administration needs of the project. Since the cumulative experience of a project team could potentially influence the overall project performance (Boh et al. 2007), we included the average professional work experience of the project team (in years) in the regression models. Finally, some project managers at our research site had obtained professional certifications on specific normative process frameworks (e.g., certified Scrum Master). To account for this difference among the managers, we included a categorical control variable in the models, coded as 1 if a project manager possessed any professional certifications and as 0 otherwise.




如前所述，我们使用功能点测量的软件代码大小来推导生产率变量。与先前研究中使用的方法类似，我们在回归分析中使用对数转换的代码大小作为控制变量（Harter 等人，2000 年；Krishnan 等人，2000 年）。我们利用参与项目的全职人员当量数量作为团队规模控制变量，以考虑项目协调和管理需求的程度。由于项目团队的累积经验可能会影响整体项目绩效（Boh et al. 2007），因此我们在回归模型中纳入了项目团队的平均专业工作经验（以年为单位）。最后，我们研究点的一些项目经理获得了特定规范流程框架的专业认证（例如认证的 Scrum Master）。为了解释经理之间的这种差异，我们在模型中加入了一个分类控制变量，如果项目经理拥有任何专业认证，则编码为 1，否则编码为 0。

Before estimating the regression equations, we examined the empirical relationship between the three process diversity scores, namely, separation, variety, and disparity scores. Pairwise correlation between the three scores (shown in Table 2) revealed a high degree of correlation (at $\mathsf { p } < 0 . 0 1 )$ between them.




在估计回归方程之前，我们检查了三个过程多样性分数（即分离分数、多样性分数和差异分数）之间的经验关系。三个分数之间的成对相关性（如表 2 所示）揭示了它们之间的高度相关性（$\mathsf { p } < 0 . 0 1 ）$。


One reason for such a high correlation between the different diversity scores could be because all of the projects at our research site during our observation period existed in a CMMI level-5 organizational environment, and they moved toward higher levels of process diversity from that baseline. So project-level variations that caused process variety also simultaneously caused a separation of KPAs into plan-based and agile frameworks in a project. Further, the effort allocation across the different varieties of process templates followed the same pattern as the proportion of KPAs allocated to the different varieties of process templates in the project. Therefore, we are not able to sufficiently tease out the differences between the separation, variety, and disparity dimensions of software process diversity using our empirical data. As a result, to avoid multicollinearity issues, we only infer our results using each of the separation, variety, and disparity scores individually and mutually exclusively in the regressions. Results were similar across the models where the separation, variety, and disparity scores were separately used as the process diversity variable. Also, the process diversity variable calculated as a linear combination (additive, average, etc.) of the separation, variety, and disparity scores yielded similar results.<sup>11</sup>




不同多样性分数之间如此高度相关的原因之一可能是因为在我们观察期间我们研究地点的所有项目都存在于 CMMI 5 级组织环境中，并且它们从该基线转向更高水平的流程多样性。因此，导致流程多样性的项目级别变化也同时导致项目中的 KPA 分离为基于计划的敏捷框架。此外，不同类型的流程模板之间的工作量分配遵循与分配给项目中不同类型的流程模板的 KPA 比例相同的模式。因此，我们无法使用我们的经验数据充分梳理出软件过程多样性的分离、多样性和差异维度之间的差异。因此，为了避免多重共线性问题，我们仅在回归中单独且相互排斥地使用每个分离、多样性和视差分数来推断我们的结果。在将分离、多样性和差异分数分别用作过程多样性变量的模型中，结果相似。此外，以分离、多样性和差异分数的线性组合（加法、平均值等）计算的流程多样性变量也产生了类似的结果。<sup>11</sup>

鉴于我们的模型规范包含三个具有潜在同期交叉方程误差相关性的线性方程（方程 1-3），我们使用看似不相关的回归 (SUR) 来估计模型 (Zellner 1962)。我们使用 Durbin-Wu-Hausman 检验检验了方程组的内生性（Davidson 和 MacKinnon 1993，第 237-242 页）。

Recall that hypotheses H5a and H5b predicted that a better fit between process diversity and process compliance efforts yields higher productivity and quality respectively. ## Robustness Test for Group Size Effects




## 群体规模效应的稳健性测试


Diversity variables operationalized to measure group-level separation, variety, and disparity scores have been reported to suffer from biases when there is a large variation of group sizes in the sample (Biemann and Kearney 2010). Recall that we treat a KPA as the fundamental unit of a software process, and group size is determined by the membership of KPAs to process templates. To rule out group-size-induced biases in our results, we specifically accounted for group size (number of KPAs), while deriving the variance and standard deviation scores as recommended in the literature (see Biemann and Kearney 2010). Regression estimates using the original and the bias-corrected process diversity scores were similar, indicating that group size-induced biases are not of a concern in this study.<sup>14</sup>




据报道，当样本中群体规模存在较大差异时，用于衡量群体水平分离、多样性和差异分数的多样性变量会出现偏差（Biemann 和 Kearney 2010）。回想一下，我们将 KPA 视为软件流程的基本单元，组大小由流程模板的 KPA 成员资格决定。为了排除结果中由群体规模引起的偏差，我们特别考虑了群体规模（KPA 的数量），同时按照文献中的建议推导方差和标准差分数（参见 Biemann 和 Kearney 2010）。使用原始和偏差校正过程多样性分数进行的回归估计相似，表明群体规模引起的偏差不是本研究关注的问题。<sup>14</sup>


## Discussion




＃＃ 讨论


## Implications for Research




## 对研究的影响


This study takes an important step forward in rigorously conceptualizing, measuring, and analyzing software process diversity. We have laid the groundwork for theorizing software process diversity as an outcome of choices made by a project team in response to specific contingencies faced during the life cycle of the project. By drawing on prior work on organizational and demographic diversity (see Harrison and Klein 2007), we conceptualized how software process diversity can be understood not only in terms of the traditional separation of plan versus agile dichotomies (i.e., the separation dimension), but also along the variety and disparity dimensions. Going beyond the surface level classification of plan versus agile, a consideration of the additional process variety and disparity dimensions might suggest that there is greater heterogeneity, such as when project teams adopt and fuse different frameworks from within the agile or within the plan-based family of process frameworks. We believe that the study has laid a good foundation to “move beyond entrenched disagreements about planning versus agility” (Austin and Devin 2009, p. 476), and has created a theoretically wellinformed and empirically verifiable framework for studying software process diversity. We have established a rigorous case for usefully combining the disparate control and flexibility-focused process frameworks that could facilitate a new generation of software process innovations.




这项研究在严格概念化、测量和分析软件过程多样性方面向前迈出了重要一步。我们已经为将软件过程多样性理论化奠定了基础，将其作为项目团队针对项目生命周期中面临的特定突发事件而做出的选择的结果。通过借鉴之前关于组织和人口多样性的工作（参见 Harrison 和 Klein 2007），我们概念化了如何不仅从计划与敏捷二分法的传统分离（即分离维度）角度，而且从多样性和差异维度来理解软件流程多样性。除了计划与敏捷的表面分类之外，对额外的流程多样性和差异维度的考虑可能表明存在更大的异质性，例如当项目团队在敏捷或基于计划的流程框架系列中采用和融合不同的框架时。我们相信，这项研究为“超越关于规划与敏捷性的根深蒂固的分歧”（Austin and Devin 2009，p.476）奠定了良好的基础，并为研究软件过程多样性创建了一个理论上消息灵通、经验上可验证的框架。我们已经建立了一个严格的案例，可以有效地将不同的控制和注重灵活性的流程框架结合起来，从而促进新一代软件流程创新。


Our finding regarding the importance of a good fit between process diversity and process compliance has important implications. It suggests that, as process diversity proliferates within a project, adequate and appropriate process compliance mechanisms are needed to mitigate hazards due to poor or ill-conceived process variants. This result calls for expanding the current theoretical conceptualizations of software project risk management to accommodate process variations within a project as a potential and distinct source of risk that spans both the social and technical subsystems of a project (Iversen et al. 2004; Wallace et al. 2004).




我们关于流程多样性和流程合规性之间良好契合重要性的发现具有重要意义。它表明，随着项目中流程多样性的激增，需要充分且适当的流程合规机制来减轻由于不良或考虑不周的流程变体而造成的危害。这一结果要求扩展当前软件项目风险管理的理论概念，以适应项目内的流程变化，将其作为跨越项目的社会和技术子系统的潜在且独特的风险源（Iversen 等人，2004 年；Wallace 等人，2004 年）。


The need to achieve a good fit between software process diversity and process compliance has at least two additional implications for software project management. First, it is important to understand how adequate autonomy and appropriate coordination mechanisms could be instituted between project-level personnel and centrally organized (at firm-level) compliance personnel without exacerbating the tradeoffs between efficiency and flexibility (Ramasubbu 2014; Subramanyam et al. 2012). Second, prior conceptualizations of project control (e.g., Kirsch et al. 2002; Nidumolu and Subramani 2003) should be complemented with new mechanisms that explicitly take into consideration the effects of process diversity. We only studied the overall effort expended on process compliance, leaving open the need for further investigations of how specific portfolios of controls could be designed and implemented to regulate the fit between process diversity and process compliance.




在软件流程多样性和流程合规性之间实现良好契合的需要对软件项目管理至少有两个额外的影响。首先，重要的是要了解如何在项目级人员和集中组织的（公司级）合规人员之间建立充分的自主权和适当的协调机制，而不加剧效率和灵活性之间的权衡（Ramasubbu 2014；Subramanyam et al. 2012）。其次，先前的项目控制概念（例如，Kirsch et al. 2002；Nidumolu and Subramani 2003）应该用明确考虑流程多样性影响的新机制来补充。我们只研究了在流程合规性上花费的总体努力，需要进一步研究如何设计和实施特定的控制组合来调节流程多样性和流程合规性之间的配合。


## Implications for Practice




## 对实践的影响


An important implication for practice stemming from this research is the need to judiciously manage the alignment between process diversity and process compliance in software projects. Based on our discussions with practitioners at our research site, we provide a few implementable guidelines for software development organizations. First, development of meta-routines or problem-solving procedures that are independent of specific normative process frameworks would provide firms with an organization-wide platform for improving the efficiency of compliance mechanisms without compromising overall flexibility (Adler et al. 1999). Such meta-routines need to be enabled through appropriate infrastructural and organizational support mechanisms such as automation of process template audits and adequate separation of concerns between organizational-level SEPG personnel and projectlevel development personnel (Ramasubbu 2014).




这项研究对实践的一个重要启示是需要明智地管理软件项目中流程多样性和流程合规性之间的一致性。根据我们与研究站点的从业者的讨论，我们为软件开发组织提供了一些可实施的指南。首先，开发独立于特定规范流程框架的元例程或问题解决程序将为公司提供一个组织范围的平台，以提高合规机制的效率，而不影响整体灵活性（Adler等人，1999）。此类元例程需要通过适当的基础设施和组织支持机制来启用，例如流程模板审核的自动化以及组织级 SEPG 人员和项目级开发人员之间的关注点的充分分离（Ramasubbu 2014）。


Second, we noticed that teams used scope and temporal partitioning for creating effective boundaries between the applications of different varieties of processes in their projects. The possibility of spatial partitioning—that is, the use of separate specialized units that could independently handle the distinct aspects of plan-based and agile processes—was also raised during our discussions with project managers. The use of such scope, temporal, and spatial partitioning of tasks within a unit has been known to aid organizational ambidexterity (Puranam et al. 2006). In such scenarios, the partitioning and switching events of a project need to be used to trigger the necessary process compliance investments. For example, whenever a software team performs process partitioning or switching, an automatic audit by SEPG could be triggered, thereby facilitating a systematic way to match the levels of process diversity and process compliance in a software project. Furthermore, process partitioning and switching milestones could be used for enrichment and learning activities such as peer-reviews, root-cause analyses, and formal training that improve software project performance (Ramasubbu et al. 2008). Thus, we recommend that SEPG managers develop infrastructural capabilities that help implement mechanisms to track and trace project-level process partitioning and switching events.




其次，我们注意到团队使用范围和时间分区来在项目中不同流程的应用程序之间创建有效的边界。在我们与项目经理的讨论中，还提出了空间分区的可能性，即使用可以独立处理基于计划和敏捷流程的不同方面的单独的专业单元。众所周知，在一个单位内使用这种范围、时间和空间的任务划分有助于提高组织的二元性（Puranam et al. 2006）。在这种场景下，需要利用项目的分区和切换事件来触发必要的流程合规性投资。例如，每当软件团队执行流程划分或切换时，都可以触发SEPG的自动审核，从而促进以系统的方式匹配软件项目中的流程多样性和流程合规性水平。此外，流程划分和切换里程碑可用于丰富和学习活动，例如同行评审、根本原因分析和提高软件项目绩效的正式培训（Ramasubbu 等人，2008 年）。因此，我们建议 SEPG 管理者开发基础设施能力，帮助实施跟踪和跟踪项目级流程分区和切换事件的机制。


Finally, since our results indicated that customer involvement plays an important role in the way project teams initiate process variations, we recommend SEPG personnel use cues from customer communication at the organizational level (e.g., customer satisfaction surveys) to triangulate and verify the actual need to approve or block process variations. Such triangulation of customer feedback from multiple communication channels (organizational and project levels) could help process compliance personnel to identify and rectify the existence of contradictory customer policies at different levels, and, thereby, avoid unnecessary or risky process variations.




最后，由于我们的结果表明客户参与在项目团队启动流程变更的方式中发挥着重要作用，因此我们建议 SEPG 人员使用组织层面的客户沟通线索（例如客户满意度调查）来三角测量和验证批准或阻止流程变更的实际需要。对来自多个沟通渠道（组织和项目级别）的客户反馈进行三角测量可以帮助流程合规人员识别并纠正不同级别上存在的相互矛盾的客户政策，从而避免不必要或有风险的流程变化。


## Limitations and Further Research




## 局限性和进一步研究


There are some limitations of this study that future research could address. First, we could not fully distinguish the unique effects of separation, variety, and disparity dimensions of software process diversity in this research. The three diversity scores were highly correlated in our data set, and we suspect that idiosyncratic characteristics of the development environment at our research site could be a reason for this. Future research could shed light on the factors that lead to distinct effects of the separation, variety, and disparity process diversity dimensions through data gathered from a broader set of firms. Second, since we observed only custom (bespoke) software development projects, we should be cautious in generalizing our results across all types of software development projects (maintenance, reengineering, product development, etc.). The research model and empirical analysis utilized in this study can be replicated in other project settings and future research could embark on the necessary comparative analyses. Third, we did not observe the long-term impacts of process diversity and only studied its impact on immediate project performance outcomes. Further research is needed to ascertain the long-term impacts of software process diversity on the learning curves and the capability development of project teams. Fourth, we studied only software projects using standard process components drawn from well-established normative process frameworks. Not all software teams utilize standard process frameworks, and we need to be cautious in extrapolating the results reported in the paper to teams that use a variety of homegrown processes. Finally, the distinct coexistence of several process designs in a software production ecosystem for longer periods of time, and the way those diverse process designs coevolve and adapt to each other, warrants further examination. We believe these are fruitful lines of inquiry for future research on software process diversity.




这项研究存在一些局限性，未来的研究可以解决。首先，在本研究中，我们无法完全区分软件过程多样性的分离、多样性和差异维度的独特影响。这三个多样性得分在我们的数据集中高度相关，我们怀疑我们研究地点的开发环境的特殊特征可能是造成这种情况的原因。未来的研究可以通过从更广泛的公司收集的数据来阐明导致分离、多样性和差异过程多样性维度的明显影响的因素。其次，由于我们只观察定制（定制）软件开发项目，因此我们应该谨慎地将我们的结果推广到所有类型的软件开发项目（维护、重新设计、产品开发等）。本研究中使用的研究模型和实证分析可以在其他项目设置中复制，未来的研究可以进行必要的比较分析。第三，我们没有观察流程多样性的长期影响，只研究了其对直接项目绩效结果的影响。需要进一步的研究来确定软件过程多样性对学习曲线和项目团队能力发展的长期影响。第四，我们仅研究使用从完善的规范流程框架中提取的标准流程组件的软件项目。并非所有软件团队都使用标准流程框架，我们需要谨慎地将本文报告的结果推断给使用各种自行开发流程的团队。最后，软件生产生态系统中多种流程设计在较长时间内的明显共存，以及这些不同流程设计共同演化和相互适应的方式，值得进一步研究。我们相信这些对于软件过程多样性的未来研究来说是富有成效的探索。

Giri Kumar Tayi is a professor of Management Science and Information Systems at the State University of New York at Albany. His research and teaching interests are interdisciplinary and span the fields of Information Systems, Operations Management, and Operations Research. He serves or has served on the editorial boards of academic journals such as Information Systems Research, IEEE Intelligent Systems, IEEE Computing Now, Decision Sciences, ACM Journal of Data and Information Quality, Information Technology Management, Information Systems Frontiers, Information and Management, and International Journal of Shipping and Transport Logistics. His papers have appeared in Operations Research, Information Systems Research, Management Science, MIS Quarterly, IEEE Transactions, Networks, Naval Research Logistics, EJOR, Journal of Combinatorial Optimization, INFORMS Journal of Computing, Journal of Computer Security, Government Information Quarterly, and Communications of the ACM.
