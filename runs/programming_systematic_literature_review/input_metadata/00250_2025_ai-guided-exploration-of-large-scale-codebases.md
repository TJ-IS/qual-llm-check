Title: AI-Guided Exploration of Large-Scale Codebases
Abstract: Modern software systems are large, complex, and continuously evolving. As systems grow in size and interconnectivity, developers face increasing challenges in understanding unfamiliar codebases - whether during onboarding, debugging, refactoring, or feature development. Prior research estimates that professional developers spend up to 70% of their time on program comprehension tasks [1]. This problem is especially pronounced in real-world systems involving layered abstractions, distributed modules, and sparse or inconsistent documentation. © 2025 IEEE.
