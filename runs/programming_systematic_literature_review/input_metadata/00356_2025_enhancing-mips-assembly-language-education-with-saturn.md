Title: Enhancing MIPS Assembly Language Education with Saturn
Abstract: We introduce Saturn, an Integrated Development Environment (IDE) tailored to students learning MIPS assembly language. Saturn combines standard development features such as syntax highlighting, breakpoints, and program tracing with educational enhancements including backward execution, detailed and mutable debugging state, and out-of-the box integration of memory mapped I/O (for display, keyboard, and audio). We have used Saturn in lectures and labs for three years, which has significantly improved student engagement. Saturn is now also available in the browser (WebSaturn), which expands accessibility and collaborative opportunities. Saturn, and its assembler (Titan), are actively developed with student and instructor feedback in mind. Our next steps include support for RISC-V and Arm instruction sets.  © 2025 Owner/Author.
