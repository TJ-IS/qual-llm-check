Title: Information requirements determination
Abstract: Information requirements determination (IRD) is the process of eliciting, representing, and verifying the functional and non functional needs for an information system (Browne and Ramesh 2002; Davis 1982; Hickey and Davis 2004; Larsen and Naumann 1992; Vitalari 1992). More broadly, IRD is a form of needs analysis, an activity required in any designed artifact, ranging from consumer products to software to industrial processes (Simon 1996). © 2014 by Taylor & Francis Group, LLC.
