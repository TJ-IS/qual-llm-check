Title: Energizing or depleting? Understanding the effects of agile methodologies on individual software developers' resources
Abstract: Despite a considerable number of valuable studies in information systems research investigating agile software development methods (ASDM), the impact of ASDM on individual software developers and their inner resources in particular remains ambiguous. Findings so far support both directions, an energizing and a draining effect of ASDM application. Using ego depletion theory as a lens, I conducted a survey with 207 agile developers to examine how the use of ASDM influences individual job outcomes (i.e. job satisfaction and turnover intention) through the mediating effect of developers' level of depletion expressed by feelings of fatigue. My findings show that while engineering ASDM reduced fatigue (energizing), management ASDM practices did not affect fatigue (neither energizing nor depleting). Moreover, I could discern fatigue as underlying mechanism that explains the effects of engineering ASDM on job outcomes. With this nuanced perspective, this study contributes to a more balanced and consistent picture of ASDM use. © International Conference on Information Systems 2018, ICIS 2018.All rights reserved.
