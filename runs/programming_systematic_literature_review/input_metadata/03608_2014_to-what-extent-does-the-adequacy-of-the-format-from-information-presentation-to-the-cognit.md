Title: To what extent does the adequacy of the format from information presentation to the cognitive style of the decision-maker influence the decision-making process and outcome?; [Dans quelle mesure l'adéquation du format de présentation de l'information au style cognitif du décideur influence-T-elle le processus décisionnel et la décision prise ?]
Abstract: Prior research suggests that information presentation can directly affect decision making as it influences how information is processed to arrive at a decision outcome. To cope with the individual dimension of the impact of information presentation on decision-making, this research focuses on the extent to which decision-making process (task content and time) and outcome (value of a managerial variable) are influenced by the fact that the information which has been provided to the decision maker matches or not his cognitive style. The investigation is supported by empirical data collection through a software that has been specifically designed to randomly present information and track its use.
