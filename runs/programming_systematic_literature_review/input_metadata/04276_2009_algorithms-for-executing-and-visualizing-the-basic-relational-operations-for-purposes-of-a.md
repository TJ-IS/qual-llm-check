Title: Algorithms for executing and visualizing the basic relational operations for purposes of a Virtual Laboratory
Abstract: Studying a "Databases" course includes understanding of complex notions and methods. Students' commonly expressed difficulties in the subject suggest that traditional approaches are not appropriate for some learners. In this paper, we present developed algorithms for execution of some operations on relations: projection, selection, union, difference, Cartesian product, intersection and natural join. We also report and explain the evaluation of algorithms' complexity. Finally, we mention program modules, performing the operations above, which are implemented as part of a developing Virtual Laboratory (VL).
