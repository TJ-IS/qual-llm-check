Title: Performance Assessment in Real-Time Reactive Systems
Abstract: This paper addresses the performance specification, validation and measurement applicable to the early phases of real-time reactive software development. The proposed in the paper measures help identify potential performance problems, and provide the critical information needed to resolve those problems. Performance assessment tool automates the measurement data collection and analysis.
