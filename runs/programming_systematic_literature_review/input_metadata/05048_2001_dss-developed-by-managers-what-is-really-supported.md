Title: Dss developed by managers: What is really supported?
Abstract: Decision Support Systems (DSS) developed by managers (DSS-M) is an important part of organizational computing. Most of these DSS are developed using evolutionary approaches stressing continuous development of decision tasks. Evolutionary approaches can have drawbacks, for example, the exploitation of old tasks instead of exploration of new possibilities. In an organization (ALFA) experiencing problems with DSS-M an intervention, using two alternative approaches, was done. Based on the ALFA case, as well as other studies of DSS-M, we present alternative discourses and views related to what DSS-M are supporting. © 2001 Taylor & Francis Group, LLC.
