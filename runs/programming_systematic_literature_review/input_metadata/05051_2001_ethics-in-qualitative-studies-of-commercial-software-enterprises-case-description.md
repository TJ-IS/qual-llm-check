Title: Ethics in qualitative studies of commercial software enterprises: Case description
Abstract: In qualitative studies of software engineering, the researcher strives to have a natural, relaxed relationship with the subjects of the study. This helps him/her to ensure that the behavior being observed is not distorted by the participants' discomfort with being observed. In some cases, an ethical dilemma sometimes arises. A particular instance is described, the names of the companies involved are disguised.
