Title: Panel on collaborative software engineering
Abstract: The collaborative research and the more traditional software engineering research have merged to come up with a panel for Collaborative Software Engineering. The panel consists of: Prasun Dewan presents a broad overview of the area of computer supportive collaborative work (CSCW), identifying its relationship to traditional software engineering; Philip Johnson reviewed and inspected the software for research and development at the intersection of software engineering and CSCW; Remo Pareschi focused on the use of tools to support collaborative work processes on Web environments; and Kanth Miriyala presents key requirements, solutions and challenges in software engineering.
