Title: Comparison of student success in Pascal and C-language curriculums
Abstract: In September 1994, De Anza College in Cupertino, California added a CS1 C programming class to parallel its traditional Pascal offering. This article reports on the history leading to this decision, the success of the program, and the impressions after a year.
