Title: Empirical validation of a domain test selection criterion
Abstract: This article describes an experimental evaluation of a domain test selection criterion called V × V. The aim of the experiment was to verify the fault-exposing ability and the real applicability of this test selection criterion and to compare them with the ones of random, All-Edges, and All-Paths test selection criteria. Test data sets, generated according to the mentioned test selection criteria, were applied over a large number of faulty programs obtained by seeding faults in a correct one. The experiment results evidenced the effectiveness of the V × V test selection criterion in detecting all kinds of domain faults. But it also showed that a large number of test cases are needed to satisfy the criterion, and an extensive effort is required to identify them. © 1996 by Elsevier Science Inc.
