Title: A theoretical analysis of the role of documentation in the comprehension of computer programs
Abstract: The Problem of Optimizing Computer Program Documentation Reading and understanding a computer program listing is a cognitive task that is critical to the development and maintenance of software. During the initial development of programs, it certainly plays an important role in activities such as code reviews and debugging, and it may also be a significant activity in program testing and software quality evaluation. In the maintenance phase, its role may be even more vital since understanding how a program works is frequently a necessary prerequisite to updating or enhancing It. Hence, the development of improved AIDS for the performance of this task can have significant benefit for the entire software development and maintenance process. © 1982 Association for Computing Machinery.
