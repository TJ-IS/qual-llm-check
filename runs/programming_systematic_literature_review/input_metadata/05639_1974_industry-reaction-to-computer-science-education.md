Title: Industry reaction to computer science education
Abstract: Today, undergraduate Computer Science Education teaches mechanics without teaching problem solving. Typical curricula include courses in assembler languages, compiler theory, list processing, and a~tomata theory. Every MSdegree holder, and most BS . degree holders, know Polish notation and have written parts of compilers. However, few of them have ever learned to write a program that can be easily enhanced or respond to changes as new management (instructor) requirements are set forth. Even fewer can read a program and describe what it does or debug a system consisting of ten or more modules. © 1974 ACM.
