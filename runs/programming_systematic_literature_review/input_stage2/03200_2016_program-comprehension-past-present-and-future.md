Title: Program comprehension: Past, present, and future
Abstract: Program comprehension is the main activity of the software developers. Although there has been substantial research to support the programmer, the high amount of time developers need to understand source code remained constant over thirty years. Beside more complex software, what might be the reason? In this paper, I explore the past of program-comprehension research, discuss the current state, and outline what future research on program comprehension might bring. © 2016 IEEE.
