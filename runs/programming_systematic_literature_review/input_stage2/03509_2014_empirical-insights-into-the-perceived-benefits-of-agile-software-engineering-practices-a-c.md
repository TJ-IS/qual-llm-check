Title: Empirical insights into the perceived benefits of agile software engineering practices: A case study from SAP
Abstract: SAP AG has taught more than 4,000 developers the Agile Software Engineering methodology since 2010. As such, the company offers a unique setting to study its impact on de-velopers' work. In this paper, we discuss how developers perceive the impact of pair programming and test automa-tion on software quality, delivered feature scope, and various team work aspects. We draw our findings from a company-wide survey with answers from 174 developers working in 74 teams and 15 product owners from five locations world-wide. We complement our findings with insights from two in-depths case studies with two development teams. As ex-pected, our findings confirm that the studied practices help developers develop better software. Deviating from exist-ing preconceptions, however, the responding developers do not report a significant drop in their development speed. In addition, high adopters are more proud of their own contri-butions to the team, report a better team learning, and feel more motivated. Copyright © 2014 ACM.
