Title: A feature model of framework applications
Abstract: Learning how to use application frameworks effectively becomes important in their widespread use in software development. Learning frameworks is often difficult because of lack of their documentation and their complexity. In order to help framework learning, we propose a behavioral model, called feature component model, which abstracts internal behaviors of framework applications in terms of their behavioral characteristics such as inversion of controls. We apply our behavioral model to an example misuse of a practical framework developed by a third party in order to show its practical usefulness. © 2013 IEEE.
