Title: TurKit: Tools for iterative tasks on mechanical turk
Abstract: Mechanical Turk (MTurk) is an increasingly popular web service for paying people small rewards to do human computation tasks. Current uses of MTurk typically post independent parallel tasks. I am exploring an alternative iterative paradigm, in which workers build on or evaluate each other's work. Part of my proposal is a toolkit called TurKit which facilitates deployment of iterative tasks on MTurk. I want to explore using this technology as a new form of end-user programming, where end-users are writing "programs" that are really instructions executed by humans on MTurk. ©2009 IEEE.
