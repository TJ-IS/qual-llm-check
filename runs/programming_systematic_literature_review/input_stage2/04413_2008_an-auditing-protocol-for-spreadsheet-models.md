Title: An auditing protocol for spreadsheet models
Abstract: Errors are prevalent in spreadsheets and can be extremely difficult to find. A number of audits of existing spreadsheets have been reported, but few details have been given about how the audits were performed. We developed and tested a new spreadsheet auditing protocol designed to find errors in operational spreadsheets. Our work showed which auditing procedures, used in what sequence and combination, were most effective across a wide range of spreadsheets. It also provided useful information on the size and complexity of operational spreadsheets, as well as the frequency with which certain types of errors occur. © 2008 Elsevier B.V.
