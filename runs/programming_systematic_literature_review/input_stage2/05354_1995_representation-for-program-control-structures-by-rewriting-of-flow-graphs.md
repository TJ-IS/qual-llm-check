Title: Representation for program control structures by rewriting of flow graphs
Abstract: Programs constructed by concatenation, selection, repetition, and loop-exit goto statements for exceptions are easy to understand. Hierarchical representation makes them more understandable. This paper describes such representation of programs for maintenance and debugging. The representation is a parse tree of a flow graph that is parsed by graph rewriting rules corresponding to control structures. A representation of programs without goto statements is extended naturally to handle loop-exit goto statements. © 1995.
